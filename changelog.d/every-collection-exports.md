Every list now has an Export button that downloads it as a spreadsheet, and a saved view exports with its own filter and columns. The file holds only what you may see, and it goes back in through Bring your spreadsheet without making duplicates.

## docs

**Take a list out as a spreadsheet.** Every list (assets, machines, projects, builds, sales, a collection you made, and any saved view) has an **Export** button beside its search. It downloads a CSV of what the list is showing: a search you typed narrows the file too, and a saved view exports with its own filter, sort and (for a table) its columns. Columns carry the names you see, with the quantity, the location and the category in columns of their own. A field your role may not see is left out of the file entirely, the column as well as its values. A name that starts like a spreadsheet formula (`=`, `+`, `-`, `@`) is written so the spreadsheet shows it as text rather than running it. Bringing the file back in with **Bring your spreadsheet** matches every row to the record it came from, so nothing is made twice and nothing changes unless you edited it. Very large lists export their first 5,000 rows and say so; narrow the list with search to get the rest.
