Where a scanned item came from is now checked against its receipt or order. When Acquired from says one place and the receipt says another (Facebook Marketplace on an eBay order), the card says so where Add would be and holds the item for you instead of filing it or quietly picking a side; spellings of one place count as one place, the seller named on an order is never a contradiction of the marketplace it sold on, and "Gift" or "Bought used" are never flagged. One tap takes the receipt's word for it as your own answer, a re-run never overwrites what you typed, and filing a flagged item writes the receipt's source rather than the guess.
