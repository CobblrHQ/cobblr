Purchases now has **vendor management**: vendors are a managed entity (name, website, account #, contact, lead time, notes) instead of free text on each order. Pick a vendor on an order, add one inline, and see per-vendor order count + total spend in the new Vendors panel. Renaming a vendor updates its linked orders; deleting one keeps the order's vendor name but drops the link.
