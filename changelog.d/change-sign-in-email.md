You can change the email address you sign in with. The change happens only when you open a link sent to the new address, every device is signed out, and your old address is told, with a way to undo it for 7 days.

## docs

**Change the address you sign in with.** Under **Your account → Identity & password**, press **Change** beside your email, enter the new address and your current password, and press **Send the link**. Nothing changes yet: open the link we send to the new address (it works for 24 hours), and from then on you sign in with it. Every device is signed out when it changes, so sign in again. Your old address gets a message saying so, with a link that puts the account back on it for 7 days; if you use that link, you choose a new password at your next sign-in, because whoever changed it knew the old one. An address another account already uses cannot be taken. If you sign in through your Cobblr account (no password here), you change the address there instead.
