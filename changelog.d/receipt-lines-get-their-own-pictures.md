Lines from a receipt now look for their own catalog picture instead of arriving blank, and the search uses the shop the receipt came from, so a "Croissant" off a Lidl receipt looks for a Lidl croissant. Each line also shows which shop it came from under its name. The "this receipt lists N other items" note is a small chip now, and it stays out of the way entirely when the rest of the receipt is already sitting in your inbox as its own rows.
