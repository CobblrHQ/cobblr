Scan a printed Cobblr QR label and Cobblr jumps straight to it. If you have a USB or Bluetooth barcode scanner, it now works like a remote for the app: from any page, scan a bin, location, or item label and that thing opens on screen. Before, scanning a bin label from somewhere other than the Scan page dropped a mystery row into your inbox instead of opening the bin. The first scan asks once whether you want labels to jump automatically (Always / Just this once / No), and remembers your answer per device. Product barcodes still land in the scan inbox as before, from anywhere. Separately, the second-screen "drive this screen with scans" control (for a wall tablet that follows a scanner on another device) is now a small link instead of a large panel, since most people never need it.
