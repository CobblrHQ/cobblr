**One image viewer everywhere, and every photo opens full screen.** Enlarging an image used to look different depending on where you clicked: the scan inbox had one viewer for web photo results and a separate, more cluttered one when you tapped the catalog or your own shot, and item galleries had no zoom at all. They are now a single viewer: click any photo (a scan's catalog or your capture, a web search result, or a photo attached to a part, asset, vehicle or machine) to open it big on a dark backdrop, click it again to zoom in to actual pixels so a VIN, plate or label reads, and flip between the related shots with the thumbnail strip or the left and right keys. Escape or a click outside closes it.
