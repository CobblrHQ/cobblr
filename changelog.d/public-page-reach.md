A thing on a public page now says so: a Public chip beside who it is shared with, or "Public until Sun 6 pm" in booth mode. Public pages wear your workspace's colours and logo, with an accent of their own if you like, and search engines are told not to list them unless you say they may. A public card with no links no longer shows a stray 0.

## docs

**Is it public?** A thing anyone can see right now has a **Public** chip beside "Shared with", or **Public until** a time when its page is in booth mode. It shows only while a link to the page works or its labels open it; tap it to go to Sharing.

**Colour**, in a public page's Look: the page wears your workspace's brand (Configuration, the dashboard's theme: colours, font, corners and logo). Pick an accent to give the page its own; **Use the brand's own** goes back.

**Search engines may list it**, in a public page's settings: off by default, so only people you give a link to, or who scan a label, find the page.
