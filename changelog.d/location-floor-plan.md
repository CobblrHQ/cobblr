**Floor plans: draw a space to scale, and let it file things for you.** A location's detail page can now hold a real 2D plan: the room as a rectangle (dimensions in whatever unit you think in: "33ft", "7.3m"), interior walls with door cutouts, and every rack/toolbox/bench placed where it stands. Each rectangle IS a storage location: click to open it, drag it into a bay and it re-files there. Zones show as faint toggleable labels, never chrome. Containers get the same treatment in elevation, a toolbox's face lays out its drawers at true scale, partial-width ones visibly partial. Or skip the drawing: describe the space in a sentence and the AI drafts the plan for you to true up. The day-to-day view shows no numbers at all, measurements live in the data and appear only while editing.
