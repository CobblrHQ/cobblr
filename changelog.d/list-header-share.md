Every list now has a Share button to share the whole list or selected records, invite someone new and see who it is shared with.

## docs

Choose **Share** beside **Export** on a list, or **More list actions → Share** on a phone. Choose someone you already share with and share the current and future records in the list. Tick records first to share only those selected. **Invite a new person** makes a link scoped to this list or selection; copy it and send it to them. A whole list's **Shared with** label opens the same sheet. **Stop sharing** removes its whole-list rule; records shared separately stay shared. In **Configuration → Sharing**, choose what each person may see, review their edits, sync waiting changes or end the share.
