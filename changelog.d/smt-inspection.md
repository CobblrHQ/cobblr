Added board inspection photos, rework history, QR labels and tested-batch traceability to reel lots.

## docs

Prepare an inspection bench after reflow, authorize its screen and record each board's front and back photos, test result and rework notes. Finish after every decision is final to move passed boards into tested stock. Open saved evidence to queue a board QR label, or search a reel ID or lot to find the finished batches that used it.
