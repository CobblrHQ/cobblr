Every action Cobb may run without asking now offers Undo on its card, and Undo puts it back: a stock move restores the count and the lots, a tag comes off again, a reorder goes back to the previous order, a grouped section returns to where it sat, a logged entry or measurement is removed, a completed task or maintenance entry reopens. A run that changed nothing offers no Undo, and a card that cannot be undone from the chat says so instead of a bare "Done."
