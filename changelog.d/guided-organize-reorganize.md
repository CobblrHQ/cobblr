- **Organize what you already track (reorganize).** The **Organize** button in the scan page header runs the same put-away planner over your *committed* entities that have no location yet — parts, assets, and machines Cobblr tracks but doesn't know a home for. Same plan review (groups, evidence from where siblings live, proposed new bins, per-group accept), and accepted groups **move the entities themselves** — each one files through its own module, so validations and history fire exactly as if you'd edited it there. The put-away walk works here too; items keep their names in the checklist even though they never touched the inbox.
