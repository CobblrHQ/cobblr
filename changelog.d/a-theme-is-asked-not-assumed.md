The suggestion that scans in a session share a tag or category is now something you ask for, on the session you are looking at. It used to run by itself on every inbox load, across your whole pending inbox, so a filtered view of two items could be offered a category for fifty others and the numbers in the sentence disagreed. It costs nothing until you press it, both counts come from the same session, and it only says things look related when something was actually found to relate them.
