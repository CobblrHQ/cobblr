Ready-made setups now come in two honest sizes. The homepage's unscoped "Ready-made setups" column shows only the genuinely multi-part setups (Maker Workshop, Kitchen & Groceries, …); pick a kind and its **pre-shaped trackers** appear — Lego, Printer Parts, Bookshelf and Vintage Tools are now one-module trackers with **label printing as an opt-in checkbox** instead of dragging the Labels module in unconditionally. Behind the scenes: a bundle quality bar now gates the catalog in CI (every tracker must ship its routing vocabulary), and capture-matching stays fast no matter how many hundreds of trackers the catalog grows to.
