Personal documents can now be read here or by your local AI, with missing values shown for checking and no cloud AI fallback. Identity, vehicle, insurance, tax, medical, bank and licence papers count as personal wherever they are uploaded, including a bill of sale; a photo of a thing is still identified as usual.

## docs

Open Personal documents in the scan inbox and choose a PDF or photo. Cobblr
reads its text here and looks for a VIN, plate, expiry, fee and confirmation
number. Check the values before using them, especially those marked Read by
your local AI. With no local model connected, code still reads what it can.
Keep the original until you file it in the Vault.
A photo of a thing, such as a kettle or a box with its brand on it, is not a
document and is identified as usual. A bill of sale is read here for now and
not filed automatically.
