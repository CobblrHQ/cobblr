You can now hand things over to someone you share with. **Give to…** on a thing's page (or on ticked rows) offers it; it arrives in their scan inbox with its photos and details, where they **Take**, **Pass** or choose **Maybe later**. Once taken, your record is marked given away to them, with its history kept, and when they file it, it says it came from you. They can send it back within 14 days, and you can withdraw an offer until they answer.

## docs

**Handing things over.** Once a workspace is shared with someone (or someone shared with you), a thing's page has **Give to…** beside **No longer mine…**, and a list's selection bar has it too. Pick who, and the things go to them.

On their side the things arrive in the scan inbox as a session "Offered by *your name*", with the photos, the make and the details you had. Each card asks:

- **Take**: it is theirs. Filed, its Acquired from says it came from you. In a shared workspace, Take moves it into one of their own workspaces (they choose which).
- **Pass**: it leaves their inbox, and your record is no longer marked offered.
- **Maybe later**: it waits.

When they take it, your record is marked **Given away to *them*** (it leaves your lists and totals, its history stays). In a shared workspace, the scans there can be offered back to the person who shared it with **Offer to …** on the card.

**Settings → Sharing → Handed over** lists everything handed over both ways. **Withdraw** takes back an offer they have not answered yet; **Send back** returns a thing you took, within 14 days: if you had not filed it, it leaves your inbox; if you had, your record is marked given back. Either way it is theirs again as it was.

It works across Cobblrs too: when the other side is offline, the hand-over waits and arrives as soon as they are back.
