Adding a category (**+ New thing in workspace**) now lets you put it straight into a navbar **menu**: so a home inventory's Pantry, Cleaning Supplies, Tools, Medications, … collapse into one tidy **Inventory ▾** dropdown instead of sprawling across the top bar. It's automatic after the first: the first category pre-fills a menu named after the kind, and every category you add after auto-joins that same menu. Deleting a category cleanly removes it from its menu.
