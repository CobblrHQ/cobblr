Discussion and tags now work on every record, including inventory parts.

## docs

### Discussion

Every record has a conversation attached to it. Open a record and look for the
Discussion section, or the "Discuss" pill on records whose detail view is a
modal. It opens the right-hand rail on that record's thread, alongside Cobb and
Pinned, and you can switch between the three without losing your place.

Type `@` to mention someone in the workspace. The picker offers the people you
share a workspace with, and the records in it, so you can point at a specific
part or machine mid-sentence. Mentioning someone follows them into the
conversation: they get a notification, and if they have connected Discord they
get a direct message straight away, with a reply box on it so they can answer
without leaving Discord.

Mentions are stored as a link to the person or record, not as the name that was
showing when you typed it. Rename a machine and every mention of it updates.

`@Cobb` brings the assistant into the thread. Cobb answers in the conversation
where everyone can read it, never off in a private tab, and replying to Cobb
asks again so you do not have to keep naming him. Otherwise Cobb stays out of
it: he never joins a conversation he was not invited into.

Reply to a specific comment with the reply arrow. When a discussion has run its
course, resolve it: it collapses to a one line count, stays searchable, and
posting into it opens it again. Nothing is ever deleted.

### Keeping up

The Discussion entry in the top navigation is one list of everything with
something new in it, in three lanes: what you were named in, what is unread on
things you follow, and everything you follow. Commenting on a record starts
following it.
