Choose a nesting layout, pin parts, avoid defects, move copies between sheets, and bring edited layouts back without losing job counts.

## docs

In a laser batch preview, choose a recorded layout, pin parts, and re-nest around pins or avoid boxes. Move whole parts with drag, position and nudge controls, or move them between sheets. Sheet limits keep the job quantity intact.

Download an SVG or DXF layout, or open it through the LightBurn REST bridge. Bring back every requested part by its ID or shape. SVGnest transforms apply to the saved parts and keep inner shapes together. New or changed shapes need an assignment or a choice to keep or ignore extras; extra cuts do not increase the requested job count.
