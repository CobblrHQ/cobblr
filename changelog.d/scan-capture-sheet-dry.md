Every "take a photo of this" spot in the scanner now uses the **same in-app capture drawer** — the scan result card's *Nice photo* and *Not it — photograph it*, plus the inbox item's *Retake for catalog* and *Add photo*. None of them launches the phone's native camera anymore: on the live scanner they reuse the running viewfinder, and elsewhere the drawer opens its own camera (with a photo-picker fallback when there's no camera). One consistent shutter → Retake / Use this flow everywhere.
