**Keep what an asset cost, where it came from and who has it.** An asset now has Paid, Bought from and Who has it, which can be anyone in your workspace's people, even someone without an account. The Assets page adds up what everything cost.

## docs

An asset keeps what it cost and where it came from, in **Paid** and **Bought from**. The Assets page adds them up beside the count, like "12 of 12 · paid 2,340.50 in all".

**Who has it** names whoever has the thing now: a member of the workspace, or anyone on its People list by name, such as a neighbour who borrowed the ladder. Their name shows on the asset wherever it appears.
