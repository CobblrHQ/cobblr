**Live Sort: zero-tap sorting and a paired big screen.** Two additions to the sort session. On the phone camera, an opt-in **auto-Done** toggle makes the next scan confirm the previous directive: point at thing after thing, zero taps, with the Undo chip covering mistakes. And the sort session now pairs across devices: open **Live Sort** on a PC, scan with your phone in Sort mode, and each directive appears on the big screen the moment it's minted; confirming on either device advances both. Same session, two screens, no setup.

## docs

- **Zero taps, and a big screen.** In the camera's Sort mode, tick **auto-Done** and the next scan confirms the previous directive (the Undo chip still covers mistakes) so the steady state is just pointing at thing after thing. And because a sort session belongs to you (not a device), you can open **Live Sort** on a PC and scan with your phone in Sort mode: each directive appears on the big screen as it's minted, and confirming on either device advances both.
