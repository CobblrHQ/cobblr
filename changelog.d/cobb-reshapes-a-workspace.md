Cobb can rename what a list is called ("call my parts spools"), give a category a list of its own ("give spices their own list"), and fold one back where it came from.

## docs

Three more things you can just ask for:

- "call my parts spools" renames what that kind of record is called everywhere it is shown: the navigation, headings, buttons. It is the label only. Nothing recorded moves, no id changes, and renaming it back is the whole undo. Say the plural too when it is not the plain plus-s ("call a Box a Bin, several Bins").
- "give spices their own list" takes one category out of a list and gives it a list of its own, with its own nav entry, fields and label codes. The records keep their ids, their history and their printed labels, so the code on the bin still resolves; only which list they are in changes.
- "fold spices back into the pantry" is the exact reverse, stamping everything with the category you name as it goes back.

Deleting a whole list is still yours to do on the screen, because folding it back is the version that keeps your records and deleting is the version that does not.
