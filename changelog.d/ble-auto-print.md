Auto-print now works with a Bluetooth label printer: pick it under Auto-print and each scan (or every N labels) prints itself from your open Labels tab.

## docs

If you have a Bluetooth label printer, auto-print can fire it straight from your browser. Open Labels, choose Auto-print, pick your Bluetooth printer, and set when to print (each label, or every N). A Bluetooth printer is held by your browser rather than the server, so auto-print runs while the Labels tab is open and the printer is connected: scan or add a label and it prints on its own, with no trip to the queue. Close the tab or disconnect the printer and it pauses until you reconnect.
