Connect your other Cobblr to copy or move selected items into an existing workspace, with a field preview and separate source restore.

## docs

**Once connected.** In **Send to another workspace**, choose **Connect your
other Cobblr**, choose its configured destination and **Make connection link**.
Open the link there, sign in, choose an existing workspace and **Approve
connection**. Back here, **Refresh after approval** adds that workspace and its
host to the Workspace picker. You can send selected items in either direction.
The connection is between the two chosen workspaces and belongs to the account
that approved it on each side. Current access is checked on every send. It
shares no collection or workspace view.

Copy and Move use the same field preview, photos, documents, placement and
arrival rules as a send file. A connected Move keeps its source until the other
Cobblr confirms arrival. **Undo import there** removes the received items while
keeping the source moved; **Restore source here** is a separate step. Sharing's
**Handed over** list keeps both actions available after closing the send dialog.
Undo refuses to discard later changes. If a reply is lost, retry the same send
to recover its confirmation.

**Revoke connection** stops further sends and keeps both workspaces and prior
arrivals. After undoing an import, its saved confirmation can still restore the
source following revocation. The cloud connects only Cobblrs its operator trusts.
If pairing is refused, a send file works without a connection.
