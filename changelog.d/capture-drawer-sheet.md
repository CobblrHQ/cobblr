## docs

Tap the capture drawer (or its grip) to open it. You get the shot full-size, the item's whole photo gallery with a tap to choose which is the cover and an ✕ to drop one, and the fields worth setting while the thing is still in your hand: **What is it**, **Quantity**, and **Location**.

Location starts from where you are standing (the session's Assign chip) and the picker here overrides it **for that one item only**, so the fast path keeps working. When a container bin is armed, the item files into the container instead and the row says so rather than offering a location that would not be used.

The sheet never covers the shutter, and the next capture always starts closed, so opening it never interrupts a burst.
