**Forward receipts by email.** Every workspace you're in now has its own **`receipts+…@` address** (shown on the Scan page. Tap Copy). Forward a receipt there and its attachment runs through the same parser as an upload, landing as line items in that workspace's scan inbox, no app, no typing. The address itself names the workspace (signed, so it can't be typo'd or forged into someone else's), so multi-workspace folks get one address each and they all arrive in one inbox on our side. Operator-configured (needs the mail Worker set up, like reply-by-email); when it isn't, the address just doesn't show.
