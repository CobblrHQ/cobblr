Paid, fully fulfilled sales orders can close when delivered, with a notification, Undo and a setting to keep them open.

## docs

In an order, check **Paid** when payment has been received. Sales settings has
**Close delivered orders automatically** turned on by default. A delivered order
closes once it is paid and fully fulfilled. The notification and order sheet offer
**Undo**, which reopens the order and keeps it open until you close it yourself.
Turn the setting off to close orders by hand. Following parcels remains a separate
opt-in that uses your own tracking connection.
