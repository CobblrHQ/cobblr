Bring your own AI from anywhere that speaks the OpenAI v1 API: a new **OpenAI-compatible** provider takes a base URL (and an optional key) — point it at **LM Studio**, vLLM, llama.cpp, LocalAI, or a gateway like OpenRouter/Groq, and every AI feature (matching, vision, summaries, embeddings) runs through it. LM Studio users: paste `http://<your-ip>:1234/v1`, no key needed.
