**A record's image is now editable, and shows whole instead of cropped.** Records shipped with a read-only thumbnail, so a book that arrived without a cover had no way to get one. The detail now has the same image controls as machines and assets: upload your own, hit **Auto** to fetch one by what the record is (its name plus its own author/artist/director/publisher field), or **search the web** and pick a specific result. The image also keeps its own proportions instead of being cropped to a square, so a portrait book jacket or film poster is no longer cut off top and bottom while the column has room to spare.
