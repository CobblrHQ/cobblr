A service job that would print something or cost money now waits for your yes, on its card in the chat or as a notification with a Yes button when an automation started it, and it runs exactly as it was shown or not at all. You can also say yes ahead of time, like "power off after this print": the job waits for the print to finish, shows "Will run when…", and can be withdrawn until then. A yes nobody gives within a day lapses.
