Filament is one page. Each filament type is a card with its spools underneath, each with where it is, whether it is sealed or opened, and a minus and plus for what is left. The dashboard has one Filament tile, and the nav has one Filament entry. ## docs

**A type and the things of that type are one page.** When one collection holds
the types and another the things of those types (filament types and spools),
they are one nav entry and one dashboard tile, **Filament**, and one page: a
card per type (brand, material, diameter and a colour swatch) with its spools
as rows under it. Each spool shows its place, whether it is sealed or opened,
and a **−** and **+** for what is left.

The header says how many spools and types there are and how many types are
low. **By material**, **By colour** and **Low** regroup the cards. **+ Spool**
picks the type or names a new one, and each card has its own **+ Spool**. The
plain tables are under **⋯**.

Spools a scan filed into the types table, or without a type, are put under
their type when the page opens, and a colour spelled any way (a picked swatch
or a tag's colour code) shows as the same swatch.
