Backup destinations now have a "View backups" button that lists the backups actually living in the destination right now (Google Drive folder, NAS folder, or bucket) with their real date, time, and size, instead of only "last: ok". And once Google Drive is connected the button reads "Reconnect Google Drive" rather than the confusing "Connect Google Drive".
