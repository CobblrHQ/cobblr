When a plan Cobb worked out from your page matches some records and not others, the card now says what it left in three parts: a sentence with the count, then every one of those records on its own line (each opens the record, with its reason where it has one), then what sending the message would do about them. The offer that appears as you type and the card in the reply say it the same way. Before, the records sat in a parenthesis inside the sentence, cut off at three with "and 1 more".
