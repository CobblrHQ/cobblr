Ask Cobb can do more of the everyday things you could only click before. It can
say that a task is waiting on another task or on a record like a purchase order,
and remove that dependency again. It can add a line to a purchase or sales order
you already raised, and add an inventory category.

## docs

Ask Cobb to mark a task as waiting on something and it records the dependency,
either on another task or on any record such as an order that has to arrive
first; the task then reads as blocked until that is satisfied. Ask it to add a
line to an existing purchase or sales order with a quantity and either a
description or a part you track. Removing a line stays in the app on purpose,
since an order is a financial record.
