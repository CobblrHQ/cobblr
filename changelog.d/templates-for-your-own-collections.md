Templates work for your own collections: a Bookshelf, or any collection built on Records, can have templates and offers New from template, the Kind of thing list is the one your workspace has, and the starting values are filled in as fields, not typed as JSON.

## docs

**Templates for your own collections.** In **Configuration → Templates**, the **Kind of thing** list is what your workspace has, named as you know it, and includes a collection built on Records such as a Bookshelf. On that collection's page, open the menu beside **New** and choose **New from template**; pick one, review the filled fields, change what you need, and press **Save**. You give a template's starting values as the kind's own fields, and choose a collection under **Fields of** for its own fields; the JSON is kept under **Advanced**.
