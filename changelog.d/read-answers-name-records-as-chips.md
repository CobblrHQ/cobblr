When Cobb names one of your records in an answer, the name is now a chip you can open, not only in the cards for changes he made but in his read answers too: ask which books you have and each book in the reply opens its own page, the words around it kept. The chip works from the keyboard, tells a screen reader what kind of thing it opens, and says so if the record has been deleted since. Cobb never links a name he did not just look up, and a name two records share stays plain text rather than a guess. The answer that appears while you type, "what is low?", and the result of a change (a done card whose words do not name the record still shows it as a chip) name their records the same way, and the chips stay when you come back to the conversation.
