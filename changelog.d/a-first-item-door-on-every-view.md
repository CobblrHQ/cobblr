**An empty collection invites your first one.** A fresh Bookshelf opening on its cover wall, or a Groceries board with nothing on it yet, now says "Add your first book" (or item) with the same add door the table has and the scan door beside it, instead of "No matching books." A view whose filter hid everything still says "No matching", and offers everything.
