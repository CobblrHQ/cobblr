An item that arrives damaged can be marked from its scan: not added to stock, and shown as arrived damaged where the delivery is checked.

## docs

**Arrived damaged? Say so from the scan.** Something that came in a delivery damaged is not stock. **Arrived damaged**, under **More** on the scan's card, settles the scan without adding anything to the count or making a record, and keeps who said so and what was wrong. **Recently committed** reads *Arrived damaged, not added to stock · Ben · crushed spool*, so whoever checks the delivery sees which items came damaged. **Undo** puts the scan back in the inbox. Use **Broken** for a thing you already had that broke; Arrived damaged never touches what is on the shelf. The assistant can do the same ("the thread in this box arrived damaged").
