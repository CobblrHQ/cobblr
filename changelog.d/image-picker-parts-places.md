Parts and places now share the photo picker for pasted images, image links, web search and AI Pick.

## docs

Open a part, an item in a collection, or a place and choose **Find a photo on the web** beside its photo. Paste an image link into the search box to use it immediately, or type a search phrase and choose a result. You can also paste a picture from the clipboard. **AI Pick** is available when AI is connected. The chosen picture becomes the main photo; any photo it replaces stays in the gallery, and the confirmation offers **Undo**.
