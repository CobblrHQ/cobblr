Moving several parts no longer opens a dialog over the list. Move to… opens from the selection bar itself: search your places, pick a recent one or something that holds things, confirm in the same box, and undo from the toast. Tag on the bar opens the same way on Inventory, Assets and Machines. Dragging a sidebar row now stays in the sidebar instead of sliding off to the right, and the search icon at the foot of the sidebar sits evenly beside Scan and Build.
