When the barcode read off a photo is a near-match (within a digit or two) to a barcode you scanned earlier, the inbox now offers to merge them: "the barcode read from this photo is one or two digits off from <X> you scanned, same thing?". Merging keeps the photo you took and adopts the scanned (authoritative) barcode, summing the quantity.
