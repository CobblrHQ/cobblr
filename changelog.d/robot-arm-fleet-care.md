Machines show their alarms with escalation, observed use with how much was seen, the tools installed in them with their wear, and their spare parts.

## docs

**Alarms, use and care.** A machine Digital Fabrication follows, a robot arm or a printer, has an **Alarms, use and care** panel. **Observed use** shows how long it ran, sat idle, was in fault or did not say, over the day, week or month, and how much of that time it was seen at all; time nobody saw is left out, never guessed. **Alarms** lists what it raised, in its own code and words, with the program and step and what a camera beside it saw; the operators are told at once, the supervisors and then the integrators if nobody chooses **I have it**. An admin sets who is told, for every machine. **Tools in this machine** are the assets installed on it, a gripper, suction cups or a nozzle, each counting the machine's cycles, prints and hours as its own wear, so a service set on the tool by its use (replace the suction cups every 200,000 cycles in use) comes due when it should. **Spare parts** are parts linked to the machine with their reorder level; at or below it they read low and go on the shopping list.
