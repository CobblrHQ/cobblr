See what everything in a place cost, including sub-places and containers, and download an insurance list as CSV or PDF.

## docs

Open a place to see **Contents worth**, the total paid for everything inside,
including sub-places and containers, with each record counted once. Stock
uses quantity times unit cost. Things without a known price remain in the
count and are shown as **with no price**. Open a sub-place for its own total.

The **Insurance list** controls download CSV or PDF with photo, name, serial,
paid, purchase date when recorded and the full place path. Stored photos are
embedded in PDF where available. Retired things and empty holdings are left
out. These controls and the total require permission to view inventory costs.
Ask Cobb "what's the garage worth?" for the same total.
