**The sorting plan no longer calls yarn or filament "similar" to spices.** Put-away only compares a thing with things of its own kind, splits a group that mixes kinds, and leaves an item without a proposal rather than sending it to a shelf of something else. A plan you just made, or just re-planned, no longer says your items changed since it was made.
