**The nav leads with the workhorses.** Lists, Projects and Purchases now start folded into the "more" menu (and off the dashboard's Jump-to tiles) instead of claiming prime space in every workspace: Inventory, Machines, Locations and your named tables come first. One tap in the navbar's Customize menu puts any of them back permanently. And the scan inbox stops hiding field chips behind a "+2" while the row still has room: it shows up to six, and never collapses a single chip into a summary.
