**The floor plan works for you now: scan into, heat, print.** Every plan's toolbar gained three buttons: **scan into** opens the camera already filing into that space (rooms' zoom views arm directly: "I'm standing at the workbench"), **heat** shades each box by how much is stored in it (rooms count their whole subtree), and **print** opens a paper-white plan in a new tab, one page per floor for a whole building.

## docs

Three buttons on every plan's toolbar. **scan into** opens the camera already filing into that space, and inside a room's zoom, "Scan into ‹room›" arms it directly. **heat** shades every box by how much is stored in it (a room's heat counts everything inside it, shelves and totes included). **print** opens a paper-white copy in a new tab (walls, rooms, and labeled boxes in black on white, one page per floor for a whole house), ready for the wall.
