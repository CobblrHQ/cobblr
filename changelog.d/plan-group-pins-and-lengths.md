A floor plan or drawer can group the things pinned on it by one of their own fields, such as a drawer of sockets by drive, with each pin tinted by its group and a legend saying which is which. Measured values now read lengths too (10 mm, 3/8 in, 1-1/4 in), and the new Hand Tools bundle fills each tool's size, drive and type from its name.

## docs

**Grouping what is pinned on a plan.** When the things pinned on a plan have a measurement or a field with choices (a socket's Size, Drive or Type, a yarn's weight), a **group** menu appears above the plan. Pick one and each pin is tinted by its group, with a legend under the plan saying what each tint means, smallest first and the ones with no value last ("no drive"). The plan remembers the choice; pick **none** to stop. Where a pin's name does not fit, it shows its recorded size ("10 mm") before anything read off its name.

**Hand Tools.** Turn on the Hand Tools bundle for a table of sockets, ratchets, wrenches and drivers, each with its **Size** and **Drive** as measurements and its **Type**. Type a tool's name the way it is labelled, "10 mm deep socket, 3/8 in drive", and the empty Size, Drive and Type fill from it (a value you typed is never replaced, and the fill can be undone). Pin them in a drawer and group the drawer by Drive.
