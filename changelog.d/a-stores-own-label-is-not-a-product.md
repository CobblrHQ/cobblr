A store's own barcode (the variable-weight deli and produce labels and in-store codes that begin 02, 04 or 20 to 29) is no longer looked up as a product. Those numbers mean something only inside the shop that printed them, so a match was always a coincidence in someone's database. The card now says it is the store's label and asks you to name it, it files like a typed item, and nothing about it is cached or shared.
