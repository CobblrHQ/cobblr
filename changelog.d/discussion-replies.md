You can now reply to a specific comment in a discussion. The reply quotes what it answers, and tapping the quote jumps to the original.

## docs

Answering one particular comment now works the way a chat app does. Hit the
reply arrow on any comment and your next message carries a quote of the one it
answers, so a long conversation stays readable without anybody having to say
"re: your point about the nozzle".

**It stays one flat conversation.** There are no sub-threads to open and no
indentation to follow, which is what keeps it readable on a phone. You can
reply to a reply; the quote simply points at whatever you picked.

**Tap a quote to jump** to the message it came from. It flashes so you can see
which one, then settles down.

**Quotes stay accurate.** The quote shows the original as it reads right now,
not a copy taken when you replied. If somebody edits a message after you have
answered it, your reply says "edited since this reply" so nobody is left
agreeing to something that has since changed. If the quoted message is removed
the quote says so, and your reply stays.
