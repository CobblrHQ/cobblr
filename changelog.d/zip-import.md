**Drop a whole folder of files in at once.** Pick many files, or a zip of a folder, on the Files page: a zip is unpacked into its own folders under the one you are in, a file you already have is kept once, and one summary at the end says what was stored and why anything was not.

## docs

**Bringing in many files at once.** **Upload** takes as many files as you pick. While they go, the button counts them ("Uploading 3 of 38"), and when they are done one summary says how many were stored and lists anything that needs a word: a file you already had (kept once, and where it is), or one that was too big.

**A zip of a folder.** Pick a .zip and you are asked what it is:

- **Unpack its folders here** brings in every file inside, keeping the folders it had, under the folder you are looking at. A downloads folder of patterns, zipped, arrives as the same folders. Files you already have are kept once, and the hidden files a Mac or Windows adds to a zip are left out.
- **Keep as a file** stores the zip itself, unopened.

A file in a zip over 25 MB is not imported, and the summary says so. A zip can hold up to 2,000 files.
