You can now add Cobblr to claude.ai and the Claude phone apps as a connector: paste one address, sign in, and choose the one workspace it may use and whether it may only look or also make changes. Connected apps are listed on the Connect page, where Disconnect ends one at once.

## docs

**claude.ai and the Claude phone apps.** Open **Configuration, Connect an AI assistant**. Under **claude.ai and the Claude phone apps**, copy the address. In claude.ai, go to **Settings, Connectors, Add custom connector**, name it Cobblr and paste the address. There is no token to paste: pressing **Connect** opens a Cobblr sign-in, where you choose the one workspace it may use and whether it may **only look** or **look and make changes**. The phone apps use the connectors on your account, so it appears there too.

What it can reach is exactly what you chose. A look-only connection is offered nothing that changes a record, it reaches no other workspace, and it can never change the workspace's settings, members or connections. Its sign-in works only for the assistant, not as a general key to your account.

**Connected apps** on the same page lists each connector you approved, the workspace it reaches, what it may do and when it was last used. **Disconnect** ends it at once; the connector has to sign in again to come back.

A try-it sandbox cannot be connected this way: it has no account to sign in to.
