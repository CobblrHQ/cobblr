Unbox → identify → put-away: once a scanned or captured item is identified, Cobblr now suggests where to put it, from where similar items already live ("Bin 4: 3 similar items are stored here"), with a one-tap "Put here" in the scan inbox. It's a hint drawn from your own placements, never applied without you accepting it, and it stays out of the way when you've already filed the item.
