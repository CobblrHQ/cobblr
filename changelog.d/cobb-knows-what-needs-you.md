Ask Cobb can now answer what needs you, what changed, what you missed, what is due for service and what is coming up: he reads your attention feed, activity log, notifications, maintenance and calendar.

## docs

**Cobb can see what is happening, not just what you have.** He could always read your records. He could not read anything about *time*: what changed, what you missed, what is due, what is coming. Five things opened up:

- **"What needs me?"** He reads the same needs-you feed your dashboard shows: stock that has run low, dates overdue or due in the next 30 days, and captures still waiting to be filed. It is derived from your own fields, so it covers trackers you built yourself. Ask "what should I do today?" or "am I behind on anything?"
- **"What changed?"** The activity log: who created, edited or deleted what, and when, with the record named rather than a bare type. "What changed this week?", "who edited this part?", "did anything get deleted?" A change made by a wire or one of your API tokens says so, rather than looking like a person did it.
- **"What did I miss?"** Your notifications in this workspace, with unread ones marked.
- **"What is due for service?"** Scheduled and completed maintenance across machines, vehicles, assets and tools. Ask for what is outstanding, what falls due inside a window, or when you last did a particular job.
- **"What is coming up?"** The workspace calendar over any date window. Ask "what's on this week?" without naming dates; he works out the window himself.

All five are read-only and obey the same **Read my data** switch as everything else.
