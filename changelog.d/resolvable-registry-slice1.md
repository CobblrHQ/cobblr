Cobblr can now resolve a scanned or typed value against the fields a module marks as identifiers, starting with a part's serial number. A workspace that lasers its own serials onto parts no longer has to write a rule by hand for Cobblr to open the right part when one is scanned. This ships the groundwork; the scan and search surfaces move onto it next.
