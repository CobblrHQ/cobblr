Added send files to copy or move items between Cobblrs with their photos and documents, a field preview and a separate source confirmation.

## docs

**To another Cobblr, by file.** In the Workspace picker choose **To another
Cobblr (send file)**, then Copy or Move and **Preview send**. **Download send
file** includes the original photos, attachments and documents. On the other
Cobblr, open the scan inbox's **Import from file**, choose that JSON file, and
**Preview import**. Choose the collection and placement, and drop or add fields
without a home before importing. Complete items file straight into the chosen
collection; only items needing a decision wait in the inbox. No Take or Pass is
needed for your own sends. The preview names version differences and refuses an
arrival that would exceed the workspace's record or storage limits. No account
connection or trusted instance is needed.

A **Copy** keeps its source. Downloading or importing a **Move** also keeps its
source until you finish the round trip: on the receiving Cobblr **Download
confirmation file**, then import it on the source Cobblr and **Confirm import
here**. The confirmation names the receiving host, workspace and person. A
source changed after the download stays here; download its current details
again instead. **Undo import here** removes only the received items and refuses
to discard later edits. For a confirmed Move, **Download return file** and
import it on the source Cobblr to **Restore source here** separately. A source
restore needs its saved history to remain available.

**Sharing → Handed over** keeps file sends and imports alongside other sends,
with downloads and the receiving side's Undo, even without a share connection.
Send files can contain up to 200
items and 64 MB. If a file would be larger, send fewer items together; attachments
are never dropped to make it fit. Secrets are omitted, and locations, holdings
and stock placement stay in their original workspace. Linked records are kept
only when their source provenance already exists in the receiving workspace.
