**"Just the essentials" trims a thing to its own fields in one click.** When a list borrows another module's shape (a Bookshelf built on Assets), its records inherit that module's built-in fields, so a book shows State, Warranty until, Serial number, and Type that make no sense for a book. The per-thing Fields editor now has a **Just the essentials** button that hides every built-in field that isn't universal in one click (it keeps name, photo, location, and notes, and leaves your own added fields like author and genre alone), plus a **Show all built-in fields** to put them back. It is the no-code form of "a kind owns its fields."
