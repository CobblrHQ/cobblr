## docs

### Trying Cobblr without an account

Opening the try link gives you a working workspace straight away. No email, no
password, no form.

- **It lasts an hour.** A bar along the bottom tells you how long is left, and
  gets more insistent near the end.
- **The link is how you get back in.** Keep the tab, or keep the URL. There is
  no account to sign in with, so if you lose the link the workspace is gone.
- **It starts with something in it.** A shelf of books and a kitchen with real
  quantities and expiry dates, so you can sort, filter and scan against real
  content instead of an empty table.
- **Keep it whenever you like.** Add your email in the bar and the workspace
  stops being temporary. Nothing is copied or moved: it becomes an ordinary
  trial workspace with everything you already did in it, and the anonymous link
  stops working.
- **When the hour is up it is deleted.** Not archived. The database is dropped,
  which is what makes it safe to hand these out to anyone who asks.

Some things are switched off in a sandbox, because it is open to anyone: no
outbound integrations, no devices, and no public pages.
