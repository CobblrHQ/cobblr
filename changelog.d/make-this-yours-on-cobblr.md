A sandbox can become a real Cobblr account in one go. **Make this yours on Cobblr** sits beside Keep it on the sandbox's strip and first on the page at the end of the hour. ## docs

**Make this yours on Cobblr.** On the demo site, the strip at the foot of a
sandbox carries **Make this yours on Cobblr** beside **Keep it**, and the page
at the end of the hour offers it first. Press it and you go to Cobblr itself,
with nothing to type on the demo side. Make an account there (or sign in) and
confirm your email. If the account is brand new and it is your only workspace,
everything comes in straight away; otherwise pick the workspace and press
**Bring it into**. Each line says what happened to it.

What comes: the bundles you installed, the collections and fields you set up,
and the records you added, with their photos. What stays: the demo's own books
and groceries. Once it has all arrived the sandbox ends, so there is only one
copy, and it is yours.
