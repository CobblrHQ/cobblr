A public page can have a memorable address, like /p/your-workspace/my-printers, that people can remember and type. It is off until you set one, shows what the page shows, and ends the moment you clear it.

## docs

**Memorable address**, in a public page's **Settings** (Settings, Sharing, Public pages): type a short name of lowercase letters, digits and dashes, and the page opens at `/p/<your workspace>/<that name>`. It shows what the page itself shows, never a link's extra detail. Your other links keep working. Clear the box to turn it off: the address stops working at once. Two pages in a workspace cannot share an address.
