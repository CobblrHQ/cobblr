Saved views can now be told which fields to use where they previously could not: a Trend view takes its date and value fields plus an optional goal line, a Gallery can show a caption under each card, and Calendar and Gantt can pick the field their entries read as. Bookshelf and Collections now open on a wall of covers, and Bookshelf keeps your books on their own shelf instead of mixing them into inventory, so it stays a clean catalog with no quantities or warranty fields.
