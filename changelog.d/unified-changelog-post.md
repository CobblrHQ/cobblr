Release notes now arrive as one post a day. Whatever shipped in that night's build is announced once each morning, in the same form on Discord and the community forum, grouped into features, improvements and fixes. Previously the two carried different content on different schedules: the forum could publish a full day of work while Discord stayed silent, because Discord only ever carried features and roughly a third of days ship none.
