Filing from the inbox now leaves the row in place saying where it went, with Go to and Undo, and a first scan files in one tap: the scan sheet offers Add to the right table, sets that table up if needed, and opens it.

## docs

**A filed row stays put and says where it went.** Filing an item from the inbox does not make it vanish. The row stays where it was, dimmed, with its picture, its name and **Filed to Yarn**, plus two buttons: **Go to Yarn** opens the record it made, and **Undo** puts it back in the inbox as it was. It stays until your next scan arrives, until you leave the inbox, or until you tap ✕. It is never on a timer. File several in a row and they fold into one line, "Filed just now: 2 to Yarn, 1 to Pantry", where each part links to its table. The first time you ever file something, the row also says where that table lives ("Yarn is new: find it any time in the menu"), and the menu button carries a dot until you open it, with **New** beside the table's entry.

**A first scan files in one tap.** When the camera's result sheet already knows where an item belongs, it offers **Add to Yarn** (with "Sets up the Yarn table on the way." when that table is a ready-made one you have not installed yet), and **Save & next** reads **Keep for later**. One tap installs the table if needed, files the item and opens that table, with a note at the top: "Filed in Yarn.", **Open it** for the record and **Scan another**. The first filing ever adds where the table lives in the menu. A sheet that opened before the item was routed keeps checking, so the **Add** appears on its own once the route lands. On a computer waiting for a phone's first scan (the **Pair phone** dialog, or the first-scan card), the sample barcode's arrival is followed too: "Got it: here's your Coca Cola. Opening it…", and its card opens by itself.
