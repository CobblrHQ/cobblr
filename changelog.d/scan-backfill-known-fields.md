When the scanner confidently recognizes a specific known item (a named book, film, album, or a product by make and model), it now fills in fields it can know for that exact item, like a book's ISBN, publisher, and publication year, even when they aren't printed in the photo. Fields filled from catalog knowledge rather than read off the item are flagged in the notes so you can double-check them. Generic or uncertain items stay conservative and only use what was actually observed.
