**Ask Cobb to remove a bundle.** "Get rid of Yarn and everything in it" now offers to remove the Yarn bundle, with a card that says what goes: the table and how many records are deleted for good, the fields, views and automations it brought, and any feature switched off because nothing else uses it. Ask to keep the records and the card says that instead. Before, Cobb could only offer to turn off a feature, which a bundle's table is not, so the offer failed when you pressed it.

## docs

**Removing a bundle by asking.** Tell Cobb what to remove, the way the app names
it: "remove Yarn", "get rid of Groceries and everything in it". The card names
the bundle and lists what happens before anything does. When you say the
records should go too, the table and its records are deleted and the card says
so, with how many, marked permanent. Otherwise the fields, views and
automations go and the table keeps its records. Removing a bundle needs the
same role as the Remove button on the Bundles page (an owner or an admin).
