**"What you built here" counts everything you made, not just some of it.** The list at the end of a sandbox reads the activity log, and ten of the platform's twenty four record types wrote to it while fifteen did not. Among the silent ones was the list item, so somebody who spent their hour adding to a grocery list, the most obvious thing to do in a stocked sandbox, met a page that named everything except that. Lists and their items, knowledge entries, builds, customers and sales orders, metrics and measurements, tags, uploaded files, catalogs and print jobs all record what was made and what was removed now, through every door including the assistant. A record made in a named collection is counted under that collection's own name. A new lint keeps the set closed: a record type either logs, or says in one sentence why it should not.
