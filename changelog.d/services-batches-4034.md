Added approved print batches with a printed count, a bed-clear press for each next copy and one-tap cancellation of the remaining prints.

## docs

Choose **Prints** in **Print this** to prepare several copies. Approve the
printer, files, loaded spools and full quantity on the Make. After each print,
clear the bed and press **Bed is clear, start the next one** on the Make,
printer station or fleet card. Every start stays in the history. Changed facts
require full review and approval again. A changed material needs a new slice.
**Cancel the remaining prints** cancels the rest immediately; a print already
starting or running finishes.
