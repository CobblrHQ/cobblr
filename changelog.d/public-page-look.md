Choose how a public page looks: which fields a thing shows and in what order, a few words and links shown with every thing, or your own HTML for the whole look, which is only ever handed what the page shows. A page also counts how often its labels are opened, and nothing about who.

## docs

**Look**, on a public page in Settings, Sharing:

- **Fields a thing shows:** tick the fields and the order they appear in. This only narrows what the page shows; a field it hides stays hidden.
- **About** and **Links:** a few words and up to ten addresses shown with every thing.
- **Your own look (HTML):** replace the standard card with your own design. It reads the thing from `cobblr.record` (title, photo, fields), runs on its own without internet access, and is handed only what this page shows.

When the page's labels open it, the page says how many times they were opened today and this week. Only the count is kept.
