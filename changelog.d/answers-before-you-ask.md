Cobb answers some questions before you finish typing them. Start writing "how many locations do I have" and the answer appears in a green bubble under the conversation, straight from your workspace, with no AI asked and nothing spent. It works with no AI connected at all. Only questions it can answer exactly are shown, so an unfamiliar word, a request to change something, or anything ambiguous is left alone for the assistant as before.

## docs
Some questions do not need a model. As you type one Cobb can answer from your own records, the answer appears in a **green bubble**, before you press enter: counts ("how many locations do I have"), where something is ("where is my drill"), what needs you, and what is low on stock. Green because it is a different kind of answer from the assistant's, and it says so: **straight from your workspace, no AI used**. It works with no AI connected at all, since nothing is asked of one. It is deliberately narrow, because an answer shown unprompted must never be a guess: a word that is not one of your record types, a noun matching two of them, or anything that would change something is left alone and goes to the assistant when you press enter, exactly as before.
