**Describe your home to Cobb, right next to the plan.** The little hint box handles quick corrections; describing places is a conversation. **Tell Cobb** on the put-away plan opens the assistant alongside it (both stay usable side by side). Cobb can see your live plan and backlog through a built-in tool, and as you describe your home ("there's an electrical closet in the hallway, the garage has a chemicals shelf") it proposes real location records, each created only when you confirm. And Cobb finishes the job itself: once your places exist, it re-plans for you (a refresh of the proposal only, nothing files without your accept) and the open plan updates in place with a small "Plan updated" note. Talking to Cobb never closes the plan, and pressing Escape in the chat leaves it alone.
