Robot arms get guided software updates read back from the controller, licences on the machine page, and a written safety boundary linked from every arm and cell page.

## docs

**Updates, licences and safety for robot arms.** A robot arm's **Software updates** guide an update without Cobblr ever installing anything: choose the version to reach and the update file kept in Files, tick the maker's steps at the arm, each starting from a backup, then **Read the version back**; the update counts only when the controller reports the new version. Each maker's remote option and way back are shown as the maker documents them. Licences, controller options and software add-ons kept in Licences and subscriptions can be linked to a machine and show on its **Alarms, use and care** panel with their renewal day. Every arm and cell page links to **Cobblr and safety**, which says plainly where safety lives (the controller, its safety devices and the integrator's risk assessment), what Cobblr never does, and the checks that hold it to that: Cobblr is never part of a safety chain, and its Stop is a program stop, not an emergency stop.
