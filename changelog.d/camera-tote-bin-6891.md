A storage tote scanned in the camera can become a bin in one tap, and the next scans land inside it without leaving the camera; undoing a scan put onto a bin you already had no longer deletes that bin.

## docs

**Turn into a bin from the camera.** The scanned sheet offers **Turn into a bin** (up front for a tote, as a quiet "Is this a container?" otherwise). One tap makes the next free "Bin N", inside the place you are scanning in if one is set, with the tote's photo, barcode and brand on it, and the camera files your next scans into it without closing. **Undo** on the note takes the new bin away and puts the scan back. Undoing a scan that was put onto a bin you already had leaves that bin where it is, with what it said before.
