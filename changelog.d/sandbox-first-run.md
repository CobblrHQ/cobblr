The try sandbox now shows you what to do first, and both ways of keeping your
work live behind one button.

## docs

### Trying Cobblr without an account

A sandbox opens on a **Scan your first thing** card. On a computer it prints a
real barcode and offers to pair your phone: pair it, point the camera at the
barcode on screen, and the product arrives in your scan inbox named and with its
photo. On a phone it just opens the scanner, since a phone cannot scan its own
screen.

The sandbox arrives stocked with a bookshelf and a kitchen so there is something
real to poke at. When you have seen enough, **Empty this workspace** clears
those records and keeps your modules, fields and saved views, so you can set it
up your own way without starting over.

**Keep this workspace** and **Take your work** now open the same window, because
they answer one question: how do I not lose this? Keeping it turns the sandbox
into your own workspace; taking it emails you one file with everything in it.
Both use the same address, and both show the two ways to carry on afterwards
(hosted, or on your own machine).

When your hour is up, the bar says so and points at those two ways rather than
only offering another sandbox. Nothing survives expiry, so save before then.
