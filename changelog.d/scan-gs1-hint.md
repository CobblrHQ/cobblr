Barcodes that the lookup services can't find now get a little more help. Every barcode's prefix encodes the country where it was registered (a Prusament filament barcode starts 859 = Czech Republic), and the AI identifier now gets that as a hint — so when web results are thin it can still make a better guess at the maker. Free and instant, no extra lookup.
