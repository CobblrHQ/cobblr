Confirming a scan offers the catalog entry it matches, such as a Lego brick's Rebrickable part, by its part number first, and tapping it files the thing bound to that entry.

## docs

**The catalog entry it matches, at confirm.** When a catalog in your workspace describes the kind of thing you are filing (the Rebrickable parts for a Lego brick, say), confirming a scan shows **Matches a catalog entry** with the entries it could be: by the part number on it first, then by its name when the entry's whole title is in it, and only then by asking the AI among close entries. Tap one to file the thing bound to that entry, the same link **Match to catalog** makes on a record; tap it again to file it without. Nothing is picked for you, nothing appears when no catalog applies, and the confirm never waits for it.
