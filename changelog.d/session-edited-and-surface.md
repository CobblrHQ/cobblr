**Sent-back items get surfaced, and a session shows when it was last edited.** When you send a committed scan back to the inbox, it now returns to its original spot in history (its scan time is preserved) and the page scrolls to it and flashes a highlight so you don't lose it. And a scan session whose items were touched well after the session (a later fix, or an item sent back from a commit) now shows a faint "edited <time> ago" next to its real scan time, so the header reflects when you scanned, not when you last poked it.
