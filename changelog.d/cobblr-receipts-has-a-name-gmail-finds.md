## docs

**Finding your receipts address by name.** Your receipts address is a long `receipts+…@` string, so it used to be hard to find in your mail. Now every email Cobblr sends you about a forwarded receipt comes from that address, named **Cobblr Receipts**. Once one has reached you, typing "Cobblr" in Gmail's To field suggests it. To get there sooner, open the **Email receipts** chip on the Scan or Purchases page:

- **Email it to me** sends you one short email from the address.
- **Add to contacts** saves a contact card for it.

If you belong to more than one workspace, each address carries its workspace's name, as in "Cobblr Receipts (Workshop)", so you forward to the right one. Sales' order address works the same way, as **Cobblr Orders**: it has **Email it to me** and **Add to contacts** under Sales settings.

Replying to one of these emails does not file anything: a reply with no attachment, or an out-of-office, is recognised and left alone. To file a receipt, forward it, or reply with the receipt attached.
