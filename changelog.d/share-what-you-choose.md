A share now holds the things you choose. **What's shared** picks whole collections, things whose field says something (loaned to Vince), or things one by one, and shows how many match as you build it. Their copies stay in step as you change them, and prices, private notes, real places and restricted fields stay out until you turn each on. Any field can now be marked **Private**.

## docs

**Choosing what a share holds.** In Settings, Sharing, each share has **What's shared**:

- **Which things:** add a whole collection ("All 3D printers"), or the things in a collection whose field says something ("Machines whose Loaned to is Vince"). Tick **With what is inside them** to bring a toolbox's tools along. The count underneath ("69 things match") is what the share will hold.
- **What they see of them:** names, photos and the other fields always go. **Prices and costs**, **Private notes** (notes, and every field you marked private) and **Places** stay off until you turn them on, and so does each **restricted field** (one gated to a permission). With places off, the other person sees where a thing is as text ("Garage, in Rack 2"); with them on, those places are made in their workspace and the copies are put in them.

Save, and the shared workspace fills with copies of those things. Change one of yours and its copy follows within a minute; take a thing out of the share (or exclude it) and its copy goes; turn a toggle off and what it covered leaves every copy.

**Private fields.** On any field's settings (Fields, then the field), **Private** keeps it out of every share and public page unless that share shows private notes.
