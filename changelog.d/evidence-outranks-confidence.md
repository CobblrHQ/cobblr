**A confident identification no longer speaks for a field the AI guessed.** Every field a scan fills now records where it came from (the code, the photo, the receipt, the AI's own knowledge), the card lists the ones the AI supplied rather than read, and a field the item itself contradicts (an author printed on the cover that is not the author the AI gave) holds the row for review with both values as taps, whatever the confidence number says. Two authors who share a surname but not a given name are two people, so the row asks rather than guesses. The catalog pane says where its picture came from, a "4 Pack" in a title is a pack size and never your quantity, three different character jugs of one maker are no longer offered as one, and a long thin thing photographed lying flat shows upright in the phone row.
