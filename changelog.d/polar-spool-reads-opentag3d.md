Scanning a Polar Filament spool's QR now fills in everything the maker publishes for it: the nozzle and bed temperature ranges, drying temperature, density, empty spool weight, the exact colour swatch and the box's UPC, all in °C, grams and millimetres. Any maker that publishes the open OpenTag3D format can be added the same way.
