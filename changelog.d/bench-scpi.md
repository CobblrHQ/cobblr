Added bench instrument readings with units, saved results on boards and test runs, scope screenshots, and confirmed power supply output-off.

## docs

The bench panel keeps small measurements at their full reported precision. Log fresh reading collects a new result and links it to the chosen record. Saved results retain the instrument identity and measurement time, and remain available when the bridge is offline. A DMM function change or overload refuses the reading. Output off requires the supply to confirm that its selected output stopped.
