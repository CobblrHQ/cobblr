**The item history says whether the AI actually answered.** A re-run is recorded the moment it starts, so the timeline read "Re-ran the lookup with AI" even for runs the AI never answered, which is exactly when you most want to know. The most recent entry now says so plainly when no model replied and the keyword fallback produced the result. Replays are unaffected: those never ask the AI in the first place.
