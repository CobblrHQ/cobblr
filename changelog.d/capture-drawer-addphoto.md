## docs

In the camera's capture drawer, **＋ Photo** arms the shutter so your *next* shot joins the item you just captured instead of starting a new one. That is how you take the informative shot first (a label, a serial number, the writing on the box) and follow it with the display shot, keeping both on one record. Barcode reading pauses while it is armed, because the label you are photographing usually carries a barcode of its own and reading it would create a separate item mid-append. The shutter turns gold to show it is armed, the drawer says whose photo it is joining, and **Cancel** gets you out.

**Retake** replaces the photo you just took and identifies it again, so a name derived from a blurry frame does not outlive the frame.
