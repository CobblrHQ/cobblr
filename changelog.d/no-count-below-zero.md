**A count can no longer go below zero.** Using one of something you had none of quietly wrote -1. Every way of taking stock away now stops at zero: on an empty record it says "Nothing on hand to use" and changes nothing, and taking more than you have takes what is there and says so. This matters now that the assistant can adjust counts without a card in between.
