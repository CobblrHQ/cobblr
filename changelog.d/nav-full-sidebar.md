**Completely sidebar.** Sidebar mode grows up: hide the top bar entirely (the panel icon in the sidebar's top-right corner) and the sidebar becomes the whole chrome — brand + workspace at its head, your nav in the middle, and a labeled action list at its foot (Build · Scan · Search · Notifications · Ask Cobb · Profile · Configuration) with your account row and its compact menu flying out beside the sidebar. Nothing in the sidebar opens a floating dropdown: the **workspace switcher accordions in place**, and you can **drag nav rows to reorder them right there** (same per-device order the top bar uses — the two always agree). Theme, pin/auto-hide, and top-bar toggles are small icons at the sidebar's top right; the verify-email nudge docks as a compact card instead of a bar over your pages; Calendar rides with the quieter surfaces in the nav. Everything is reversible from the same corner it came from.
