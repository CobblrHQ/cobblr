**Orders that may not have added their stock are yours to check.** Before a fix, marking an order arrived with its button didn't add its stock. Cobblr can't tell which past orders that affected, so it never changes stock by itself: the Purchases page lists the lines from orders marked arrived since mid-August, and for each one you choose Add to stock or Already counted.
