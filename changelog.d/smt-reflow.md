Added paste reflow profiles, oven bridge drivers and authorized station logs with temperature limit checks.

## docs

Prepare a paste profile and oven on the placed board work order, open its authorized reflow station and send or confirm the loaded profile before Start. Record the controller curve or measured manual values after cooldown. Runs retain their paste profile, operator, temperature results and inspection flags, and uncertain starts cannot heat the oven twice.
