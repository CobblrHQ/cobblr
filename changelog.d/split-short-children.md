A pack split into parts of one kind shows each part as a short row under a parent that says the table and shared values once, and the parent's picture loads on a phone

## docs

**Alike parts are short rows.** When every part of a split line is the same kind of thing (ten spools of one filament), the parent says once where they all go, with a picker that moves all of them, and the values they share (spool size, diameter). Each part is then one short row with its picture, its own name ("yellow"), anything else that is different about it, **Add** and **Details**, which opens its full card in place. Parts headed to different tables keep their full cards.
