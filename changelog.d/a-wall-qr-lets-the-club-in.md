**A join link for a wall QR.** A club, a makerspace or a shared shed can put one QR on the wall and let everyone join: make a **join link** in Members, press **Print QR**, and anyone who scans it joins as a member (as many people as you like, or stop after a number). You see who joined through it and can remove anyone, and revoking it closes it for everyone. A member who scans it again goes straight in. After that, the label on each item is the door: scan it and press −1.

## docs

**A join link for a wall QR.** In Members, tick **A join link for a wall QR**, pick the role, and
optionally **Stop after** a number of people. Press **Print QR** beside the link for a sheet to tape
up. Everyone who scans it joins once; the list shows how many joined and who, and revoking the link
closes it for everyone. A member who scans it again goes straight into the workspace. After joining,
scan the label on anything you take and press −1.
