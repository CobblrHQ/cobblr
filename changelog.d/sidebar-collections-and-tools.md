Once a workspace's sidebar holds six rows or more it groups them under two quiet labels, Collections (the things you keep) and Tools (Locations, the Scan Inbox, Labels and other things you do), so a busy sidebar reads at a glance; a small workspace stays flat.

## docs

**A grown sidebar sorts itself into Collections and Tools.** Up to five rows the sidebar is a plain list. From six rows (Home counted) it shows two quiet labels: **Collections** for the things you keep (Home, your instances, Inventory, Purchases, your grouped categories) and **Tools** for the places you go to do something (Locations, the Scan Inbox, Labels, Lists, Digital Fabrication). Which side a module lands on is the module's own declaration: it can say so outright, and otherwise a browse page every workspace has, or a module that acts on other modules' things, is a tool and everything else is a collection. You can still drag rows to reorder them, within their own group; a row dropped on the other group stays where it was. Nothing ever moves on its own, by usage or otherwise. The top bar and the phone menu keep the flat list.
