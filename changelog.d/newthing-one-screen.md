**Adding a new thing to your workspace is one screen.** It used to be three: the first asked which "kind of thing" you wanted and explained the difference between two options while offering only one, because the second had been removed and its question left behind. The second asked what your thing was "like" and listed "Like Inventory", "Like Assets", "Like Machines", which meant picking by the name of the machinery underneath. Now you type what you want to track and choose from lines about your own stuff ("things you own one of", "stuff you have amounts of"), with a suggestion pre-picked when the name gives one away, type "Cars" and it points at the right one. Where it sits in the navbar is a folded one-liner with a sensible default instead of a step of its own.
