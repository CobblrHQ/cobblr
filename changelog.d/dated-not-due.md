Doing your shopping no longer makes five things overdue. A date field that
records when something happened (bought on, received, opened) is no longer
treated as a deadline, so it cannot turn red and crowd out the things that are
actually late. Dashboard rows also stopped showing raw internal names like
INVENTORY-DATE, and a view of 18 things now says "18 items" rather than
"18 inventorys".
