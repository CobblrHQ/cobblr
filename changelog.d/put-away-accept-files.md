**Accepting a put-away group files it, and the walk covers everything you accepted.** Accept on a put-away plan used to give the items a destination and say "Filed" while they sat in the inbox waiting for Add; the walk listed only the plan you happened to be on, so a group accepted a moment earlier was missing and "All put away" was not true; and a finished walk came back as "Resume put-away walk" after you navigated away. Now an accepted item is filed into its collection right then and the confirmation names the records it filed (an item that cannot be filed yet keeps the destination and is named with the reason), the walk lists every accepted group still to place from every plan and says what it left out and why, and placement is remembered on the item, so a finished walk stays finished.
