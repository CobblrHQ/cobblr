## docs

**Who gets told.** A workspace sends a handful of kinds of notification on its own: things to use up, maintenance due, an order that should have arrived, a parcel that moved, a comment or mention. All of them used to go to every member. Under **Configuration → People → Who gets told**, an owner or admin picks, for each kind, **Everyone**, **Owners only**, or **Specific people** (tick the members). It saves as you choose. Everyone is the default for every kind, so a workspace that never visits the page behaves as before. This decides *whether* a person is told; *how* they are told (the bell, a Discord DM, email) stays each person's own choice under **Your account → Notifications**. Someone you remove from the workspace drops out of any list automatically.
