Series detection now works for barcode-scanned media too, not just photos: when a scanned book/movie/album's barcode resolves to a titled work in a known series (Harry Potter, Little House on the Prairie), the series is captured, so scanning a boxed set by barcode groups it under its series just like photographing the covers does.
