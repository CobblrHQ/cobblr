**The scan header uses the whole width on a phone, and search takes the row when you tap it.** The controls used to bunch against the left edge with dead space trailing after the ⋯ menu; spare width is now shared evenly between everything on the row, so it breathes and the actions finish at the right edge. Tapping search no longer squeezes a stub of a field in beside everything else: it expands across the row, with its own close button. Search also sits to the left of upload now, matching the order you read them in, and the placeholder is shorter so it stops being cut off mid-word ("Search, paste a UPC or link" - dropping a file still works, and uploading has its own button).
