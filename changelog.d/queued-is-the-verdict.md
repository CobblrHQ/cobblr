**Print label says what happened: queued, with the count.** Pressing Print label on a record queued the label and, in the same breath, said the Print module was not enabled, as if the press had failed. The button now says "Queued (3 in the queue)"; a second line appears only when a walk-up printer you set up cannot be reached from here, and says what would make it print. The Home Inventory starter's Labels option now says what it gives without a printer.
