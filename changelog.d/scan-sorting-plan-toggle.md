**The put-away plan is now a lens on the inbox, not a popup.** The sorting plan is just your pending scans regrouped by where they should go, so it lives right in the scan inbox behind a header toggle: **By session** (grouped by scan time, the default) or **Sorting plan** (grouped by destination). Flip to Sorting plan and the inbox becomes the plan in place, using the full height of the page instead of a cramped modal. Everything you could do before is still there: category starter bins, evidence-backed existing bins, ready groups, accept, rename, re-route, the hint box, and handing it to Cobb. The dashboard and scan-page "Put them away" buttons flip straight to the Sorting plan lens. Nothing is committed until you accept, and you can switch back to By session at any time.

## docs

- **Two inbox lenses: By session / Sorting plan.** A header toggle in the scan inbox switches between your scans grouped by capture session and the same scans grouped by destination (the put-away plan). The plan renders inline on the page rather than in a modal; "Put them away" (dashboard card and scan strip) flips to it.
