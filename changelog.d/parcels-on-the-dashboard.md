Parcels on their way now show up on the dashboard, and a tracked receipt shows where its parcel is on the item card itself.

## docs

### Seeing where your parcels are

Once a receipt or an order carries a tracking number, the parcel appears in an
**On its way** box at the top of your dashboard. One row each: what it is, where
it has got to, and the place to act.

Delivered parcels sort to the top and are the only ones with something to do
next. A parcel whose receipt is still in the scan inbox says **File it**; one
already filed into an order says **Confirm**, which is you saying you have taken
it in and put it away.

The box is only there when something is actually in flight.

On the scan inbox itself, the receipt's **+ Tracking #** button becomes the
status once there is one: **In transit**, **Out for delivery**, **Delivered**,
**Needs attention**. It is green whenever a parcel is being followed, so a
glance down the list tells you which receipts are live.

Tap it for the rest: the carrier's own wording, the last place it was scanned,
the number itself, and a way to correct it.

Cobblr checks on its own schedule rather than continuously, and tells you about
the two states that change your day: out for delivery, and delivered.
