**A page for something that isn't turned on says so.** Opening Inventory, Lists, Purchases or any other app that is off in your workspace used to show its whole page with buttons that could not work. It now says it is off and offers to turn it on, or, for a member, to ask an admin. On a phone, an empty Purchases page no longer cuts its sentence off.
