Rebuilt the Lego bundle. Installing it now gives you a Sets table of its own (each set sealed, built, or disassembled) instead of Lego fields bolted onto the generic parts table, with checkboxes to also track individual Bricks in bins, link the Rebrickable catalog so sets and parts match real data, disassemble a built set into its bricks, and print QR labels.
