**Live Sort now works on a totally empty workspace: bins that name themselves.** No bins yet? **Set up bins** in a Live Sort session creates numbered **Bin N** locations (plus an optional **Unsorted** catch-all); you write matching numbers on real containers with a marker. Scan the first item of a brand-new family and the directive becomes a bind offer: "go to **Bin 4**, this starts your **Fasteners** bin". Confirming names the bin ("Bin 4 · Fasteners"), the number on the container stays right forever, and the next fastener routes there automatically. Undo un-names it. QR labels stay optional: print them from Labels any time, the bin's identity never changes. The new-workspace homepage now offers the whole flow as a first step ("Got a pile of stuff to put away? Start a Live Sort").

## docs

- **No bins yet? Start anyway.** **Set up bins** in the Live Sort session header creates numbered **Bin N** locations (numbering continues past any you already have) plus an optional **Unsorted** catch-all: grab a marker, write matching numbers on your containers, done. When you scan the first item of a brand-new family, the directive becomes a **bind offer**: "go to **Bin 4**, this starts your **Fasteners** bin". Confirming names the bin ("Bin 4 · Fasteners"), the marker number stays right forever, and the next fastener routes there like any other bin. QR labels are optional and can be printed from Labels any time later; the bin's identity is the location record, never the sticker.
