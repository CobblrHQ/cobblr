## docs

**Named once, known to everyone.** When a barcode no product catalogue knows is
named and filed, the name, brand and category you settled on join Cobblr's
shared barcode network. The next person to scan that barcode gets the name at
once, with no AI identification. Until a second person agrees, the card says
"Named by one person on the barcode network. Check it, or correct it", and a
correction you make is shared the same way. A name one person gives never
replaces one a catalogue or several people already agree on. What is shared,
and what never is, is on the privacy page.
