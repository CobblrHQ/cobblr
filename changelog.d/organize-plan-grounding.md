**Organize plans stopped guessing.** A put-away plan could previously file items into a room-level location on vibes ("Garages is the natural home for vehicle equipment") with no evidence anything similar lived there. Now: destinations backed by what's actually in a bin show their evidence as before; a pick with no evidence is either converted into a proposed **new bin under** that room (rooms aren't homes, bins are) or clearly labeled as **the AI's suggestion, not a match** (amber). Plans also use your workspace's own vocabulary for group names instead of invented retail categories, plural forms and the Accept-all button now say what they count, and the dialog title matches how you opened it ("Put away your scanned backlog").
