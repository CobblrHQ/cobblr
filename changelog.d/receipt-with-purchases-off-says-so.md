**A receipt read with Purchases off says so.** It used to read into your inbox and quietly record no purchase. Now it says "Turn on Purchases to keep this as a purchase", the receipt keeps that offer, and one tap turns Purchases on and records the purchase with every line on it.

A CSV receipt with a Total column and no total row no longer reads its first line as the total (a 6.74 receipt was recorded as 30.25).
