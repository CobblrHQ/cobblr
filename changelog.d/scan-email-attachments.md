Emailed invoices now unpack themselves: attach a PDF/CSV invoice (or a photo) to an email sent to your workspace inbound address and each line item arrives as its own confirm-ready row in the scan inbox: grouped so one tap turns the lot into a single purchase order. Multi-item email bodies ("1x Pico W, 2x 10k resistor") split the same way, and an email that can't be parsed always lands as a note, never lost.
