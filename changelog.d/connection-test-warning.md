Connection tests gained amber warnings that explain the limitations of a working connection.

## docs

Press **Test connection** on a connection card to check it again. A green
**Verified** badge means the test passed. An amber **Works with limitations**
badge means it answered, but the test found something to know about, such as
an empty library or limited access. The line under its name explains the
limitation. The connection can still be saved and used; after changing its
settings or filling its library, test it again to refresh the result.
