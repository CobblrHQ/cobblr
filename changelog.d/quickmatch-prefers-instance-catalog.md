**A Sets form leads with sets, not minifigs.** When you quick-match on a new item, results now put the instance's own catalog first, so typing "falcon" on a Lego Sets form surfaces the Falcon *sets* before Falcon minifigs. The preferred catalog is worked out from the fields the instance already declares, so it needs no extra setup and applies to any catalog-backed instance, not just Lego.
