A new or replaced AI key is tested the moment you save it, on the workspace AI page and under Your account → Connections: the row says Verified, Key not valid, Could not verify, or Unverified, the save says which, a rejected key is only saved if you say so, and a rejected replacement leaves the key you had in place. A Test connection button checks a saved key again. When a provider fails, Cobb, the scan card and the connection row now say what the provider said and what to do about it (a key that is not valid, a usage limit, a model it does not serve, a provider that did not answer), never a bare status code.
