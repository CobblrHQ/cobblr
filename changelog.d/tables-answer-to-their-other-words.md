**Notions is now Supplies, and a table answers to every word it offers.** The Yarn bundle's Notions table is renamed Supplies on this update, with a line under its name saying what goes in it (markers, needles, safety eyes, stuffing, buttons); everything in it stays. If you call them notions, pick that word in the table's Settings. Search (⌘K) now lists your tables too, by their name or by any other word they offer, so typing "notions" opens Supplies and "skeins" opens Yarn.
