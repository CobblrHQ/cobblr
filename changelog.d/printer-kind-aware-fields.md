A 3D printer's detail form now shows only the fields its **kind** needs. A **Bambu** (or Prusa) — a closed product where the vendor owns the hardware — hides the DIY build-detail block (hotend, extruder, mainboard, firmware, bed size, local IP); a **Klipper / RepRap / Marlin** printer you configured yourself still shows them. **Quantity** is gone from individual machines too (a printer is one unit, not a stock count). Existing Bambu printers clean up automatically; a **Kind** selector lets you adjust.
