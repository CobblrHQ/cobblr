A station your team shares now asks who's working, and records each change to the person who tapped their name.

## docs

**A station your team shares asks who's working.** In Configuration → Devices → Stations, choose **Who works here** on a screen and tick the people who use it. The tablet then opens on their names: tap yours, and everything you do there is recorded to you and checked against your own role. The header says who is working, with **Switch** to hand over. It asks again after the quiet minutes you choose (10 by default) and whenever the screen wakes. You can also make it ask for each person's four-digit PIN; each person sets their own under Your account → Identity & password. A change made offline still goes out in the name of the person who made it.
