See what each field was before an edit and restore a saved version from the record's history.

## docs

Choose **Restore this version** in a record's History to bring back its values from before that edit. The confirmation replaces later edits and records the restoration, so you can undo it from history. Workspace owners, admins and editors can restore saved versions. Fields you cannot read keep their current values. If someone changed the record while you were reading, refresh its history first. Older entries without a saved version have no restore control.
