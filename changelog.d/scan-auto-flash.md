Scanning in the dark now turns the flashlight on automatically, and off again when the scan lands, the lighting improves, or you close the scanner. It waits out passing shadows, never flickers (once lit it stays lit until a real reason to stop), and backs off for the whole session if you turn it off by hand. The top-left flash button is now the auto-flash switch, and a small manual flashlight button sits next to the shutter.

## docs

Covered in the User Guide scanner section: auto-flash behaviour (fires on sustained darkness while scanning, goes out on scan landing or scanner close, never strobes), the top-left button as the auto mode switch, the manual flashlight button by the shutter, and the manual-off override that keeps auto quiet for the rest of the session.
