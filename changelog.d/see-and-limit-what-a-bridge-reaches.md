**See what a bridge can reach, and cut one off.** Each bridge now shows what it will reach, and you can hold it to a fixed list of addresses with a setting on the machine it runs on, which nothing in Cobblr can widen. Disconnect stops a bridge at once by turning off its key. The setup screen explains in plain words what a bridge can and cannot do.
