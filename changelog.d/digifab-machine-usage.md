Machines now accrue **usage** as your prints run. Link a print job to a machine, and each completed print bumps that machine's lifetime **print count** (and hours, when the manager reports a duration). It's the foundation for **maintenance-by-usage**: pair it with a maintenance threshold to get reminded "nozzle due at 500 prints" instead of guessing. Runs through a wire on print completion, so it's tunable like anything else.
