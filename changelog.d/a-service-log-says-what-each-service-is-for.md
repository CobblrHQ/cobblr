**The service log says what each service is for.** Each entry on the Maintenance page now names the thing it belongs to, and the name opens it. On a thing's Service schedule, each schedule has a Done button that logs it today and schedules the next one, a pencil to change how often, and the services done before are listed underneath. The Recurrence field on a service entry is gone, since it never repeated anything: a service schedule does that.
