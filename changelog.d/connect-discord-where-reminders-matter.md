**Connect Discord from where reminders matter.** When your dashboard first has something going off or coming due, it offers a Discord message before things go off, one tap to connect. The same door is on a new collection's Set up card, first under Your account, Notifications, in the account menu, and in search (discord, notifications, reminders, dm). You come back with the test message sent and things due today set to arrive as one message at 08:00 your time.
