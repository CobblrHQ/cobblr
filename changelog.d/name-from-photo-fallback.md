When a scanned barcode finds nothing in the catalogs or on the web but you snapped a photo, the item is now named from the photo instead of being left blank. The AI already reads the photo to describe and sort the item — now it turns that read into an actual title (e.g. a museum-gift souvenir box, a foreign-language book edition the catalogs miss), so you get a usable name to confirm rather than an empty "name required" field.
