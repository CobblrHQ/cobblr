A design can now lift its finished-object photo **straight out of its pattern PDF** — one tap on "pull photo from PDF" in the pattern & photos panel. Cobblr reads the images embedded in the PDF (crochet patterns, manuals, spec sheets all carry them), picks the real photo over the diagrams, and attaches it — no separate upload needed.
