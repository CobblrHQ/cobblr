**An order you typed in line by line now counts in your spend.** Spend figures read only the order's total, which a receipt or a typed total fills, so an order entered by hand, and every past purchase filled in line by line, counted as nothing spent. When no total is entered, the lines plus shipping are the total now, in the list, the spend chart, top vendors and each vendor's total. An empty number field (an order's Total cost with nothing entered) now stays blank instead of showing a 0 nobody typed.
