The label already on your things can now be a public door: turn on "Its things' labels open it" for a public page, and a stranger who scans the label sees that thing as the page shows it, while you and your members still get the full record. Booth mode makes a page, its links and its labels private again on their own at a time you choose.

## docs

**Settings** on a public page in Settings, Sharing:

- **Its things' labels open it:** someone who is not in your workspace and scans the label on one of the page's things sees it as the page shows it, with no account. The same printed label still opens the full record for you and your members; nothing needs reprinting. Only the label's own QR address works, never the short code printed beside it.
- **Public until** (booth mode): at this time the page, its links and its labels go private again by themselves. Until then the page says "public until" that time.
- **Get in touch:** a web address or an email address for a button visitors can press. Nothing about them is collected.
- **Show "Made with Cobblr":** on unless you turn it off.

Someone who is signed in to Cobblr but not a member of your workspace sees the same public view, marked as a visit; if the thing is not public, they are told it belongs to someone else's Cobblr.
