**Filing a scan into an app you have not installed yet now installs it first, whichever button you press.** A scanned book headed for Bookshelf used to fail with "No instance 'bookshelf'" from the session's File button, one-tap Add or the dashboard, and only worked through the card's Install & add. Every way of filing now installs the app and files the book there.
