Added editing for saved sync connections and print notification channels, plus demo creation, account notices and selected feedback resolution in the operator console.

## docs

Choose **Edit connection** on a saved connection to correct its name, address,
active state or credentials without replacing its history or record links.
Leave every credential blank to keep the saved values. To replace credentials,
enter all of them. Changing the address keeps your bridge and destination choices.

For print notifications, go to Digital Fabrication, Setup, Print updates and
choose **Edit** beside a channel. You can rename it, pause delivery or replace
its webhook address while keeping its rules. Leave the webhook blank to keep it.

Platform operators can create a time-limited demo or send a targeted account
notice from Workspaces. Feedback offers **Resolve several reports**, with a
selection step and one optional combined reply per reporter. These controls
remain available only to the people allowed to use them.
