**Put things where they live, on the floor plan.** Place items shows your plan beside a list of everything that has no place yet: drag a thing onto a shelf, a toolbox or a drawer on its face (or long-press and tap on a phone), drop it on the bare floor to pin it to that spot, and see how many things each place holds. Every table of physical things has a "Place on the floor plan" link.

## docs

**Place things on the floor plan.** Open **Place items** on Locations (or **Place on the floor plan** under any table of physical things, which lists only that table's things). The plan takes most of the screen; beside it (a sheet you pull up on a phone) are your things, the ones with no place first. Search them, filter to one collection, or tick **Show things that have a place** to move those too. Drag a thing onto a place to put it there. A place with drawers or shelves in it asks which one, and shows its face so you can tap drawer 3 where it is drawn; dropping on the bare floor puts it right there, pinned to that spot, and scanning that place later lists pinned things first. On a phone, press and hold a thing to pick it up, then tap where it goes; tap several to carry them together. Each place shows how many things it holds; tap one (with nothing picked up) to see them, change a count with −/+, or take one out. A thing kept in several places asks before it moves: move all of it here, move some from one of its places, or keep some here too. Every move can be undone from its toast, and its history says it was moved from the floor plan.
