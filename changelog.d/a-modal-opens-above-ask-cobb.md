**A window opened from Ask Cobb is the one on top.** On a phone, a record or a sheet opened while the Ask Cobb panel was open landed underneath it, with only its title showing and nothing in it you could press. Whatever opens last is now always on top: a record over the panel, a menu over the record, a confirmation over both.
