A third pill in the chat header lets you switch which AI answers your chat, from a dropdown of what the workspace already has and what has been shared into it, without a trip to the AI page. Each model shows what it costs: on the free Google tier, Flash Lite at 500 a day or Flash at 20, and on a Claude connection, Sonnet, Opus or Haiku. It is your choice for your chat; everyone else keeps the workspace default.

## docs

Next to "Read my data" and "Changes" there is now a third pill showing which AI is answering you. Click it to switch between the models the workspace has configured and any connection shared into the workspace, with a note beside each on what it costs or how strong it is. Pick Flash for a hard question, Opus for a change, and come back to the workspace default with the last row in the menu.

It is your choice, for your chat only. The workspace's default, set on the AI page, is what everyone else gets, and what you get when you have not chosen. If a model you picked stops being available, because its provider was turned off or a shared connection was withdrawn, you fall back to the default rather than to nothing.
