Added photo editing to every place page, with camera, upload, paste, web search and a gallery, and the main photo appears in the tree and on shapes with photo fill on the floor plan.

## docs

**Photos on a place you already made.** Open any place and tap its photo box to add a picture, or use **Change** and **Remove** beneath it. The same photo control as other records offers the camera on a phone, uploads, pasted pictures and **Find a photo on the web**, where you can paste an image link or use **AI Pick** when AI is available. Add more pictures in the gallery and use the star to make one the main photo. Replacing a photo keeps the old file in the gallery; Remove clears the main photo without deleting its files. The tree shows the main photo and refreshes when you change it. On the floor plan, turn on **photo fill** for a shape to use that photo.
