What the person you share with changes now comes back to you. By default you review each edit first (Accept or Reject under **Their edits**); for someone you trust, let their edits change your records at once. Either way your history names them, each applied edit can be undone, and when you both changed the same thing it waits for you instead of one overwriting the other.

## docs

**Their edits.** The person you share with can change the copies in their shared workspace. Each change comes back to you, field by field, under **Their edits** on that share in Settings, Sharing (a number shows how many wait). Each says the thing, the field, and the value before and after.

- **When they change something** (in What's shared): **You review it first** (the default) keeps each edit waiting until you **Accept** or **Reject** it. **It changes your record** applies their edits at once; each can be undone with **Undo**, as long as nobody changed that field since.
- Your history shows every edit of theirs under their name, "through your share", never as yours.
- If you both changed the same field, their edit waits for you even when edits apply at once, and says what yours says now.
- While an edit waits, their copy keeps what they typed, even when you change something else on the same thing.
