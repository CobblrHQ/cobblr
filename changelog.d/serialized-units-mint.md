You can now file serial numbers under an item from its own page: open it, use the Units panel, and each serial becomes its own record you can label and place while the item keeps the count you gave it. Filing serials into a fresh item no longer nags you mid-scan. The question only appears once you have stopped adding them and the two numbers still differ.
