A scanned item flagged for review (a crop that could not be cut, two pieces of one photo that disagree, a doubtful lookup, no name) is now left for you by every bulk action: the card offers Review with the reason instead of Add, File N counts only the rows without a flag, File lists it under "left for you" with the reason, and the dashboard's "ready to put away" does not count it. Naming it, choosing the field in question, or Looks fine clears the flag. A receipt read without AI is not flagged just because the code chose the table.
