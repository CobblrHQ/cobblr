Added browser stations for large-format printers with reviewed PDF and image jobs, ink alerts and tracked roll media.

## docs

Connect a network printer through the shared IPP bridge, then open its machine
station in any browser or tablet. Load Roll stock from Inventory and map ink
cartridges to Liquid stock. Upload a PDF or image, choose copies, media type and
margins, and prepare the print. PDFs keep their page size; images need an output
size. A job wider than the loaded roll is refused.

An owner or admin authorizes the station before Review and Start. Stop stays
available while another operation is pending. After printing, record the used
length, including margins and waste. Interrupted prints use measured waste and a
scrap verdict. Optional per-cartridge ink use is labelled estimated. Print history
keeps the document size, media and length used. Printer supply readings remain
unknown when unavailable, and low ink raises one alert until a known refill.
