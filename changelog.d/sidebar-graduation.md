The first time the top bar runs out of room it asks once whether you want the sidebar, and the more menu always carries a Switch to the sidebar entry; nothing changes your layout without a click.

## docs

**Outgrowing the top bar.** New desktops start on the top bar. The first time it has to fold a row you did not pin into **more ▾**, a small card appears under it: *Your workspace outgrew the top bar. Switch to the sidebar?* **Use the sidebar** switches you and saves that to your account; **Keep the top bar** saves that instead. Either way the card never comes back, on this desktop or any other, because your answer is your layout preference, and you can change it anytime in Your account → Appearance. Any time the more menu holds folded rows, its last entry, **Switch to the sidebar**, does the same thing. Nothing ever changes your layout on its own: not opening a big workspace, not turning on another module, not an update.
