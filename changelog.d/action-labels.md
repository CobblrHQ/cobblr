**Action labels.** Print a code that does something when you scan it with your phone's camera. Stick one on the bathroom shelf, and scanning it opens "Toilet paper: 12 → 11" with a big Confirm. Nothing changes until you press it, and you can undo it. At the minimum it offers to add the item to your shopping list, and your history says it was the label.

## docs

On anything you keep a count of (toilet paper, coffee pods, a spare filter), press **Action label** next to Print label. Choose what scanning should do, for example **Use one**. Give the label a name ("Bathroom") and the words to print beside the code ("Took a pack? Scan me"), then press **Make the label and print it**. It goes into your print queue like any other label.

Scan it with your phone's normal camera, not the Cobblr scanner. The page shows exactly what will happen, "Toilet paper: 12 → 11", and nothing changes until you press **Confirm**. Afterwards you get **Undo**. If that took it down to the minimum you keep, it offers **Add to shopping list**.

One label can do several things. To put a second item on the same label, press **Action label** on that item, choose **Put it on: Bathroom**, and print it again.

If you're signed out, the scan asks you to sign in and then takes you back to the label. Only members of your workspace can press it, with their usual permissions.

Manage your labels under **Labels → Action labels**: print one again, rename it, or turn it off. A turned-off label says so when scanned and does nothing.
