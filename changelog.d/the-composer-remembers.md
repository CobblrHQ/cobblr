Three things about pointing Cobb at something. A half-written question now survives a page refresh instead of being thrown away. A highlight stays visible on the page while you type about it, rather than vanishing the moment you click into the chat box. And a highlight that names a record is sent as that record: highlighting "Rack 1" and asking to delete duplicates used to have Cobb search for racks called Rack 1 and report there was only one, when the duplicates were the shelves inside it. The context also stays for the whole conversation now, so an answer to Cobb's follow-up question still knows what you were talking about.
