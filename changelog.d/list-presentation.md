Cobb can change a list's icon, navigation visibility and order, grouping, and the word used for its records, with Undo to restore the previous settings.

## docs

Choose Edit beside a list in Presentation to change its icon, hide it from navigation, choose its navigation order, or nest it under its module. A module's default list also offers a Categories heading. For a list, One of them is called and More than one set the words used for its records and New button.

Cobb can make the same changes. Try “hide Bookshelf from navigation”, “give my tools the wrench icon”, or “nest Spices under Inventory”. A saved presentation change offers Undo, which restores its previous settings. Clearing a text setting restores its default; settings omitted from an assistant request stay as they are.
