**A record with a cover opens wide, image-left, instead of scrolling tall.** The asset/collection detail (a book on your Bookshelf, a machine, anything with a photo) now uses a two-column layout: the cover large on the left, its fields in a grid on the right, so an image-bearing record uses the width of the modal instead of a long vertical scroll. The header subtitle also honors your field-hiding now: a Bookshelf that hid Manufacturer and State no longer leaks "Scholastic, Working" under the title. On a phone it stacks back to a single column.
