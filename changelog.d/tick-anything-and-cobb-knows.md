Ticking rows now tells Cobb what you mean on every list, not just Locations. Parts, assets, machines, projects and purchase orders all put what you selected in context, so "order more of these" or "move these to the Garage" acts on exactly the rows you picked. Lists whose checkboxes are not workspace records, like the scan inbox, say so rather than sending ids that would mean nothing.

## docs
Tick rows on any list and the chip above the message box says what is in context: "12 parts", "3 machines", "2 orders". Your message carries those records themselves, so "order more of these" means the ones you picked rather than a search. This works on Parts, Assets, Machines, Projects, Purchases and Locations. A few screens select things that are not records yet, like the scan inbox, and those deliberately do not put anything in context.
