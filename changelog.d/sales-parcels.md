An order can now be sent in several parcels: each one keeps its own tracking number, carrier and what is in it, and the order reads shipped once every line is in a parcel.

## docs

**Parcels.** An order that goes out in more than one box lists each one in its **Parcels** panel: the carrier, the date, what it carries and its tracking number. **Ship the 2 you have** records a parcel for what left, and Ship on an order that is short of stock sends what is on hand as a parcel with the tracking number you type; the order stays open until every line is in a parcel. A parcel recorded without a number takes it later with **Add the tracking number**, and stock taken out that is not in a box yet has a **Record a parcel** button. A line reads "taken from stock" until a parcel carries it. Telling the customer works per parcel, and is still never ticked for you.
