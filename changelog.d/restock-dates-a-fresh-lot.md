**Restocking from the shopping list dates the fresh stock, and something you have used up is no longer "overdue".** Checking milk off the list used to bump the count and leave the old lot's dates on it, so the milk you just bought read "expired 5 days ago". Now the check-off starts a fresh lot: bought today, good until today plus the item's shelf life, and the row says so before you check it. An item at zero drops out of the expiry schedule and the dashboard until it is restocked; there is nothing left to spoil.
