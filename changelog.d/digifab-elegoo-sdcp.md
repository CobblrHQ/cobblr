**Elegoo printers, no middleman.** A new built-in `elegoo_sdcp` driver speaks the Centauri Carbon's own SDCP protocol directly on your LAN — discovery, status, upload, print start/pause/stop — with no manager software in between. Point a connection at the printer's IP and it joins the fleet, pools, and production runs like everything else. *Hardware-unverified first release — written to the SDCP spec and field-observed firmware behavior; tell us what your printer does.*
