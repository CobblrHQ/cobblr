One machine record can now stand for several identical machines, and any of them can have a name of its own under it. A record of five printers says so in the list and counts as five printers, not one entry, including when you ask the assistant how many you have. Name one to give it its own notes, service log, label and links while it stays one of the five, split it into a record of its own at any time, or make a single machine one of a group again.

## docs

**Several identical machines in one record.** A machine record can stand for
more than one machine: set its **Quantity**. The list says so under the name
("×5 · 2 named"), its tile shows "×5" in the corner, and the count beside the page title gives machines as well as
entries.

**Name one** on the machine's page gives one of them an identity of its own
under the record: its own name, serial number, state, notes, service log, label
and links. It stays one of the record's count and never shows in the list on
its own; its page says which record it is one of.

**Split into its own** makes a named one a record of its own, keeping
everything it had, and the group counts one fewer. **Split one off** does the
same for one with no name yet. On a single machine, **One of a group…** makes
it one of another record's machines, counted again. Split and merge undo each
other.
