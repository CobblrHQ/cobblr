Sets and Bricks share one navigation row, and you can join or split collection rows from their menu or by asking Cobb.

## docs

Two or three collections can share a navigation row, each with its own link.
Use Arrange to split a row or join another collection. Cobb also accepts
“put Sets and Bricks on one line” and “split LEGO row”. Choices stay on this
device and survive reloads without changing other people's navigation.
