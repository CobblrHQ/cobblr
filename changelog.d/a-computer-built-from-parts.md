A computer, a machine or any asset can now be built from parts you have recorded: install some of a part from its page (2 of 4 RAM sticks), see each part's value and the total, take parts back out with undo, and hide what has nothing on hand from the Inventory list.

## docs

**Built from parts.** On an asset or a machine, **Install** in its Contents puts a part you have recorded inside it. For a part you have several of, say how many (2 of 4 RAM sticks): those go inside, the rest stay where they were, and the part's page shows both places under Where it's kept. Installing more of it later joins the ones already inside.

Each installed part shows its count and its value, and the foot of the panel adds them up: **Other value (not itemized)** is what you paid for the thing itself besides its parts (labour, a prebuilt's base), then **Parts**, then the **Total**. Once a thing has parts inside it, its own price reads **Other value (not itemized)**, so nothing is counted twice: a place's **worth** and any total count each part once, as itself. A part's value is its cost times its count, so someone who may not see costs gets those values left out, and the panel says so.

**Take out** sends a part back where it came from, where it joins the spares; **to…** takes it to a place you pick. Both offer **Undo**. The thing's own History says what was installed in it and what was taken out, and when.

**In the Inventory list,** an installed part stays listed, because it is still yours and still worth what you paid: the row reads "0 spare · 1 installed in Gaming PC", or "2 spare · 2 installed in Gaming PC" when some are left. **Hide unavailable** leaves out the rows with nothing on hand (everything installed, or none left). It is off until you turn it on, and remembered per list and per saved view. Only being inside an asset or a machine counts as installed: a part in a box, a bin or on a shelf is on hand and never hidden.

The assistant does the same: "install 2 of the RAM sticks in the Gaming PC", "take the SSD out", and "what is the Gaming PC built from?" with its value.
