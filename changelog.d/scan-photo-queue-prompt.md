The scanner now greets you with the items you marked for a photo, so you never have to find them again in a long inbox. Tap the line to point the camera at one; your next shot becomes its picture.

## docs

When you have marked items with the camera button, opening the scanner shows a single line at the top naming the first one, with a count if there are more behind it.

Tapping anywhere on that line points the camera at that item: the next photo you take becomes its picture, and the mark clears. Pressing the ✕ means not now. It moves to the next marked item and keeps the mark, so it asks again next time you open the scanner. Clearing a mark for good is the camera button on the item's own card.

The line only appears while you are actually scanning, so it never covers a result you are reading.
