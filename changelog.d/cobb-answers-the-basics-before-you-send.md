Ask Cobb now answers the everyday questions while you are still typing them, in a green bubble that says where the answer came from and costs no AI call. "What can you do?", "where do I scan?" and the rest come from what he already knows; questions about your own records still come from a read of your workspace. Cobb's written answers also know whether your workspace has AI connected, so a connected workspace is never told it isn't.

## docs
Three kinds of answer arrive before you press enter, each labelled in the bubble:

- **from the page you are on** - "what page am I on?", "where am I". The browser already knows, so nothing is asked of anyone.
- **straight from your workspace** - "how many parts do I have?", "what's low on stock?". One read of your records, no AI.
- **from what Cobb already knows** - "what can you do?", "how do I add a part", "where do I scan?". Cobb's built-in answers, which you can edit and add to under Configuration.

Press enter only if you want the full answer from AI. If the green bubble already said it, you have saved the call.
