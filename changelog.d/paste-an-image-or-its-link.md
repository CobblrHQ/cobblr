Paste now means paste when you are choosing a picture. Paste an image address into the image search box and Cobblr fetches that exact picture instead of running the address as a search phrase, and the button says Use rather than Search as soon as what you typed is a link. Press Cmd+V with a picture on your clipboard anywhere in the scan inbox that shows photos, either the search box under an item or the full-screen viewer, and that screenshot becomes the item's catalog photo. Copying an image from a web page puts both the picture and its address on the clipboard; the picture wins, because it needs no second trip to the site and cannot be blocked by it.

## docs

When you already have the picture you want, you do not have to search for it. The image search box (under an inbox item, in the full-screen viewer, and on a record or machine) understands a pasted link: paste an image address, press Enter, and Cobblr fetches that exact picture and keeps it rather than treating the address as words to search for. The button beside the box says Use instead of Search as soon as what you typed is a link, so you can see which it will do.

Cmd+V (Ctrl+V) with a picture on your clipboard works too, anywhere in the scan inbox that shows photos: in the search box under an item, and anywhere in the full-screen viewer while it is open. A screenshot or a copied image becomes that item's catalog photo straight away.
