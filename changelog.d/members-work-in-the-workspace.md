**Members now land in the workspace, not the member portal.** A member does the day-to-day work: the scan inbox, every module's pages, every record's own link, scanning and filing, adding records where a module allows it. Configuration, the bundle composer and the builder stay with owners, admins and editors and are simply not shown to a member; the portal remains reachable from the account menu and offers a way back. A guest still lands in the portal and reads what the workspace shares there. In the scan inbox, a row a member cannot file says Ask an admin on the row, on its screen and in the session strip alike, and the strip says "waiting on an admin" instead of offering to file it.

## docs

Every role but guest works in the workspace. A **member** sees the whole workspace (the scan inbox, every module's pages, every record's own link), scans and files, and adds records where the module allows; where a module gates one action on a capability (creating or editing inventory parts), the row or button offers **Ask** rather than a dead end. Members have no say over people or settings, cannot install bundles, and are not shown Configuration or the builder. A **guest** lands in the member portal and reads what the workspace shares there. Owners, admins and members can open the portal from the account menu; its header offers **workspace** (or **admin**) to come back.
