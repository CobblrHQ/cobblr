The dashboard now opens with a **"needs you"** feed: things that are low on stock, dates that are overdue or coming up in the next 30 days (renewals, refills, returns, maintenance — read from your trackers' own date fields, whatever you named them), and captures waiting to be filed. Each line is one tap to act; when nothing needs you, it shows nothing.
