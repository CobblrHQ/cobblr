Something you keep in several places can now go to one place in a single step. Every lot keeps its expiry and lot number, each place records the move in its history, and the places it emptied are archived rather than deleted. Ask the assistant to "move all the saline to the van", or drop it on a place in the floor plan once that arrives. A move made on the floor plan says so in the activity ("via floor plan"), and a thing dropped there keeps the spot it was dropped on.

## docs

**Move it all to one place.** When you keep the same thing in several places,
you can move everything to one location at once. Every lot moves with its
expiry and lot number, each place records the move in its history, and the item
is then kept at that location only. The places it emptied are archived, not
deleted, so their history still reads back. The activity shows one line for the
move, and "via floor plan" when you made it there.
