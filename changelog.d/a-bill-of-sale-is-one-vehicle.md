A bill of sale, from a person or a dealer, is now read as one vehicle at its unit price, with no AI, and its VIN, make, model and year fill the vehicle's fields. Fees, taxes, title and registration, documentary and processing fees are kept as charges on the purchase on any receipt and never become inbox items or go looking for photos. An extra like wheel locks stays on the purchase with Make it an item beside it. A vehicle you already have is offered by its VIN, whatever you named it.
