**Changing a bundle's features keeps your records.** Saving a feature change on a bundle (turning Designs on for Yarn, say) removed the bundle's collections and every record in them before putting the bundle back, under a dialog that promised your entities stay. A feature change now leaves the collections and their records exactly where they were, at the same links. Records are deleted only by the Uninstall confirmation that names them.
