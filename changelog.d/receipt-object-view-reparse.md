**A receipt in your inbox now keeps its original, and you can re-parse it or re-run any line.** A receipt session remembers the exact thing it was read from, so "Original" opens the source (the PDF, the photo, or the emailed receipt) full-screen, and "Re-parse" re-runs the reader on it and replaces the still-pending lines when the first read was weak. The per-line re-run button also works now for lines that have only a name (a receipt line), so you can fetch a product image or re-identify it instead of being stuck.

A receipt session in the scan inbox now keeps the original it was read from. "Original" opens the source (PDF, photo, or the emailed receipt) full-screen.
