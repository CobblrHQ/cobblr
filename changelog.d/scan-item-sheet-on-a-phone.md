On a phone the scan inbox is a list of compact cards and each item opens as its own full-screen review: the picture, what the AI read, the table it is going to with the override right on the card, a real form for its fields, and the filing action in reach of a thumb. A value you type on a pending item now stays on it until you file it, through every filing door.

## docs

**On a phone, the inbox is a list of compact cards and an item is a screen.** Each card shows the picture (a tall one uses the column, a short one sits at the top), the name with a **Details** link, the barcode, brand or shop and a few of the table's own fields on the same lines (a dotted value is the AI's suggestion, a solid one is yours), then one row with the quantity (− ×1 +), a camera button for a better photo, **Re-run AI** (it spins on the card while the AI works, and reads amber when the last run did not answer, with Retry beside the reason) and a **⋯** sheet with the rest (Replay, Split, Read as a receipt, Undo split, Turn into a bin, box state, Looks fine, Discard). The sheet offers the tools the item could need first: one the item's own evidence calls unlikely sits behind **More tools** with the reason ("one unit seen", "not a container"), and one it rules out is not on the phone at all; the desktop card's menu reads the same hints. The last row is the filing pair: the table the item is going to, with a chevron that opens the list of your tables (choosing one never files), and the action beside it: **Add**, **Install & add** when the table is not set up yet, or **Review** in amber when the item needs a look first.

Tap the card or **Details** and the item takes the whole screen: a pinned header with **All items** (back to the queue), where you are in the queue, and previous/next arrows; then the name with its barcode (tap it to correct the code; saving re-runs the lookup), your photo beside the catalog picture (tap either to see it full size, where Rotate, Crop for catalog and Use as catalog live), the strip of your photos and pictures found on the web with **Search** and **Pick best** in a toolbar that stays put while the thumbnails scroll; then **Where it goes, and its details**, the table and every one of its fields as a form, with the location choices opening right under the Location field; **What the AI found**, folded until you open it; **Correct it**, with the research hint and **Replay** and **Re-run AI** side by side, each with a line saying what it does; and **More** with the item's other verbs as buttons, the unlikely ones behind the same **More tools** fold. The footer stays pinned: **Discard**, **Cancel**, **Confirm**. On a phone a session's header is one line: who it is (the time or the receipt, its order number, the count), then **Location**, **File N** (files the ready rows) and the open arrow; the location dialog says which rows it files and carries **Review the plan first**, the plan-first door for that session (the put-away strip at the top is the same door for every session). The put-away strip and a series offer are one line each, the list/gallery switch sits in the page menu, and the page runs closer to the screen's edges (8px), the cards' own padding keeping the text readable. The desktop inbox is unchanged, except that its open card's photo strip has a row to itself.

**A typed value stays on the item.** Type a value against the table's fields on a pending item and it is kept on that item, filed or not: move to the next item, close the screen, reload, come back at the desk and the value is there, on the card, in the form, and in what Add, File N, File everything and the assistant write. It is kept for the table you typed it for: if a re-run switches the item to another table, the value waits and comes back with that table. Clearing the field puts the AI's value back.
