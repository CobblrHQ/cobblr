Scan a stack of books (or movies, albums) from the same series and the inbox now recognises it: the photo identify names the series a titled work belongs to (Harry Potter, Little House on the Prairie), and when two or more pending items share a series, a one-tap banner offers to tag them all with it: the tag rides to each item's entity when you confirm, so the whole set is grouped under its series regardless of where each lands.
