**A closed rack shows its shelves as chips in its own row.** Instead of "5 inside" and a click to find out what the five are, the row reads Rack 1 · Shelf 1 · Shelf 2 · Shelf 3 · Shelf 4 · Shelf 5, and each chip opens that place. A chip with its own contents carries a small count. Past eight, the row shows "+N more" which unfolds the rack. Phones keep the count, since the chips would wrap under the name.
