Sending a scan back to the inbox now removes the item it created, on a table you added yourself as well as on a stock one. On your own tables (a Bookshelf, a Wardrobe, a Pantry) the send-back used to reopen the scan but leave the item behind, with a message asking you to go and delete it from its own page. The same gap quietly disabled undo, take and return, and putting an item inside a container for those tables, so all four work there now.
