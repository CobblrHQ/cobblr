A scan made where there is no screen can now be watched on one that is: open Live scans on a tablet, a second monitor or a phone propped at the bench, and the last few captures appear there within a couple of seconds, largest first, with where each is going.

## docs

**Watching scans on another screen.** A scanner does not always have a screen of its own, and holding up a phone to check what a wireless scanner just read defeats the point of carrying one. **Live scans** (`/scan/live`, linked from the inbox) is a screen you can leave open anywhere in the workspace: the last thing captured is shown large with its name, its picture and the table it is headed for, and the few before it sit underneath.

It updates every couple of seconds, and it catches up rather than losing its place: a tablet that went to sleep, or a tab left in the background, knows on its next look whether nothing was scanned or whether it missed some, and says how many did not fit.

Nothing on the page is a control. There is no filing, no discarding and no selection, so a screen somebody walks past cannot lose work by being leaned on. Filing still happens in the inbox.

It shows what somebody **captured**: barcodes, photos, links and typed notes. The lines a receipt was read into, the children of a split, and rows brought in by an import do not appear, so a file you loaded cannot sweep the display.
