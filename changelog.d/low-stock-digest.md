Added a daily low-stock summary with counts and minimums, optional alerts for existing workspaces, and a way to leave individual items out.

## docs

**Running low.** New workspaces receive one daily summary of items at or below their minimum stock, with each item's count and minimum. It uses your usual notification channels and joins your daily digest where you have one set. The bell keeps the summary immediately.

Existing workspaces stay off until an owner or admin presses **Turn on low-stock alerts** on a stock list or its Settings page. The same control turns alerts off. **Who gets told** still decides the recipients.

On an item's page, tick **Don't tell me when this item runs low** to leave it out. This does not stop an automation from adding it to a shopping list. The alerts control links to Lists, where you can choose a shopping list for running-low items.
