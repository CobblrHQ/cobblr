**Ready groups get an "I did it!" button, and every destination is clickable.** Items that already have a home now show an **I did it!** button in the put-away plan: one tap files them as real records at their set location and clears them from the backlog (previously they were quietly folded into a walk, which also spawned a phantom "resume put-away walk" nag just from loading the page; that's fixed). You can re-route a ready group by clicking its location chip, and a proposed new bin now has two editable parts: click the name to rename it, click the "in <location>" to change which location it goes under (both use the proper location picker). A related fix: confirming an already-located item now keeps its location instead of dropping it.
