**A task can repeat.** Open a task, tick Repeats and choose how often, like every week or every 3 months. When you mark it done, the next one appears, due one interval later, with its checklist unticked. Choices in a task now show the moment you make them, even on a slow connection.

## docs

**Repeats** in a task makes it come round again. Tick it and choose *every [1] [week]*, or any number of days, weeks, months or years.

- Marking the task **done** makes the next one, due one interval after this one was due, with the same name, details and assignee, and its checklist unticked.
- A **cancelled** task does not come back.
- *Every month* from the 31st lands on the last day of a shorter month, then back on the 31st.
- A repeating task shows **↻** on its line in the project.
