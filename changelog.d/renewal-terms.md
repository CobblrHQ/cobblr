Renewals now keep linked terms with coverage dates, costs and documents, and let you end a renewal without losing its history.

## docs


The Terms list keeps each renewal's dates, cost and currency, reference,
documents, seats or quantity, and who renewed it. Renewed prefills the last
cost and next coverage dates. Edit term corrects a past entry while preventing
overlap. Lifetime and payment-year totals keep currencies separate.

Didn't renew ends a chain and stops future reminders without removing past
terms. Stop reminding me still silences only the current occurrence. Cobb
and MCP can read paid terms, renew at a stated cost and edit a term.
