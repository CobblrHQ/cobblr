**A dropdown field can carry a one-off note beside its value.** Pick "eBay" from the list and note the specific seller next to it, and the record reads "eBay · detroitaxle". The value stays "eBay", so grouping, filters and saved views still gather every eBay purchase together however many sellers you have noted. Useful when a detail is worth keeping but does not deserve a permanent spot in your choices list.
