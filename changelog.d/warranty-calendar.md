Added warranty and purchase dates to the calendar and iCal feed, with separate filters and optional 30-day warranty reminders.

## docs

Warranty ends on parts and assets, and asset purchase dates, appear on the
calendar and in its iCal feed. Use Show on calendar to hide either source on
this device. Purchase dates never become overdue.

An owner or admin can choose Remind us 30 days before a warranty ends for the
workspace. It starts on for new workspaces and stays off for existing ones
until you enable it. Reminders appear in notifications and join your daily
digest; choose its time in Your account → Delivery. Lifetime warranties and
things marked gone are left out.
