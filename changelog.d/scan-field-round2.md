Two field fixes on the scanner. The catalog picture now takes over the main image the moment it arrives, marked "checking" until it is confirmed against your photo, instead of appearing in the strip seconds before the main image caught up; if the check concludes it is the wrong product, your own photo steps back in. And the auto-flash no longer lights up while the phone dangles at your side: a flash burn that reveals nothing scannable ends after a few seconds and stays off until you actually raise the phone to something.
