### Promoting a category into its own table

Your catch-all table collects categories as you scan: Electrical, Plumbing, Fasteners. Sooner or later one of them earns a table of its own, because it has a lot of items in it or because it wants fields the others do not need.

**Promote** it, and that category becomes its own table. Its items move across but keep their **ids, their history, their pairings and their QR labels**, so a printed bin label still resolves to the same item afterwards. The fields the parent table defined come with them, so nothing that was filled in is suddenly unshowable.

**Demote** does the reverse: the table's items fold back into the parent as a category, with that category written onto each of them and added to the parent's category list.

The pair is deliberately reversible. Promote, demote, and promote again leaves you exactly where you started. That is the point: you should not have to design your table structure up front, before you have scanned a single thing. Scan first, let the categories emerge from what you actually own, and restructure later when the shape is obvious.

Both are owner/admin only, and both move real data, so a promotion tells you how many items it moved.
