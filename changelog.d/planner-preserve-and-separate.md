**Adding a scanned item no longer wrecks the plan you built for the others, and unrelated things stay separate.** Two planner fixes. First: if you'd grouped some items (say a headset and a mount into "Aviation Accessories" via a hint) and then scan one more unrelated thing (a TV), the plan now keeps your existing groups and just adds the new item, instead of re-planning everything from scratch and losing your work. Second: items that have nothing in common no longer get lumped into one "Unassigned" group. Each unrelated orphan is its own entry with its own destination, so a TV, a headset, and a tablet mount are three separate decisions, not one impossible-to-file pile.
