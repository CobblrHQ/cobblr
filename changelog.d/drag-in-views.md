**Boards, calendars and timelines now move things when you drag them.** Drag a card to Done on a board and it is done. Drag a calendar entry to Friday and it is due Friday. Drag a timeline bar a week later, or pull its end out to give it more time. The change shows straight away and says what happened, and if it is refused it goes back and tells you why. It works with a mouse, with a finger (hold, then drag) and from the keyboard. On a phone the calendar becomes a list of the days that have something on them, a busy day's "+N more" opens that day, and the timeline scrolls sideways under a column of names that stays put. Tasks and projects now say which statuses, priorities and sizes they take, so a board of them has exactly those columns, and a task's own fields show in views like a project's always have.

## docs

**Move things by dragging them.** In a saved view you can change a record by
moving it.

- **Board.** Drag a card to another column and it takes that column's value.
  Drag it to **No value** to clear the value. You can only drag on a board
  grouped by a field with set values (a status, a dropdown), because its columns
  are exactly the values the field allows.
- **Calendar.** Drag an entry to another day and it is due that day, at the same
  time if it had one. **+N more** on a busy day opens the whole day.
- **Timeline.** Drag a bar to move it, or drag its right end to change only when
  it ends.

On a phone, hold a card or bar for a moment and then drag it. A quick swipe
still scrolls. The calendar shows as a list of the days that have something on
them, and the timeline scrolls sideways while the names stay on the left.

From the keyboard, focus a card or entry, press Space, move it with the arrow
keys and press Space again. On a timeline the arrows move a bar by a day and
Shift with an arrow moves its end. Escape puts it back.

Each move shows at once and tells you what changed. If it is refused, for
example because you can only view the workspace, it goes back where it was and
says why. Dates that nobody sets, such as when a record was made, can't be
dragged.
