**The location picker starts with where it probably goes.** Setting where a scan session goes now shows a suggestion first, with the reason ("where your other 3D Printers are"), then your recent places and a search box, above every room and bin. A scanned printer is suggested the room your other printers are in, not wherever printer filament happens to be. Counts on a room now say what they count ("73 inside"), and one scanned thing reads "Where does this one live?".
