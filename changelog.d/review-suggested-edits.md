Edits suggested by someone you share with now wait where you look: on the thing itself, on a new **Suggested edits** page, and in Needs you. Accept or reject each one, a whole record's or a whole person's at once, and undo either. When you changed the same field too, you choose whose value stays. You hear about their edits once per burst, and they hear your answer.

## docs

**Suggested edits.** When someone you share with changes one of your things and you review their edits first:

- The thing's page shows "2 suggested edits by Vince", each with what it said and what they made it. **Accept** or **Reject** each, or **Accept all**.
- **Suggested edits** (from Needs you, or Settings, Sharing) lists everything waiting across your workspace, grouped by thing. Pick a person to see only theirs.
- If you changed the same field since, it shows yours and theirs: **Take theirs** or **Keep yours**.
- Every decision can be undone from the message that follows; undoing puts the thing back and the edit waits again.
- An edit on something you have stopped sharing stays until you decide, marked "no longer shared".
- You get one notification for a burst of their edits, and they get one saying what you accepted or turned down.
