Press the up arrow in the Cobb box to bring back what you sent before, and the down arrow to come forward again, the way a terminal does. A half-typed message is kept while you look and comes back when you reach the end, and your history survives a page refresh. Inside a message that runs over several lines the arrows still move the cursor.
