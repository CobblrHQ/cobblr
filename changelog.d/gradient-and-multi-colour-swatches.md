A colour can now be a gradient or several colours. Add a second, third or fourth colour to any colour field, reorder them, and choose whether they blend or sit as bands, and every place the colour shows draws it that way: gradient and dual-colour filament, variegated yarn. A name that plainly says its colours, like Dual Color Red and Blue or Rainbow, fills them in by itself.

## docs

**Gradient and multi-colour.** A colour field can hold 2 to 4 colours. On the
field, **+ add colour** adds one, the arrows reorder them, **×** removes one,
and **Gradient** or **Segments** says whether they blend along the length or
sit as hard bands. Every place the colour shows (the field, a tile, the photo
slot, the Filament page) draws the same blend or bands. A single colour is
exactly as before. A name that plainly says several colours ("Dual Color Red
and Blue", "Rainbow") fills them in by itself; a colourway name that is not a
colour ("Sunset") stays a name.
