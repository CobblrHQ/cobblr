Makes can now design a request, keep its source and preview for review, and resume slicing after a restart while requiring approval of the exact print shown.

## docs

On a Make that starts from words, press **Design this** and choose a template
and its measurements, or bring CAD source. Review the model and preview, then
press **Print this** to choose a printer and slice. The Make keeps each step's
files and settings. Nothing prints until an owner or admin presses **Start the
print**. A file, printer, settings or spool changed since the card was prepared
needs a new review. Print this prepares one print at a time.
