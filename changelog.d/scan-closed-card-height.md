**Closed scan items are as short as their photo.** Two standing lines are gone from every closed card: the routine "Identified via ..." provenance (it lives in the expanded Source data panel) and the untaken "Not right? Fix the name" offer (now in the card's ⋯ menu; the amber double-check warning still shows on low-trust reads, and the inline editor still appears right on the card once summoned). The catalog-photo strip in the expanded view now scrolls sideways only, so it no longer wobbles vertically under a thumb, and and the add buttons read in action order as a matched pair: "Add to ..." first, "add somewhere else..." after.
