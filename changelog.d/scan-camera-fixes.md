**Scan faster: the camera stops flickering and the confirmation gets out of your way.** After each photo, the "saved — identifying it" note now appears briefly at the *top* of the viewfinder instead of a toast at the bottom that covered the shutter and UPC field — so you can fire off a series of shots back-to-back. And the camera preview no longer blinks/resets when that note comes and goes (a toast was quietly restarting the video stream). The on-screen hint also reminds you that you can snap a photo, not just scan a barcode.
