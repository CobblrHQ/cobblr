**Uploading from your phone is findable again.** Consolidating the scan header into one row put photo upload, receipt upload and import behind a paperclip inside the search box, which a phone collapses to a magnifier: the only route to "upload a photo from this device" was through an icon that reads as Search. All three now sit at the top of the ⋯ menu. In the same menu, rows that are switches look like switches instead of spelling out "on" and "off", and the receipt drop-box address is shown before it is copied rather than landing on your clipboard unseen. Separately, an installed home-screen app no longer paints a pale strip behind the status bar in dark mode.
