A Heatmap view can now be told the cadence you meant to keep (every day, weekdays, every week, or your own rule). The days you were supposed to show up get outlined, so a gap on an expected day reads as a miss while a rest day doesn't count against you, and the legend keeps score of the days you kept plus your current streak. A weekend never breaks a weekday streak.
