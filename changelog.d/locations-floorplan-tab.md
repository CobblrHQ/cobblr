**A Floor Plan tab on the Locations page.** The visual layouts were always there, but buried one click into each location. Now the Locations page opens straight onto a Floor Plan tab: pick a room to see its top-down plan, or a container to see its drawers and shelves as a front elevation at true scale. The old tree is still there under a List tab. Nothing drawn yet? It opens on List so you are not staring at a blank canvas, and the tab is right there when you want to draw.

## docs

The Locations page now has two tabs. **Floor Plan** (the default) lets you pick any room or container and see its layout right away: rooms draw top-down, containers draw as a front elevation so drawers and shelves show at their real size. Use the chips to switch between spaces, and edit, describe, or print the plan just like before. **List** is the familiar tree of every place. If no space has a layout drawn yet, the page opens on List so a fresh workspace is not an empty canvas.
