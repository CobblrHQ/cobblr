The location picker is now an accordion of areas (renamed from "Rooms": an Outside or a Garage zone is an area). Each area appears once: tap its name to pick the area itself, or expand it to reveal its bins inline, with a shelf's bins indented under the shelf so the hierarchy is visible. Collapsed by default so the picker stays compact; a pre-filled selection auto-expands into view; bins that live in no area sit in a final "Loose bins" group.
