Ready-made setups are now named for the thing they track rather than the chore: Plant Care is Plants, Pet Care is Pets, Kitchen & Groceries is Groceries, and Warranties & Receipts, Medications & Refills, Subscriptions & Recurring Bills and Important Documents & Renewals lose their second half. The table each one creates gets the same name, so what you pick in the catalog is what appears in your nav. Setups you already installed keep the names they have.
