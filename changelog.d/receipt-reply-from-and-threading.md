**The reply to an emailed receipt now comes from your receipts address and threads under your email.** When you email or forward a receipt, the reply you get back now comes from the same `receipts+...@` address you sent to, nests under your original message in your mail client, and quotes what you sent. So it reads like a real reply in the same conversation, and hitting reply with the receipt attached drops it straight into your scan inbox.

## docs
The reply Cobblr sends after you email a receipt now comes from your own `receipts+...@` address (the one you sent to), threads under your original email in your mail client, and quotes your message. Replying to it with the receipt attached routes straight back into your scan inbox, so the whole exchange stays in one thread.
