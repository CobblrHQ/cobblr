**Print a landscape label in portrait.** A new **Rotate 90°** toggle on the Labels page turns a label's content a quarter turn, so a wide face like a 50 × 30 mm roll reads portrait when you stand the sticker on end: the title runs up the long side with the QR beside it. The toggle only shows for non-square labels, since a square turns into itself, and it is remembered for next time. The on-screen preview and the browser (⌘P) sheet both reflect the turn, so what you see is what prints. Bluetooth and direct-to-printer output do not rotate yet; that follows in a later update.
