You can now talk about a record with the people you share a workspace with: every part, printer, location or entry has its own conversation, in the panel beside it.

## docs

Every record now has a **Discussion**: open the side panel on any part, printer,
location or knowledge entry and you will find a Discussion tab beside Cobb.
Whatever you write there stays with the record, so the next person to open it
sees it too. This is for the things a field cannot hold: why you picked this
one, what to watch out for, what you decided and when.

**One conversation per record.** Not a thread per topic. It reads top to bottom,
oldest first, the way a chat does, and everyone in the workspace sees the same
one.

**It is about the page you are on.** Unlike the Cobb tab, which follows you
around, the Discussion tab is the record in front of you: navigate somewhere
else and it shows that record's conversation instead. On a page that is not a
record, the tab is not there at all.

**On the record itself** you get a short preview: how many comments there are
and the most recent one. Click it to open the panel and reply.

**Removing a comment** takes the text away but leaves a marker, so a reply that
answered it still makes sense to whoever reads it later.

**Settling it.** A conversation can be marked resolved once you have decided.
It stays searchable and nothing is deleted, and if someone says something new
it simply opens again. A resolve is a bookmark meaning "decided", not a lock.
