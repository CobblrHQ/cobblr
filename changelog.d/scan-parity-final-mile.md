The scan inbox reaches full companion app parity. Scanning something you already track shows an "Already tracked" banner: bump its quantity, move it to your active bin, link the barcode, or open it, and the camera's new Move mode relocates tracked items hands-free. Photos are first-class: multi-photo gallery, rotate, "retake for catalog" (a nice shot as the display image), and "split into items" turns one photo of several things into separate items. Plus gallery view, session select-all + merge, a stale-items filter, "looks fine" review dismissal, bulk "Add all to…", pack-size and box-state chips, an undo on the scanner, and a "fill photos" backfill.
