Record forms waste less space: short fields like Year, Trim, License plate and Mileage now share a row (up to four on a computer, two on a phone) even when they carry help, which stays folded under its own field. The "which one, specifically?" box no longer appears under every dropdown: only the field that says where a thing came from offers a detail, as a small pencil, and a detail already saved is shown beside its value.
