The App Builder learned "now change it". After a build, a new **Refine** box under Apply takes a plain-English change (*"also add a price column"*, *"drop the label wire"*) and revises the **current** result instead of making you re-describe everything or hand-edit JSON. Everything you didn't mention is preserved verbatim, the same validation gate applies, and every refine is kept as a linked draft so you can trace how the design evolved. Works with hosted AI or the copy-paste prompt flow.
