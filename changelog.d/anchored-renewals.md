Track registration and inspection expiry dates, renew without losing time, and snooze or stop overdue reminders.

## docs

On a vehicle, use Add registration or Add inspection to set the expiry, repeat rule and reminder. Renewed proposes the next expiry and accepts a cost and document photo or receipt. Editing the expiry field moves the schedule and its reminder too. The calendar shows both dates.

Renewals can also be added to other records. Choose the same date each year, the last day of a month, or a custom month or year interval. Remind me accepts a lead time or the first day of the expiry month. Existing service schedules keep their seven-day lead time.

Overdue reminders repeat weekly by default. Configuration > Notifications sets the workspace cadence, and a schedule can override it. Snooze for a week, month or chosen date, or Stop reminding me for this occurrence. The record stays visibly overdue. Turn reminders back on reverses either choice, and renewing starts the next occurrence with reminders enabled.
