Cobblr can now sign you in through the identity provider your server already runs, such as Authentik, Authelia, Keycloak or Pocket ID, so one login (and its two-factor) covers Cobblr too. Link it once from your account; after that, Sign in with on the sign-in page takes you straight in.

## docs

**Sign in with your Cobblr's own sign-in.** When whoever runs your Cobblr has set up a sign-in provider, the sign-in page shows **Sign in with** its name. Link it once from **Your account → Identity & password → Sign in with → Link** while signed in the usual way; after that, the button signs you in. A matching email address never links it on its own.
