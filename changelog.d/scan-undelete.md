Discarding a scanned item from the inbox is now a **single tap on the ✕** — no confirmation dialog (it's not a big deal to remove a scan). And nothing's truly lost: a **Recently deleted** section at the bottom of the Scan Inbox lists what you discarded, each with a **Restore** button that puts it right back, enriched data and all.
