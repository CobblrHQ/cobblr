A Discord mention now arrives as a card showing what was actually written, instead of a single line saying only that somebody mentioned you somewhere. Replying from the DM also shows your reply back to you afterwards, so you can see what you sent rather than just a tick. Names and record links inside a comment are rendered as words, never as the internal markers the app stores them with.
