Groceries can clear everything past its date in one step, as used up or thrown out, from the dashboard card or the top of the list, with one Undo; a cleanup teaches no shelf life and adds nothing to the shopping list

## docs

Groceries and any other list that goes off now have **Clear what's past its date**: **Used up** or **Threw out** ends every unit of everything past its date in one step, after a confirm that names the items and units. A fresher lot of the same thing stays, one **Undo** puts it all back, and a cleanup teaches no shelf life and puts nothing on the shopping list.
