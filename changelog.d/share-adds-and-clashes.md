Something the person you share with adds to the shared workspace now reaches you: take it into your own collection or leave it. When you both changed the same thing, their copy tells them what yours says now. An edit they make right after you decide on the last one is no longer lost.

## docs

**What they add.** When the person you share with adds something to the shared workspace (a part, a printer they found), it shows under **Added to the share** on Suggested edits, and in Needs you. **Take** makes it yours: it joins your collection, stays shared with them, and their copy and yours are one thing. **Leave** keeps it in their shared workspace only.

**When you both changed it.** If you changed a field they also edited, their copy says so: "Sam changed Colour since you edited: theirs is now red." Their edit still waits for you.
