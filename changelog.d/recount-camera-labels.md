Recount with the phone camera, read a label off a photo for things with no barcode, and print Cobblr labels so the next recount is a scan

## docs

A recount now reads barcodes with the phone's camera, and for things with no barcode, **Photo of the label** reads the words printed on the label and matches them to what you have (or type the words instead). A Cobblr label printed earlier scans as the thing it was printed for, **None of these: it's new** answers a question about two lookalikes, and **Print labels** queues a Cobblr label for every thing counted without a barcode, so the next recount is a scan.
