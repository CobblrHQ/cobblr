**The locations list folds.** Rooms open to show the racks and bins in them; what is inside each rack stays closed behind a chevron with a "5 inside · 23 items" summary until you open it, so a house of rooms, racks and shelves is one screen instead of a thousand rows. What you open stays open, per workspace, across refreshes. **Expand all** and **Collapse all** sit above the tree, and a **Find a place** box narrows the whole tree to matches and the path to them (type "shelf 3" and you get shelf 3 of every rack, nothing else). Adding something to a closed rack opens it so you can see what you just made.
