Meet **Cobb** — the cobblestone-headed craftsman is now the face of the assistant (the Ask Cobblr chat and the Build page). And **Ask Cobblr no longer dead-ends without AI**: if your workspace has no AI provider connected, it tells you up front — a "basic mode" strip before you type — and still answers the essentials with no AI at all ("what can you do", "how do I add a part", "where do I scan", "build my workspace"), pointing you to Connect AI for anything about your actual data.
