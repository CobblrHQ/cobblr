On a phone, tapping a pin on a floor plan opens a card with the thing's whole name, its size, how many and where, with Open to go to it. ## docs

**Reading a pin on a phone.** A finger has no hover, so on a phone a tap on a pin opens a small card over the plan: the thing's whole name, its size, how many are there and which place holds it. **Open** on the card, or a second tap on the same pin, goes to its record; tap the bare plan or **×** to close it. With a mouse nothing changes: hover shows the name and a click opens the record.

**Colours on a plan.** Give places that belong together the same colour, every drawer of one tool chest, say. In **Edit layout**, select a box and pick a colour from the swatches beside its size (**×** takes it off). The box is outlined in that colour and keeps its name, and a legend under the plan lists each colour in use with the places that carry it, so a colour never has to be remembered. The assistant can set them too: "make the chest drawers red" colours each drawer, and every change can be undone. There are seven colours: red, orange, yellow, green, blue, purple and grey.
