**Print a label where you are standing.** With a Bluetooth label printer set as your default, hitting **Print label** on any record now prints it immediately instead of only adding it to the queue. Add a bin at the shelf and the label is in your hand, so you can label a whole room in one pass without walking back to a desk. The printer stays connected between labels, so your browser asks which printer to use once rather than once per label. If nothing prints (no Bluetooth printer, or an iPhone, which cannot drive one) the label simply waits in the queue as before.

## docs
Hitting **Print label** on a record adds it to the label queue. If your default printer is a Bluetooth one, it also prints there and then, and the row leaves the queue because the label already exists.

That is the walk-around workflow: stand at a shelf, add the thing, take the label out of the printer on your belt, stick it on, move to the next one. The connection is held between labels, so the browser's printer chooser appears once per session, not once per label. If the printer drops the link while you walk, the next label reconnects on its own.

Nothing changes if you do not have a Bluetooth printer. The label queues, and you print it from the Labels page as usual.
