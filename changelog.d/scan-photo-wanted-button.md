Scan inbox cards now carry a camera button. On a phone it opens the camera pointed at that item. At a desk, where you probably cannot take the picture, it marks the item instead so it comes to you on your phone rather than you hunting for it in a long list.

## docs

A closed scan card has one camera button, next to the ⋯ menu. It means "I will photograph this myself".

On a phone or tablet, pressing it opens the camera ready for that item, and the picture you take becomes the item's display photo.

At a desk, pressing it marks the item instead. Marking is for the common case where you spot a bad or missing picture on a computer that cannot usefully photograph the thing. A marked item stays marked through anything else you do to it, including re-running the AI, and clears when you either take the photo or press the button again.

The rarer photo choices (retake for catalog, add another angle, split a photo into items) stay in the ⋯ menu.
