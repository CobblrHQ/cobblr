The dashboard now lists the items you marked for a photo, and each one has a button that opens the camera pointed straight at it.

## docs

Items you marked with the camera button appear on the dashboard under "needs you", as a row saying how many are waiting for a photo from you.

Opening the row lists them individually, each with a Photograph button. Pressing it on your phone opens the camera already pointed at that item, so the next picture you take becomes its photo. That is the point of the row: you never scroll the inbox looking for the thing you are standing in front of.
