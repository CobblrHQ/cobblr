**The Fleet tab on 3D Printers shows your 3D printers, not the whole shop.** A machine collection's Fleet tab (3D Printers, Laser Cutters, ...) now scopes to devices linked to that collection's machines: the laser no longer photobombs the printer floor. Nothing is silently hidden: a footer counts the farm devices outside the scope and links to Digital Fabrication, which remains the whole-shop floor (pools and production runs cross collections on purpose).
