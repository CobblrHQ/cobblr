**Import from Ravelry, from the Yarn table.** One button on the Yarn table (and in Cobblr for Yarn, which has no Configuration page) brings your Ravelry stash into Yarn and your projects into Patterns. The first time it asks for your two Ravelry keys and checks them; after that, pressing it again updates what is already here and adds what is new.

## docs

**Import from Ravelry.** On the Yarn table, open the import and export tools and press
**Import from Ravelry**. The first time, paste your Ravelry username and personal key
(made at ravelry.com/pro/developer, "Personal"); Cobblr checks them with Ravelry and
brings your stash into Yarn and your projects into Patterns. Press it again any time to
update.
