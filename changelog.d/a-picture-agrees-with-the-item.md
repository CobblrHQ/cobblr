A scanned item no longer gets a catalog picture that contradicts it. When the item's name or photo says white plastic, a stainless or black picture is never chosen for it automatically; with no picture that fits, your own photo stays and the choice is left to you. Pick best follows the same rule, and when it cannot pick it now says why in the provider's own terms, so a usage limit reads as a usage limit rather than a provider that could not be reached.
