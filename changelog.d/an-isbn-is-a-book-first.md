**A scanned book is a book, even when a product catalog has junk for its ISBN.** An ISBN that a grocery or product catalog answers first now asks the book catalog before the answer is accepted, so a well-known title no longer lands in Groceries as a "Fit Shake". A wrong answer cached earlier corrects itself the next time anyone scans that book.
