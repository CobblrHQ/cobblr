The workspace list (the menu you open from the workspace name) is tidier. Names sit further left now that the active workspace is marked with a slim accent bar instead of a reserved checkmark column, so more of each name fits. Hovering a row no longer squeezes or hides the name: the rename, members, and default-star buttons fade in over the right edge instead of pushing the name aside. The drag-to-reorder handle is hidden until you hover the row, so it stays out of the way.
