## docs

**Put Cobblr on your home screen.** On a phone, near the end of the guided
tour, a short walk shows how to install Cobblr so it opens full screen from
your home screen, like any app. Each step is numbered and has a small drawing
of the screen you will see; an arrow points at the first tap, on the browser's
own bar. **Back** and **Next** move between steps. The same walk is offered
once more after your first scan lands on the phone, as a slim line at the top
("Show me" or "Not now").

- **iPhone, Safari 26** (the compact bar: back, address, **⋯**): tap **⋯** at
  the bottom right, tap **Share** at the top of the menu, tap **View More** (the
  last of the round buttons), tap **Add to Home Screen** at the end of the list,
  keep **Open as Web App** on and tap **Add** at the top right.
- **iPhone, older Safari, or Safari set to the Bottom or Top layout:** tap
  **Share** (the square with an arrow) in the middle of the bottom bar, scroll
  down and tap **Add to Home Screen**, then **Add**.
- **Android (Chrome and other Chromium browsers):** an **Install** button raises
  the browser's own install prompt. Without one, the walk shows the menu route:
  **⋮** at the top right, **Install app** (or **Add to Home screen**), **Install**.

Then open Cobblr from your home screen. An app already opened from the home
screen is never asked, a desktop never sees the walk, and **Not now** is
remembered on that device.
