**Rotating a photo no longer clones it, and landscape shots stop wasting space.** Each tap of rotate used to file the previous orientation into the item's photo strip, so straightening a sideways label left two or three near-identical photos behind; a rotation is now treated as an edit of the same photo rather than another photo of the item. The big photo panes also size to the picture instead of always being square, so a landscape shot no longer sits in a band of empty black. Two staleness fixes as well: the "the AI didn't answer" note now stays attached to the run it describes instead of jumping to the newest history line, and the catalog-photo search follows a rename immediately rather than needing a page reload.
