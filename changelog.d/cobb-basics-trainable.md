**Teach Ask Cobb what to say without AI.** A new **Configuration → Assistant** page lets you edit the answers Cobb gives when your workspace has no AI connected — reword a built-in reply, turn one off, or add your own keyword-triggered answers (give it trigger words like "returns, refund, send it back" and the reply). A live **"try it"** box shows exactly what a question matches. It's per-workspace and matches by keyword — no AI involved — so basic mode goes from a fixed floor to something you can actually train.
