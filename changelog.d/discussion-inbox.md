Discussions now keep up with you: a Discussion page listing everything with something new in it, an unread dot, and a bell to follow or stop following any record.

## docs

A conversation per record is the right shape for reading one record and the
wrong shape for keeping up. The new **Discussion** page in the nav is the other
half: everything with something new in it, in one list, newest first.

Three lanes, in the order attention is owed:

- **Addressed to me**: somebody used your name. This is the one that cannot
  wait, and it is also the only kind of notification that interrupts you rather
  than waiting for your usual delivery time.
- **New**: unread on something you follow.
- **All**: everything you follow, read or not.

**Following happens by itself.** Comment on a record and you follow it; get
mentioned and you follow it. The bell in the Discussion panel turns that off
again, and turning it off sticks. Reading a conversation never quietly
re-subscribes you.

**Unread clears by reading.** Opening the panel marks it read, because an
unread count you have to clear by hand is a number people learn to ignore.
