Recount a whole collection across every place: scan barcodes, type a label for things with none, or tap them in the list, and pick it up another day

## docs

To count everything of a kind again across every place, open the collection and choose **Recount all…** in its **Import / export** menu (or **Recount this category…** when the list is narrowed to one). The recount lists what it expects to find, with pictures and where each is on record, and keeps your progress so you can pick it up another day. Scan a barcode, type what a label says for something with no barcode, or tap it in the list: identical things are counted one each without asking, a label that could be two things you have shows both to pick from, and anything that matches nothing is noted as new. Nothing on your records changes while a recount is open.
