## docs

### Search inside your patterns

A word or phrase inside a pattern finds the pattern. Search for *magic ring*,
*worsted* or *amigurumi* from the search bar, the palette, the search page or
the Patterns table's own search box, and every pattern whose PDF says it comes
back, with the line it is on under the pattern's name. A pattern saved from
Ask Cobb is found by its words the same way.

Patterns you imported before this are read in the background, a few at a
time, so an older library becomes searchable over the first hours after the
update. A PDF that is only pictures of pages (a scan) has no words to find.
