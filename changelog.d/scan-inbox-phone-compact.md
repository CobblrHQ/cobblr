On a phone the scan inbox starts with its items: the put-away strip, a series offer and each session's header are one line each, the page runs closer to the screen's edges, a card offers a better table beside its filing pair, and the picture strip outlines the catalog picture.

## docs

**The top of the queue on a phone is four short lines.** The put-away strip is one line ("187 without a home, 4 ready · Put away"); a series offer is one line ("2 in the "X" series · Tag"); a session's header is one line: who it is (the time or the receipt, its order number, the count), then the location pin, **File N** and the open arrow. Tapping the session's name folds it. **Review plan** is no longer a button on every session: the put-away strip at the top is that door for every session, and the **File N** dialog carries **Review the plan first** for one; the single-session page keeps the button. The list/gallery switch sits in the page menu (⋯ → Show as). The page's side gutter is 8px on a phone; the card keeps its own padding.

**A better table sits beside the pair.** When a table set up after the scan was routed fits it better, the card offers it to the right of `[table ▾][Add]` as "Groceries?"; a tap moves the pair to that table and files nothing.

**The picture strip outlines the catalog picture.** On both surfaces the tile that is the item's catalog picture carries the accent outline; it follows a new pick, and no tile carries it when the picture is not among the tiles. On a phone the item screen shows **Catalog** then **Your photo**, the desktop's order.
