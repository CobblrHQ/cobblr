A scanned or forwarded receipt can now become **one purchase order** instead of a pile of unrelated parts. With the **Purchases** module on, a receipt in your scan inbox shows a **"Confirm as purchase order"** banner: one tap creates a purchase order (vendor, date, total) and files every line as an order item linked to its part. You can still fix or discard individual lines first; it rolls up whatever's left. Without Purchases, the lines just confirm into parts as before.
