Connecting a Bambu printer now lives right in the **New 3D printer** flow: pick **what kind of printer** it is (Bambu, Klipper, Prusa, …) and **how to connect** — **Directly through Bambu API** or **through a manager** (FDM Monster / OctoPrint). Choosing Bambu-direct signs you into your Bambu account **once**, discovers **every** printer on it, and lets you pick which one this is — with the name and model **pre-filled** and the printer linked the moment you create it. One login covers your whole fleet.
