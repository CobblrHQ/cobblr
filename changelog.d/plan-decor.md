**Items on the plan, photos in the rects, trapezoids for the wrenches.** A layout can now place the things themselves: machines, assets, and parts each take a cell on their location's plan, with an "items here, not placed" strip in edit mode and drag-to-re-file into any box. Placed rectangles (items and bins alike) can carry decoration: photo fill, or a silhouette outline (taper-left/right, triangle) drawn inside. Overlapping rectangles are fine; the smaller stays clickable on top.

## docs

A drawer's plan isn't only for sub-bins: the ratchets, the impact gun, and the parts filed there can each take a cell. In edit mode, everything located here but not yet placed waits in an "items here, not placed" strip; tap to drop, drag to true it up, and dragging an item into another box re-files it there. Every placed rectangle can carry decoration: photo fill (the rect renders the record's own photo) or a silhouette outline (taper-left / taper-right for fanned wrench rails, triangle). Rectangles may overlap; the smaller one stays clickable on top.
