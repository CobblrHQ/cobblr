The **Configuration** page is no longer a wall. It now opens to the ~11 settings you actually use day-to-day, grouped and fully visible; the other two dozen (tokens, integrations, diagnostics, …) tuck behind a single **Show advanced settings** button. Search still finds every setting regardless. And **Simple mode** now does something visible: turning it on drops you straight onto your clean workspace home — and if you come back to Configuration it shows a calm one-card state instead of the control room, with one click to turn it back off.
