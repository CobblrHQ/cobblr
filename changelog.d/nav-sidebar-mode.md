**Your nav, your way: top bar or left sidebar.** A busy workspace runs the top navbar out of room fast — so, Vivaldi-style, the tiny panel icon left of the **cobblr** wordmark now flips navigation into a skinny **left sidebar** (and back). In sidebar mode every dropdown child — specialisations, instances, grouped categories — is simply *there*, indented in the hierarchy, no hover menus and no "more ▾" fold. The sidebar can be **pinned** (a fixed column) or **auto-hiding** (a thin left-edge strip that slides the panel over the page when you reach for it) — flip that with the pin at the sidebar's foot. Per-device preference; mobile keeps its menu either way.
