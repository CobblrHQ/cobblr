**Every record has a History.** Open anything and its History panel lists who changed it and what, newest first, so "who renamed this, and when" has an answer on the record itself.

## docs

Every record now has a **History** panel. It lists each change to that record, newest first: who made it, whether by hand, through the assistant or an automation, and which fields changed ("name → Tall ladder"). Press **Show earlier** for older changes.
