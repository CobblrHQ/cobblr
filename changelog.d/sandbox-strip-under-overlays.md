The sandbox countdown strip no longer covers the camera's shutter or any open dialog on a phone: it steps aside whenever something is open over the page and comes back when it closes. For most of the hour it is now one quiet line with a single Keep button; the full bar with both choices appears for the last fifteen minutes. The feedback bubble rides up over the strip instead of sitting on its buttons.
