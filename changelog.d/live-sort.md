**Live Sort: scan a thing, get told where to put it.** The scan page's new **Live Sort** button (and the camera scanner's **Sort** chip) opens a streaming put-away session. Every scan comes back with a destination directive ("go to Bin 3", with the evidence: "4 similar items already live here"); you drop it in and confirm with one gesture (spacebar at a bench, a big Done button on the phone, or scan a bin's QR to file it there instead). Similar items in a row route instantly ("Same as the last one."), a no-confidence match parks honestly in your catch-all bin, an Undo chip reverts the last confirm, and End session shows the payoff: items sorted, per bin, plus what's left for review. The Guided Organize put-away walk now runs on the same session engine, so a walk resumes exactly where you left it, and a phone sort session resumes on the PC.

## docs

- **Live Sort: sort the pile as you scan it.** Guided Organize is for a pile you already captured; **Live Sort** is for standing at the pile with a thing in your hand: scan it and Cobblr answers immediately with a destination ("go to **Bin 3**", with the evidence: "4 similar items already live here"); you drop it in, confirm with one gesture, and scan the next. Two ways to run a session: **at a bench** (the **Live Sort** button in the scan page header: a full-screen session with the directive in across-the-room type, a running tape, and **spacebar = "Done, next"**, plus a manual UPC field, a **Different bin…** override, **Skip**, and an **Undo** chip) or **walking around** (the camera scanner's **Sort** chip: the directive overlays the live viewfinder, the camera never stops, and scanning a **bin's QR label** while a directive shows files the item into that bin instead). Similar items in a row route instantly ("Same as the last one."). No confident answer parks the item in your catch-all rather than guessing, and it stays in the scan inbox for review. **End session** shows the payoff: items sorted, per bin, plus what's left for review. Toggling Sort off pauses the session; it resumes where you left off, on the phone or the PC (it is the same session).
