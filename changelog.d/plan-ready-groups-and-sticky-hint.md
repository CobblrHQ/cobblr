**The plan now covers what's already set, and your hint survives the session.** Items that already have a home (you filed them by hand, or scanned them with an active bin) used to vanish from the put-away plan as "already filed"; now they lead it as **ready groups**: "All set, just put them away," pre-accepted and included in the put-away walk with nothing to click. And a hint you give the planner **carries for your whole session**: scan more items or send one back, and the recomputed plan still honors it; the plan line shows *guided by your hint "…"* with a clear link. Also in this pass: the plan dialog matches the inbox column's width, the hint field and its Re-plan button are one joined control with "or tell Cobb instead" beside them, and Tell Cobb now opens the assistant with ghost guidance in the composer instead of pre-typed text.

## docs

They're never re-planned, and a selection where everything is already set shows an all-ready plan.
