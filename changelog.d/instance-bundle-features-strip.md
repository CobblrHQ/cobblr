**Enable more of a bundle's features right from its page.** If you installed a
bundle in a hurry (say the one-tap "Install Vehicles & add" from a scan) and
skipped its optional features, the instance's page now shows a "More you can turn
on from <bundle>" strip listing what's still off (maintenance schedule,
connected-car…) with an "Enable features" button that opens the bundle's modal to
switch them on. Renders nothing once everything's enabled.
