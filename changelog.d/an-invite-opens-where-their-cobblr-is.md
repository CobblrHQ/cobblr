Sharing with a friend whose Cobblr is somewhere else no longer means editing the invite link by hand: the invite asks where their Cobblr is and makes the link for it. Someone with no account there yet can make one from the invite and comes straight back to it, ready to accept.

## docs

**Where is their Cobblr?** After **Share with someone**, the invite offers where to open it: this Cobblr, or the Cobblr cloud. Pick the one their account is on, then copy the link. Every choice is the same invite, opened in a different place. Where the cloud does not take invites from your Cobblr yet, it says so beside the option.

**No account there yet?** Opening the invite signed out offers **Make an account here**. After signing up, and confirming the email if asked, they are back on the invite, ready to accept.
