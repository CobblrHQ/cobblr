**Press ? to see every keyboard shortcut.** Only ⌘K was ever written down. Now **?** (anywhere outside a box you are typing in) opens a list of every key Cobblr answers to, what it does and where it works: Esc, Enter and Space, ⌘Enter to answer and run, the arrows in lists, the photo viewer and the tour, Tab to take the chat's suggestion, and the comma and Backspace in a box of choices. ## docs

**Keyboard shortcuts.** Press **?** anywhere except inside a box you are
typing in, and a sheet lists every key Cobblr answers to, with what it does
and where it works.

- **⌘K** (Ctrl+K off a Mac) opens the command palette.
- **Esc** closes whatever is open on top.
- **Enter** and **Space** open the highlighted row or press the focused button.
- **⌘Enter** answers and runs when an action asks you something first.
- **↑ ↓** move through a list; in the chat box they bring back what you sent.
- **← →** step through photos and the guided tour.
- **Tab** moves to the next control; in the chat box it takes the suggestion.
- **,** and **Backspace** add and remove choices in a box that takes several.

The command palette shows its own keys along the bottom, with **All
shortcuts** to open the list. On a phone, type "shortcuts" in the palette.
