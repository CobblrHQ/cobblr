Cobb can lay out a form for you: "put purchase date and supplier under Buying" creates the heading and moves those fields under it, in the order you named them, and taking them back out removes a heading nothing is left in.

## docs

A kind's custom fields can sit under named headings on its form, and you can say so rather than dragging:

- "put purchase date and supplier under Buying"
- "group cost and supplier together under Buying"
- "take supplier out of Buying"

Name the fields the way they read on the form. The order you name them is the order they get under the heading, and the heading is created if it does not exist yet. Taking fields out puts them back in the ungrouped list, and a heading with nothing left under it is removed rather than left as an empty line.

A field that applies to a whole class of records (everything physical, say) is not grouped this way: its place on the form belongs to the class rather than to one kind, so those stay on the Fields screen.
