On a phone browser, the dashboard now suggests putting Cobblr on your home screen, even if you skipped the tour and never scanned, and the account menu always has **Install Cobblr on this phone**. Suggestions like this come one at a time, at most one new one a day, and one you put away stays away.

## docs

**Tips.** The dashboard's message line can carry one short tip at a time. The
first one is for a phone browser: "Cobblr works best as an app on this phone",
with **Show me**, which walks you through putting Cobblr on your home screen.
You see at most one new tip a day, and **×** puts a tip away for good. A tip
about you is remembered on your account, on every device. A tip about the device,
like installing the app, is remembered on that device.

Whatever you answered, **Install Cobblr on this phone** in the account menu
opens the same walk whenever you are in a phone browser.
