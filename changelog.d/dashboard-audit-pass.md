**The dashboard tells the truth and wastes nothing.** Overdue **tasks** now lead the "needs you" feed (they were invisible: the feed only read tracker date fields, never the projects module's own due dates). Activity bursts stop lying: "created K1 Max ×2" for two *different* machines now reads "created **2 machines**: K1 Max, Voron 2.4, Beta". Enabled-but-empty modules collapse from full-size zero tiles into one quiet "Also enabled: …" line. The scan entry shrinks to a single row, and the "add more" bar moves below your data, prime rows belong to what's actually happening in your workspace.
