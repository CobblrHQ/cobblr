Settings you can actually navigate. Every Configuration page now has a **persistent grouped sidebar** — your current page highlighted, every other setting one click away — instead of bouncing back through the hub each time. The hub itself regrouped into six plain-language sections (set up · your data · people & sharing · automation & AI · machines & devices · safety & system), search understands synonyms ("roles" finds Permissions), the Modules/Members dialogs are deep-linkable, and a signpost points to your *personal* settings so workspace-vs-you is no longer a guessing game.
