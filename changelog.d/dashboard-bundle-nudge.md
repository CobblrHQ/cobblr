**Your dashboard now nudges you to install the bundle your scans are asking for.**
If you've scanned things that fit a starter bundle you haven't installed (a pile
of VIN scans with no Vehicles table), the dashboard shows a "3 look like Vehicles,
install" card. One tap installs the bundle and files every one of those scanned
items into its new table. It only appears on an established workspace (a brand-new
one already gets the same offer in its first-run panel) and only for owners/admins.
