Sales orders are paid by a list of payments (part payment and split cash and card), Home and Sales say what you are owed and who owes it, and a draft order can hold its stock for a customer so it is never sold twice.

## docs

An order shows what was paid and what is still owed. Record a payment for each amount that comes in, by cash, card or anything you type, on a day you choose: two payments on one order are a part payment or a split between cash and card, and the order says Part paid until the rest arrives. Home shows what you are owed in total, and Sales lists who owes you. On a draft order, Hold this stock sets its stock aside for the customer, optionally until a day; the item shows as held and the same thing is never sold twice.
