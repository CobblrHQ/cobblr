When many people sign in from the same network, the sign-in page no longer says it could not reach Cobblr and tells you to check your connection. It now says the network is making too many attempts, how long to wait, and counts the Try again button down. Loading the sign-in page also no longer counts against the sign-in attempt limit, so a busy shared network can always see the form.
