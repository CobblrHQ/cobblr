A PDF now shows its first page as its picture in the scan inbox and in a record's Files, read or not, and opens in the PDF reader. A document in the inbox is named by its file, with where its reading stands in words. Opening the original of a scanned PDF no longer shows its raw bytes as text: what a file is comes from its contents, not the type it arrived with, and a file that is not text offers a download instead.
