A correction you type now sticks to the item. Tell Cobblr "color: black" once and every later re-run still knows it, instead of forgetting and re-answering with the thing you already corrected. Corrections given before this are recovered from the item's history, so you never have to type one twice.

## docs

The **research hint** on a scan item is a standing correction, not a one-off. Type something like "color: black" or "it's the 5mm one" and press Re-run AI, and that hint keeps applying to every later re-run of that item. You do not have to type it again.

The identify treats your words as authoritative: if the hint disagrees with what the photo or the hang tag seems to say, your version wins. The hint also feeds the photo search, so a colour you give is used to find pictures of the right variant.

To change a correction, type a new one; the newest hint replaces the old. The item's history shows which runs used a hint and what it was, so you can always see what Cobblr is currently going on. Corrections you gave before this shipped are recovered from that history automatically.
