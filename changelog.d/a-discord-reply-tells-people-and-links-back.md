Replying to a mention from Discord now behaves like replying in the app: the people in the conversation are notified, the reply carries your name instead of "Somebody", and you start following the thread you spoke in. Previously a Discord reply saved silently and told no one. Mention notifications also link back to the conversation now, so the DM and the bell can take you to the room or the record instead of leaving you to find it, and notifications routed through channel subscriptions respect the same Discord app check as everything else after an app switch.
