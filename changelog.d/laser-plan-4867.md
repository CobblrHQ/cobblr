Prepare SVG and DXF laser batches with material profiles, sheet layouts and station approval.

## docs

Upload an SVG or DXF in the Library and press **Prepare laser batch**. Choose your laser and import its LightBurn material profiles, map the drawing layers, enter the quantity and select sheets or offcuts. Preview the nested parts and remaining free area, then verify and queue the sheet jobs. Approve each sheet at its laser station. Good verdicts count the sheet's parts toward the Production run, and clearing the bed records the used area. Configure Absolute Coords and save the machine's bed size once during setup. Unsupported geometry or mismatched settings refuse the batch.
