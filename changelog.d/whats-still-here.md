**What's still here?** A place's Stock here panel lists everything still recorded in it. Tick what is really there, set what is left of a part-used one, and the rest is marked gone in one go, with one Undo. Gone means gone by today, not recorded how, so it never teaches Cobblr how long food keeps.

## docs

**What's still here?** When the records have drifted (the Fridge lists 37 things and holds 6), open the place and press **What's still here?** on its **Stock here** panel. Everything still recorded there, and in the places inside it, is listed newest first with its picture. Tick what is really there, or press **Start from a photo** and take one of a single shelf or door: the things it recognises are ticked for you to check, and anything it saw that is not recorded there is offered with an **Add** button, never added on its own. A whole packed fridge in one photo reads badly, so go a shelf at a time. For something part used, set how much is left: half the cheddar keeps the cheddar at a half. The button says how many will go (**Mark 31 gone**), and nothing changes until you press it.

Everything you left unticked is marked **gone, not recorded how**. It leaves the lists and the totals and keeps its history. It is not "used up" and not "threw out", so the use-by dates Cobblr learns from your household stay as they were. The activity feed gets one line for the whole stocktake, and that line opens it, with **Undo this stocktake**. Undo puts every one back in its place with its count.

If nothing at all is left, Cobblr asks once more and says how many it is about to mark gone. You can also tell Cobb: "here's what's left in the fridge: milk, eggs, half the cheddar". Cobb shows the list of what stays and what goes before anything changes, and offers anything it cannot find as a new item instead of adding it on its own.
