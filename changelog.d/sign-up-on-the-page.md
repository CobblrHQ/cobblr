Making your Cobblr account happens right on the sign-in page now: your name, email and a password, then Create account, and the link in your email brings you straight back, signed in. The sign-in page is one card, Sign in or Create an account, with "Email me a sign-in link" underneath, instead of four different ways in.

## docs

**On the hosted Cobblr (one Cobblr account for everything).** The sign-in page is
one card. To make an account, tap **New here? Create an account** (a sign-up link
or `/signup` opens it straight away), type your name, email and a password, and
press **Create account**. You stay on the page: it asks you to check your email.
Open the link on any device and you come back to Cobblr, signed in, in your own
workspace. Creating the account agrees to the Cobblr account terms, linked
beside the button.

To sign in, type your email and password and press **Sign in**. The same
password works on every Cobblr site. **Email me a sign-in link** sends a
one-time link instead, for an account that has signed in here before, and
**Forgot password?** resets your Cobblr account's password.

Coming from a sandbox (**Make your sandbox yours**) or an app's start page works
the same way, and what you asked for comes with you: the sandbox's things, or
the app, set up in your new workspace.
