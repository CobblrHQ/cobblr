**Uploading a photo from your device is a button again.** It had ended up inside a menu, behind a paperclip, inside the search box - which on a phone collapses to a magnifier, so one of the main ways things get into the inbox was effectively hidden. It is now a single-purpose control on the header row: one tap, straight to your picker, no menu in between. On a desktop it sits next to the camera deliberately, since the camera opens the live scanner and the picture icon takes photos you already have; on a phone this row has no camera at all, because the app's own top bar already carries one. To make room on a row that has to stay one line at phone widths, **Live Sort** moved into the ⋯ menu, where it gains a readable name instead of relying on a tooltip that a phone cannot show.
