**Picking a colour saves once.** Dragging across a record's colour picker saved the colour on every tick of the drag, dozens of times in a minute, each with its own line in the record's activity. The colour now follows your hand as you drag and is saved once, when you let go. Sliders, such as the print-failure sensitivity, work the same way.
