**The camera scanner feels confirmed everywhere.** Every read now beeps as well as vibrates (iPhones ignore the vibration API, so scans there were silent), and a Sort-mode "Done" gets its own two-note chirp. Scanning a bin or location label no longer flickers the preview: the stream stays live while the label resolves. Locking the phone or switching apps mid-session is recoverable, the scanner re-acquires the camera when you return. A photo whose upload fails is kept with a one-tap Retry (stamped with the area you stood in when you shot it) instead of being lost, a failed serial names the code so you can retype it, and captures upload at 1600px instead of full sensor size, so saves are faster on cell connections. The desktop "Pair phone" code now lives 5 minutes instead of 90 seconds: finding and unlocking your phone routinely outran the old window.
