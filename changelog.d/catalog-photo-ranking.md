Scanned items now get a **cleaner catalog photo**. Image options are ranked by catalog quality: a retailer or brand product page (clean studio shot on white) floats to the top, while recipe blogs, Pinterest, and styled/social photos sink, so the good photo is the first one you see instead of buried at #6. The barcode lookup's own auto-chosen image uses the same ranking. And when you **pick** a better photo for a scanned product, that choice is fed back to the shared barcode database as an image correction, so the next scan of that product (in any workspace) gets your clean image, beating whatever the providers had.
