No-AI matching now understands everyday household things: bedsheets, towels, bedding and furniture route to Home Inventory; detergent, batteries, toilet paper and their kin to Household Supplies; clothing to Wardrobe; pet food, vitamins and fertilizer to their trackers. Under the hood, trackers carry a small taxonomy vocabulary, a keyword that names what the item IS (its head noun) counts as a confident match on its own, and multi-word keywords only match as full phrases, so "paper towel" can't claim your bath towels and a ribbon cable still isn't yarn.
