A yarn's weight is read from its name when the name says it: "4 Ply" is fingering, "DK", "Aran" and "Chunky" are what they say, whatever the AI guessed, and the card says what the label said. Two pieces of one split that share a name now also treat a value on one and nothing on the other as no answer: both are left for you to choose, with the reason; a colour or a dye lot stays each piece's own.
