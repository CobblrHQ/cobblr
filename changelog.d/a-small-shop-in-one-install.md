**Set up a small shop in one install.** The new **Small shop** bundle turns on Sales, Builds, Inventory, Purchases, Digital Fabrication and Shipments together and gathers them under one **Shop** menu, instead of six modules switched on one by one. Your Home page opens with the boards a shop checks every day: orders by status, stock, products and purchase orders. Orders get a due date and where they came in, parts get a SKU and whether you make or buy them, and products get a sell price.

## docs

**Small shop** (search the bundles for *shop*, *business* or *orders*) sets up a making business in one install:

- **Turns on** Sales, Builds, Inventory, Purchases, Digital Fabrication and Shipments. Shipments has no page of its own; it shows as a panel on each purchase order.
- **One Shop menu** in the navigation holds Sales, Builds, Inventory, Digital Fabrication and Purchases.
- **Home** shows four boards to start from: orders by status, stock, products and purchase orders by status. A print queue board is there too, one click away in your views.
- **Fields it adds:** a due date and "came in through" (Website, Etsy, Email, In person, Wholesale, Other) on orders; a SKU and "made or bought" on parts; "sells for" on products.
