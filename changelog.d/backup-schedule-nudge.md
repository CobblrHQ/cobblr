**Automatic backups nudge you to turn on a schedule.** A backup destination
starts manual-only, and a manual backup nobody remembers to press never happens.
Now any manual-only destination shows an amber reminder with one-tap **Daily** /
**Weekly** buttons, scheduled rows show their **next** run time, and connecting
Google Drive confirms the connection and points you straight at the schedule.
