**Label captions now size themselves to the name.** A short name like "Office" prints large and fills its space, while a long one like "2019 Honda Civic" shrinks and wraps to two lines, instead of every caption sharing one fixed size (which made short names look tiny and forced long ones to wrap awkwardly). The QR is already at its max, so the name is what adapts. The same fit rule drives the on-screen preview and the Bluetooth print, so what you see is the size that prints.
