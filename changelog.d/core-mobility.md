New **Mobility** capability: give a tool, bin, or asset a **home**, mark it **mobile**, and Cobblr flags it **away** when its current location drifts from home — with a one-tap **Return home** that snaps it back. Fixtures (the default) are untouched. The away age is preserved across unrelated edits and shows as "3d ago", not a timestamp. Works on inventory items *and* assets. Along the way the field system grew two generic pieces every module now gets: **relation fields render as a real picker** (choose the record by name, never paste an id), and **server-managed fields render read-only** — no more inputs that silently revert.
