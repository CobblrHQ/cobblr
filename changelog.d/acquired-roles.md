**Four new field roles describe how something came to be yours:** where you got it, when, what it cost you, and who sold it. Mark your own fields with these and a receipt can fill them in for you, whatever you happen to have named them. "Acquired from", "Bought from" and "Source" are the same idea, and saying so is what lets Cobblr work on all three.
