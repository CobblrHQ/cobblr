**Every app is one search away, even when it is not in your sidebar.** Type an app's name (or what it does) in the search palette: one that is on opens, one you hid from your sidebar opens and offers to come back, and one that is off offers **Turn on**, or **Ask an admin** if you cannot turn it on yourself. **All apps**, at the end of your sidebar's collections, lists everything this workspace can do in the same three groups.
