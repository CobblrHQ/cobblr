Mention @cobb in a discussion and he answers there, where everyone can see it. Replying to him carries on the conversation without mentioning him again.

## docs

Type `@cobb` in a discussion and he answers **in the discussion**, not in his
own panel. That is deliberate: his panel is private to you, while a discussion
is shared, so an answer given there would leave everyone else looking at a
question nobody answered.

**Replying to Cobb asks him again**, so a back and forth does not need `@cobb`
every time. He does not otherwise join in: he never comments because something
happened, and he never follows a conversation.

**You will know before you send.** When your message is going to reach him, the
composer says "Cobb will reply" with a "don't ask Cobb" next to it, because
replying "agreed, let's take it" to his last answer is usually addressed to a
person rather than to him.

**Whoever asks is who he answers as.** Cobb reads your workspace under the
permissions of the person who asked, so if you reply to an answer somebody else
requested, the next one runs under yours. Each answer says who asked for it. If
you have not connected an assistant, he says so in the conversation rather than
staying silent.

**Answers take a moment** and arrive on their own. You will see "Cobb is
thinking" while it happens, and if it fails it says so rather than leaving you
wondering whether to ask again.
