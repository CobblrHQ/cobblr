**The labels page uses the width of a wide screen.** The queue and its sheet preview now sit side by side on a wide desktop (preview on the right), and fall back to stacked on a narrower or portrait window. The browse panel's Locations tab lays its areas and containers out in two columns to match the other tabs, instead of one tall single-file list with empty space to the right.
