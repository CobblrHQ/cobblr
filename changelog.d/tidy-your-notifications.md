**Delete a notification, mark one unread, or clear everything you have read.** Each notification on your notifications page now has Mark unread and Delete, for "I read it and I still have to do it" and for the ones you are done with, and Clear read deletes every one you have read, in every workspace, after asking. Read notifications are also removed on their own after 180 days, so the inbox no longer only grows; unread ones are kept until you read them. Browser push is no longer offered as a channel, because it is not built yet and sent nothing; a binding made before now says so, with the way to remove it.
