**Production runs** — tell the farm *"make 250 of these, stop when done."* Pick a pool, a plate file, and a target quantity (with parts-per-plate); Cobblr queues exactly the plates needed, drips them onto free printers, and replaces failures automatically. A plate only counts when you clear the bed with a **good** verdict — scrapped plates are reprinted without you thinking about it. The run closes itself at target and can be paused, resumed, or cancelled mid-flight from the Digital Fabrication floor.
