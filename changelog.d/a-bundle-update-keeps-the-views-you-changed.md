An update to a bundle no longer undoes the changes you made to its views. Change the columns, sort or filter of a view a bundle brought, or rename it, and the update keeps what you changed and still brings in what the bundle changed that you did not touch. The view stays the same view, so a dashboard pin or an app page that shows it keeps showing it. When you and the update both changed the same setting, you are asked, with your version beside the update's. A view you changed has a button under Edit to put it back as the bundle made it, and the assistant can do the same.

## docs

**Your changes to a bundle's views stay.** Change the columns, sort or filter of a view a bundle brought, or rename it, and an update keeps what you changed and still brings in what the bundle changed on that view that you did not touch. The view stays the same view, so a dashboard pin or an app page that shows it keeps showing it. Only a setting you and the update both changed is asked about, with your settings beside the update's. A view the update no longer has goes with it, unless you had changed it, in which case it stays as your own. **Put back as the bundle made it**, under **Edit** on a view you have changed, goes back to the bundle's version.
