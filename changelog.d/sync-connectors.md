You can now **keep a live link to another system**. Add a *sync connection* (the first is **companion app**), and its records mirror straight into your workspace: connect it once, and e.g. every room, rack, and bin you add in companion app shows up as a Cobblr location, hierarchy and all. It's a real connector, not a one-off: changes arrive **instantly** via webhook, with a periodic reconcile that backstops anything missed and removes things deleted at the source. Credentials are encrypted at rest, and the same machinery is built to host more connectors over time.
