The "receipts to confirm as purchase orders" panel is gone. A receipt's purchase order is created the moment the receipt is read, so there was nothing left for that button to confirm, and what it did instead was file every line without a look at what it was or where it goes, and add each line to the order a second time. Filing a line from the inbox now claims the order line the receipt already has; sending it back releases it; the order always says what the receipt said. Delete a receipt you never filed from and its order goes too, so a better picture can make a fresh one. Orders the old panel doubled are repaired on the next start.
