Folding a scan session away is now the chevron's job alone. Tapping its name used to do it too, which meant reading the title and hiding it were the same gesture. The checkbox that selects the whole session also sits further left, clear of the chevron, so the control that acts on the section is not flush against the one that acts on the row.
