**A forwarded receipt now keeps the whole purchase, not just the item.** Alongside the line items, Cobblr reads who sold it, what it actually cost you after any discount, and when it is due to arrive. Marketplace orders keep the shop and the individual seller as separate facts, because you shop at one again and probably never deal with the other twice.
