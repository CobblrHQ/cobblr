After a scan session, the inbox now spots a shared theme and offers to act on it in one tap: tag every item with a derived label (scan a pile of aviation gear → "tag them all 'Aviation'") and suggest a category for the non-book items ("Aviation Accessories"), all derived on the fly. Nothing pre-baked. The tag rides along and attaches to each item's entity when you confirm it, so books land on the Bookshelf and accessories in inventory, but all carry the same tag.
