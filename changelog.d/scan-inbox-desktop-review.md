On a desk the scan inbox's cards are as tall as what they have to say: the tools sit in one row beside the title instead of a rail down the side, and a row that still needs you asks its one question with the answers a tap can give, instead of a paragraph and a percentage.

## docs

**A desktop card shows the decision, not a rail.** The camera, ⋯, **Re-run AI**, discard and the expand chevron sit in one row at the card's top right, so a ready row is the height of its picture or its two lines, and a picture is shown whole at up to 128px tall on the closed card.

**A row that needs a look asks one question.** Where the amber line used to read the whole AI note, a flagged row now asks what is actually open: **Check weight: 1 - Fingering** (the name says "4 Ply") **or 4 - Aran** (the AI's read), and tapping an answer sets that field on the row, the same as typing it in the form; when it was the last open question the row is marked looked-at in the same tap and offers **Add**. A field the pieces of one photo disagreed on reads **Fibre is not set** with **Type it** opening the form. A crop that failed, a throttled lookup or a low score asks you to check the name, with the reason as the tooltip. The full note is still on the open card and in **Source data**. The phone card asks the same question.

**An offer says what it does.** Two look-alike pending rows offer **Combine rows**: one row stays here, the other goes to Recently deleted, and nothing already filed changes. A session offers **Move into the previous session** (only which session the rows sit in changes; nothing is combined). The series and "these look related" offers add a tag to each row and combine nothing. The one offer that changes a record you already have, on an open card's match, names that record and fills only its empty fields.

**A long listing title reads as its identity.** On the closed card a marketing title shows its lead (the brand and type, the model or capacity that tells it from its sibling, a colour when that is the difference); the full title is the tooltip and is on the open card, and the stored name is never changed.

**A colour is shown as itself.** A colour field on the closed card is a swatch beside the field's word; a colour code stays in the tooltip, and a colour with a name keeps its name.
