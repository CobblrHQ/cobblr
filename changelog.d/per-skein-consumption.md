Consumables can now be tracked one unit at a time. On a yarn's page, under Consumption, turn on "Track each skein separately" to get a plain count by state ("3 skeins, 2 new, 1 open") with the metres left in the skein you are actually pulling from shown quietly, never a blended total across skeins. It is opt-in per item and reversible, so existing consumables keep their single gauge until you ask for the per-unit view.
