**The label is the door.** A join link can now be offered from the workspace's item labels: someone who scans a label without an account sees "Join the club to take one", and joining takes them back to the thing they scanned. It is off unless you tick **Item labels offer it too**. People without a password can ask the join page to email them a link that joins them and opens the item. "Sign in to join" on an invite now keeps the invite instead of losing it.

## docs

**Item labels can offer the join link.** When you make a join link in Members, tick **Item labels
offer it too**. Anyone who scans one of this workspace's item labels without an account then sees
**Join <workspace> to take one**, and joining takes them back to the item. Leave it off if labels
leave the room with the things they are on. On the join page, **Email me a link to join** sends a
link that makes the account, joins and opens the item, for someone who would rather not pick a
password. A link asked for before you revoked the join link no longer joins.
