A shared thing now has one conversation across both workspaces: what the person you share with says about their copy is in your thing's Discussion, and your replies are on their copy, each marked as coming through the share.

## docs

**Talking about a shared thing.** Comments travel with the share. What the person you share with writes in their copy's Discussion appears in yours, named as them and marked **through the share**; your replies appear on their copy the same way. Each comment crosses once.
