Bookshelf now keeps your books on their own shelf instead of mixing them into Inventory, so they read as a catalog with no quantities or warranty fields, and an existing bookshelf moves itself across on upgrade with your books and their authors intact. There is also a new Lending Library bundle for the shelf you lend from: it counts copies and lets you reserve one against a borrower until it comes back.
