The location picker's areas now wrap into a compact chip grid (like the camera's drawer) instead of one area per line, so a house full of rooms is a few rows, not a long scroll. An area holding bins is a split chip: tap the name to pick the area, tap its count/chevron to open that area's containers in a panel below (one open at a time, shelf's bins indented under the shelf). Loose containers sit in their own section below, open by default.
