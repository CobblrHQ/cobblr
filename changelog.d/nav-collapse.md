Your navigation is now just the things you keep. Files, Locations, Tags, Views, the Scan Inbox, Discussion, Maintenance and the Calendar fold under one "more" row in the sidebar, the top bar and the phone menu, and "Dashboard" is called Home. A new workspace no longer opens on seven rows that lead to empty pages, and the first screen asks one thing: type what you have, or scan it. Everything else is one tap further, behind "More ways to start".
