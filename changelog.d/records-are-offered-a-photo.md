A record without a photo, however you made it and including the ones you already have, is now offered one: Cobblr looks it up on the web in the background and shows a Suggested photo you can use, swap for another or turn down.

## docs

**A record made without a photo is offered one.** Make a record with no photo, by any route: the New form, a quick add in a list, an import, or asking Cobb. Cobblr looks for a picture of it in the background, the same way the scan inbox does, from its name and maker and with no AI. The picture shows on the record's card with a dashed edge and a **Suggested** label. On the record itself it reads **Suggested photo**, with three buttons:
- **Use it** puts it on the record.
- **Show others** opens the web picker on the same search.
- **No thanks** puts the offer away. It stays away until you rename the record.

Records you already had without a photo are looked up too, a few a minute in the background. Each record is searched once. While it is, its card reads **Looking for a picture…**; if nothing turns up, the card reads **No picture found**, and the record itself shows **No picture found · Search**, where **Search** looks once more.

The picture is never put on the record until you say so, and a photo you set yourself is never replaced. An import of hundreds of records is looked up a few at a time rather than all at once. To have the top match put on new records automatically, turn on **Put the photo Cobblr finds on new records** in the Scan photos section of the AI page.
