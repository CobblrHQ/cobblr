Upload a registration renewal to update the right car, keep its original in your Vault and undo the change in one step.

## docs

When a registration photo leaves a value blank, check the document and fill
it in on the renewal card. You can choose the car yourself if the reader
cannot identify it safely. Nothing is renewed or filed until you press
Confirm. Check who can open the Vault folder before you confirm.
