Put-away no longer routes chilled or frozen food to a spot that is neither. It
used to follow the evidence alone, so a few yoghurts sitting in a cupboard were
enough to send the next one there, and the storage check then complained about
a placement the system had just made. Where there is nowhere suitable it says
nothing rather than naming somewhere warm.
