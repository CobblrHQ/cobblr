**Put away a fresh pile even without AI: the plan now proposes starter bins by category.** When you plan a backlog on a workspace that has no bins yet (and AI is not connected), the planner used to leave every item marked "Needs a destination" with nowhere to send it. It now reads the category each item already carries and rolls them into a handful of common shelves (Groceries, Beverages, Liquor & Spirits, Books & Media, 3D Printing & Filament, Personal Care, Electronics & Cables, Household & Home, and so on), then proposes a new bin for each. A pile of a hundred scattered items collapses into a short list of bins you can accept, rename, or send elsewhere. Items with no category still cluster by name or wait for you to pick a spot. Detail and edit modals also use a bit more of the screen height.

## docs

- **Category starter bins in the put-away plan.** On a workspace with no matching bin, the planner groups items by their category into common shelves and proposes a new bin for each, so a fresh pile has real destinations to accept instead of a wall of "Needs a destination". Every proposed bin is renamable and re-routable before you accept it. Items with no category cluster by name or stay unassigned for you to place by hand.
