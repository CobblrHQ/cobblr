Every record's detail page now has a small Cobb button beside its other actions. Press it and that record is what Cobb is talking about: the panel opens, the chip says its name, and your question carries the record itself rather than a name he has to go and find. Press it again to drop it. It is always visible rather than appearing on hover, so it works the same on a phone as on a desktop, and it lights up in the same amber as the highlight on the page.

## docs
On a list, ticking rows is how you say "these". On a record's own page there is nothing to tick, so its actions now include a small Cobb button: press it and that record is in context, the chat opens ready for your question, and the chip shows its name. Press it again to drop it. The button lights amber while that record is the one in context, which is the same amber used to highlight text you selected, so both read as the same idea. It appears on every detail page that shows record actions, including ones added later.
