Tracked licences, subscriptions and warranties with cancellation reminders, paid terms, vendor totals and receipt drafts.

## docs

Install Licences & subscriptions from Bundles to track renewal dates, costs, seats and vendors. Choose a starter template or review a draft from a receipt before saving. Licence keys stay hidden; payment descriptions accept last four digits only. Auto-renewing records show a cancellation deadline and remind you one day before it. Renewed records retain each paid term, and Spend by vendor this year totals those payments separately by currency. Link a warranty to a thing to see its coverage date there.

See [the full guide](modules/licences-subscriptions.md) for setup, receipt review, saved views and coverage links.
