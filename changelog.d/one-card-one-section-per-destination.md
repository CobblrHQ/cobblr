When Cobb works out a plan with several destinations from your page ("get the grocery and spices out of inventory and into their sections"), the card now has one section per destination, each with its own Do this button, and a Do all button at the bottom. A part you run ticks off in place with what it did and its own Undo; a part that fails says why and keeps its button; a card you leave half done is still half done after a reload.

## docs

Documented directly in the user guide's Ask Cobb section in the same change: one card with a section per destination, Do this per section, Do all for the rest, the done morph with its own Undo, and the half-done card surviving a reload.
