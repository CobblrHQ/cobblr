Things that keep at room temperature now get a suggested spot too. A tin goes
where the rest of your groceries are kept, and in a brand new workspace it goes
to the Pantry, or the Kitchen if that is what you have. The same rule works for
anything, not just food: it asks where the rest of that kind lives, so books and
spices and parts each learn their own home. It never suggests the fridge as a
general purpose spot, and it never files something into a pantry unless the item
is actually shelf stable.
