A receipt that could not be read no longer turns up in the inbox as an unnamed item asking for a name. It now says the receipt was not read, with Read again as the first button, and Treat it as one item if you meant it as a single thing. This holds on the phone list, in item review and on the desktop card, whether the receipt was uploaded alone or together with photos. It is no longer offered a table, an Add or Review button or a quantity either, since there is nothing to file until it is read, and the dashboard counts it as a receipt that could not be read rather than as a scanned item without a home.
