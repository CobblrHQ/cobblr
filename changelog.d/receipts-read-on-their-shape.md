A receipt dropped into the scan inbox becomes a receipt session before any AI is asked: the inbox reads the text off the picture itself and, when it has the shape of a receipt (lines ending in prices and a total), sends it straight to the reader. The reader then reads a clear receipt image with no AI at all when its lines add up, and asks the AI only when they do not. When a picture does go to the AI, the AI is asked outright whether it is a receipt, so one with a big product on it is no longer filed as that product. A photo the AI could not name now says the one reason why, with the fix beside it, instead of listing every possible reason.
