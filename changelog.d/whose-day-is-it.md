Dates are your dates now, not the server's. West of Greenwich the app used to
roll over to tomorrow at teatime, so a reminder could say "expires today" the
evening before, something restocked at 6pm was dated tomorrow and counted its
shelf life from a day it had not reached, and a form pre-filled the wrong day.
A reminder meant for a whole workspace now waits until the day has started for
everybody in it, and anything you date by acting takes the day it is where you
are, from the timezone in your communication preferences.
