**What you tick is what Cobb is talking about.** Ticking rows on a list, the Scan Inbox and saved views included, now puts them in Cobb's context straight away, shown as the "About:" chip above his message box, so "file these in Mugs" means exactly the ticked ones and Cobb never asks which. Before, you had to press Ask Cobb in the selection bar first. The × on the chip drops them, and unticking them does too.
