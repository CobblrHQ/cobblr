## docs

**A barcode someone filed is filed the same way next time, with no AI.** When you file something that has a barcode into a table that came with a bundle (your yarn, your filament), the shared barcode network also learns which kind of table it went into and what kind of thing it is: its material, size or colour. It never learns a lot number, a serial number, a price, where you bought it, where you keep it, a note or anything you wrote yourself. The next person who scans that barcode and keeps the same kind of table gets it routed there with those details filled, without the AI being asked, and the card says whether one person or several filed it that way.
