Plan a layout for a room before anything moves: drag racks and things into a draft, keep several options and compare them, print one to scale, and apply it as one step you can undo.

## docs

**Plan a layout.** On a room's floor plan, **Plan a layout** opens a draft of the room ("Option A"). Drag racks around the floor, and drag things onto any shelf of any rack; things from elsewhere wait under **Bring things in**, the ones with a size first. Each shelf shows whether what is on it fits, and the list under the drawing names every shelf that does not. **Plan a rack you do not have yet** adds a rack of the size you give, with its shelves, drawn dashed; it becomes a real place only when you apply. The banner says **Draft: nothing has moved yet** for as long as that is true. **New option** and **Copy** make more options, **Compare with…** shows two side by side, and **Print to scale** opens the option on paper with a scale bar and every size written in (print it or save it as a PDF). **Apply** asks first ("This will move 14 things, make 1 planned rack, as one step you can undo"), then does it all at once, each move recorded on the thing; **Undo** on its message, or **Undo it** on the applied option, puts everything back, except a thing somebody has moved again since. Cobb can work on the same options: ask him to "lay out the 3D printers on Rack 1 and Rack 2, biggest on the bottom shelf" and he drafts it for you to look at and apply.
