A shared pattern brings the needs you typed for it, and a change to them reaches the friend's copy.

## docs

**What a shared thing brings with it.** A shared record arrives as what it is (a Pattern on their Patterns page, with its own fields), with its attached files (a pattern's PDF), and with what you wrote about it in lists of its own: a pattern's needs, as you typed them. Change or remove one and their copy follows. What you did with it stays yours: the yarn from your stash you matched it to, your shopping list, your runs and stock.
