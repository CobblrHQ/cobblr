The morning something expires, the reminder now arrives. The "expires today"
line was being prepared and then dropped whenever nothing new was coming up
in the same hour, which is most mornings, so since early September it only
reached you when a new heads-up happened to ride along. A reminder that could
not be delivered is also no longer marked as sent; it comes back on the next
hourly check instead of being lost.
