**Use / restock on the What's on hand board updates the card at once.** Using one or restocking one saved the change but the card kept its old count and "in stock" until the page was reloaded, so it was easy to tap twice. Every action now refreshes everything on screen that shows that record the moment it completes, on the board, the table, the dashboard and the record's page, and Undo does the same.
