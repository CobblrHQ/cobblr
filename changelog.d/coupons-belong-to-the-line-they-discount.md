A coupon printed under an item on a receipt now comes off that item instead of being dropped. The line keeps the price it was rung up at and records what came off, so two coupons on a 2-for-0.49 pair of croissants correctly leave them costing nothing, and the receipt's items add up to what you actually paid. A coupon never becomes an item of its own, so there is no row to triage for something nobody owns.
