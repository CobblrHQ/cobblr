The scanner and the inbox now pick a location by tapping a chip, not opening a dropdown. When you set the scan area on the camera, or file a selection from the inbox, your locations show as a grid of tappable chips under a Rooms (and Bins) heading, one tap to pick. It is the same mobile pattern across both surfaces, so they stop feeling like two different apps. New location is one tap away at the bottom.
