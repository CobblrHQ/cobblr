The scanner and the inbox now pick a location by tapping a chip, not opening a dropdown. When you set the scan area on the camera, or file a selection from the inbox, your locations show as a grid of tappable chips under a Rooms (and Bins) heading, one tap to pick. It is the same mobile pattern across both surfaces, so they stop feeling like two different apps. New location is one tap away at the bottom.

## docs

**Setting a location is a tap, not a dropdown.** On the camera scanner's **Assign** sheet and the inbox's bulk **Location** action, your locations render as **tappable chips** grouped under **Rooms** (areas) and, in the inbox, **Bins** (containers). Tap a chip to pick it; tap it again to clear. **+ New location…** at the bottom adds one without leaving the flow. The camera's Assign shows rooms only (you're stamping which room you're standing in); the inbox shows both, since you file into a specific bin or room.
