You can now choose a picture already on your device when adding one to a scan item, instead of only taking a fresh photo. A picture you choose is attached to the item without changing its name or its main photo, because a file off your camera roll is often a listing screenshot or a spec sheet rather than a photo of the thing.

## docs

Anywhere the photo sheet opens you now get a Choose button beside the shutter, so a picture already on your phone (a marketplace screenshot with the dimensions on it, a spec sheet, a shot from yesterday) can be added without retaking it. Before, that option only appeared on a device with no camera at all.

A picture you take and a picture you choose are treated differently, on purpose. A photo you take now is a photo of the thing in front of you, so it can become the item's main picture and can help identify it. A picture you choose is simply attached: it will not rename the item, will not raise a "this looks wrong" prompt, and will not become the main picture on its own. If a chosen picture really is the photo you want as the item's face, make it primary from the photo strip; if you want it read for identification, use the re-identify action on it.
