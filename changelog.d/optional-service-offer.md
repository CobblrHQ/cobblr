Adding Sets offered BrickLink as an optional service, with existing BrickLink data kept out of the sidebar.

## docs

When adding Sets, choose whether to turn on BrickLink with "Sets works with BrickLink. Turn it on?". It starts off. Services live on the Connections page: use Add a service to turn one on, or Open to use one already enabled. An install names required additions before you confirm and offers Undo afterwards. Undo keeps your records and collections.
