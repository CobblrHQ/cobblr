**Ask Cobb keeps your conversation across a page refresh.** The chat used to reset to empty whenever you reloaded the page. Now each workspace's conversation is remembered on your device, so a refresh (or reopening the panel) picks up where you left off. Only the plain back-and-forth text is kept, not live buttons like a pending Confirm card, and a new **trash icon** in the panel header clears the conversation whenever you want. History is per workspace and stays on your device (it is not uploaded).
