The Cobb button that puts one record in context now sits on list rows too, not only on a record's own page. Every area and container on Locations has one.

## docs
The small Cobb button beside a record's other actions puts THAT record into the chat's context and opens the panel ready for your question, and it now sits wherever the record does: on its own page, and on each row of a list that shows one. On Locations that means every area and every container, so you can ask about one rack without ticking it first or naming it in your message. Press it again to drop the context; it stays lit amber while it is the one in play.
