The per-device theme override (Profile → Appearance → **This device only**) gains a **Match OS** option: make just this device follow its own operating-system light/dark setting — live, so it flips with your system day/night schedule — even when your **account default** is a fixed Light or Dark. Previously a device could only use the account default or hard-lock to Light/Dark; now "follow this screen's OS" is a first-class choice, independent of and without changing your global preference. The Appearance card was also tightened to about half its previous height.
