In the inbox's "receipts to confirm as purchase orders" list, each receipt can now be looked at before you act on it: hover shows the picture, and clicking the name opens the original in the same viewer the session row uses. A receipt with no picture, such as an emailed or CSV one, still opens; it just has nothing to show on hover.
