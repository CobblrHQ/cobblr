A scan item whose receipt listed other things now says so on its card, and opens to show what they were.

## docs

When you attach a receipt to an item, everything else on that receipt is recorded on the purchase too. The item's card now tells you: a quiet line reading "This receipt lists 4 other items", which opens to list them with their quantities and prices.

They are recorded, not owned. Nothing on that list is one of your items unless you say so, because plenty of what is on a receipt gets used up rather than kept. For now the list is there to look at; turning one of those lines into an item of its own is not wired yet.
