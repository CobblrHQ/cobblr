**A build adds what it makes to stock.** A build made on the builds page never added its product to stock, because nothing on the page could say which part it makes. A new build now asks what it makes, starting from its own name, and the build sheet says so above the Build button. A build that makes nothing says that before you press Build. On a phone, the Build button now fits on the card.
