The sorting planner now matches items to bins by their category, not just by name, so parts land in the right bin at a finer grain (a "1x1 plates" bin apart from a "wedges" bin). It also plans a specific pile of items on request and handles large batches without an AI ceiling, so a whole disassembled set can be organized at once.
