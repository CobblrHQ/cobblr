## docs

### Adding another one

Once you have told Cobblr how long something keeps, adding more of it needs no typing.

Set **Good for (days)** on the item. From then on, recording that another one arrived stamps today's date on it and works out its own use-by. Add two on the same day and they are recorded as one delivery of two, which is how they arrived.

Two things follow that are easy to miss and are the whole point:

- **A new one never hides an old one.** Adding fresh stock cannot push an existing use-by out of sight. The date you are shown is always the soonest one you still have.
- **Using one always takes the oldest.** So once you have eaten the urgent one, the warning moves to the next by itself. You never have to correct a date.

If you have not said how long something keeps, nothing is dated for you. A guess would be worse than a blank.
