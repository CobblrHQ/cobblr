## docs

- **Organize a whole pile at once (Guided Organize).** Scanned a bench sweep or an unboxing and everything's identified but nowhere? Select 2+ items and hit **Organize…** in the bulk bar. Cobblr looks at the pile *and* at what already lives where, and hands back a **put-away plan**: the items grouped by what they are, each group with a destination and the reason: an **existing bin with evidence** ("3 similar already there: Resistor 1k, Resistor 4.7k…"), a **proposed new bin** (named, parented sensibly, created only if you accept), or **needs a destination** when there's no defensible answer (the planner never guesses). Accept groups one at a time or all at once, accepted items get that location and commit through the normal confirm flow; **Change…** on any group re-points it with the location picker (which is also how a "needs a destination" group gets one). Items you already filed by hand are shown untouched, and unidentified items are excluded until you identify them. Works without AI too, a similarity plan grouped by where siblings live, labeled as such. Accepting is the bookkeeping; **Start put-away walk →** is the physical half: it walks the accepted groups bin by bin, each bin becomes your **active filing bin** while you stand at it, and you check items off by **scanning them with a hardware scanner** (a scan that isn't in the current group tells you so) or just tapping. Progress saves as you go, so if you wander off mid-walk the scan page offers **▶ Resume put-away walk** with the count still left to place.
