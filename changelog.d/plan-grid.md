## docs

**A grid to place things by.** The **grid** button above a plan turns on a grid for that plan, and the plan remembers it. Its cells follow the plan's own units: 1 in, 2 in, 6 in or 1 ft on a plan measured in inches or feet, 1 cm, 5 cm, 10 cm or 1 m on a metric one, picked so a cell is never too small to see at the size the plan is shown (a drawer reads in 1 in cells on a desk and 2 in cells on a phone). The button says which. While the grid is on, a box you drag snaps to it and a thing you put on the plan lands in the middle of the cell you dropped it in. To fix the size, open **Edit layout** and pick it under **grid**: one of the plan's steps, **Gridfinity 42 mm** for a drawer of Gridfinity bins, or any size typed beside it. A grid is a guide, not a divider: it does not say whether a thing fits. With the grid off, which is how every plan starts, pinned things go exactly where you put them.
