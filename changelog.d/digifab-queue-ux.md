The Digital Fabrication queue is easier to work: click any job to open a detail view with its full error, timeline and the build/material it draws; retry a failed or cancelled job in one click; filter the queue by Active / Failed / Done; edit a connection (rename, move host, rotate its key, pause it) instead of delete-and-recreate; and the page finally fits a phone screen. A fresh workspace now sees a clear "Enable Digital Fabrication" prompt instead of a dead page.
