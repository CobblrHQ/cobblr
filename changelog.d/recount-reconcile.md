Finish a recount with a preview of what changes, choose what happens to things not seen, and undo the whole recount in one press

## docs

When a recount is done, press **Finish…** to see what will change before anything does: seen things are confirmed, new things are added like the ones they read as, and each thing not seen can be marked gone, kept, or left to check later. Turn on **The scan is the truth** to make the scan the record: things not seen default to gone, and places and amounts come from the scans. **Apply** writes it all as one line in the activity, and **Undo this recount** puts everything back. Nothing is ever deleted.
