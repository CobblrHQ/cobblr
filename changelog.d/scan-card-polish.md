**Bigger scan photos, and nothing cut off on mobile.** Inbox cards now show the item photo at the full height of the card — a book cover or product shot reads at a glance instead of squinting at a thumbnail — with a consistent width so every card's text still lines up. And the expanded item detail no longer runs off the right edge of a phone screen: the photo/source/hint columns now wrap to fit any width.
