A suggested photo now has to show the thing: a printer is no longer offered a banner that only shares its maker's name, and when nothing clearly fits there is no suggestion. On the record, the suggestion fills the photo box with Use this photo and Not this one, stays put on every open, and a picture you pick from the web is kept even when its site refuses the full-size copy, or you are told why not.
