A service visit can say where it is. Pick a place you used before or type the address, and the visit's calendar event carries it, with directions on your phone.

## docs

**Where a visit happens.** When you add a maintenance entry, the **Address**
box takes where the visit is: the motor vehicle office for a registration
renewal, the garage for the car's service. Type the start of a place you used
before and it is offered, with the visit or record it was used on; or type
the address, or enter its street, city and postal code. The visit shows the
address with **Directions**, and its calendar event, in the app and in your
subscribed phone calendar, says where it is. A place a visit went to is
offered on any record's address field afterwards. Cobb can add the address
when it logs or schedules a visit for you ("the registration renewal is at
the Lodi MVC on the 14th").
