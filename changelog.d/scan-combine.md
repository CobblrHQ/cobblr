The scan inbox now offers to **combine items that look like the same product** — when you scan several of one thing but a pack carries a different barcode, a banner appears ("N items look like the same product — combine into one?"). One tap merges them into a single line with the summed quantity, keeping each distinct barcode in the item's data. Always opt-in (a different barcode can be a different size), and dismissable to keep them separate.
