Uninstalling a bundle now puts back what it set up for you: the modules it turned on because it needed them are turned off again, and the empty places it made are removed. Anything you have used since stays, and the uninstall says so ("Kept Pantry: 12 things live there"). A module you turned on yourself and a place that was already there are never touched.
