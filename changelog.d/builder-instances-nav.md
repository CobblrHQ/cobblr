The AI builder got real about two things it used to wrongly refuse: **"track Books, call it Bookshelf"** now creates a proper Bookshelf: its own nav entry and page (a named instance), not book fields bolted onto Assets, and **"put these under a Machines parent"** now works, because bundles can declare **nav headings** (a parent nav entry with module/instance members, including ones that already exist). The Build page also spots when a ready-made featured bundle already covers your ask and offers to install that instead of generating a worse one.
