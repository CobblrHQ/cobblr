Added copy and move between your workspaces, with a field preview and undo.

## docs

Open an item's actions and choose **Send to another workspace**, or select items
in a list and choose **Send N to…**. Choose another workspace where you can add
records, Copy or Move, and a collection. Cobblr suggests the matching collection.
An owner or admin can create a matching collection or add missing fields there.

**Preview send** shows where every field goes and which fields need a home.
Choose to drop a missing field or add it in the target. Photos, attachments and
documents are copied. Locations and stock placement stay in the source workspace;
choose a place in the target or choose to arrive unplaced. Links are kept only
when their linked records already arrived there. Secrets stay out by default.

Confirm Copy to keep the original, or Move to take it out of the source after
it arrives. Complete items file straight into the chosen collection. Items that
need a collection, a field or a placement wait in the target's scan inbox and say
what they need. Your own sends have no Take or Pass question. Add missing fields
or explicitly drop them before filing an incomplete item.

Use **Undo send** in the result or **Sharing → Handed over** to remove the arrival
and restore a moved source. If the arrival changed afterwards, Cobblr refuses to
overwrite those edits. An arrival you already removed does not block restoring
its source. Sending the same item again asks whether to update the
earlier copy or make another. Updating can be undone to the earlier values.
