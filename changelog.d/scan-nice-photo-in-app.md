Taking a "Nice photo" in the scanner no longer launches the phone's native camera on top of the one already running. It now opens an **in-app capture drawer** off the live viewfinder: frame it, hit the shutter, then **Retake** or **Use this**: and the catalog photo swaps in place with a clear "updated" confirmation instead of a fire-and-forget toast. (Desktop, with no live camera, still uses the file picker.)
