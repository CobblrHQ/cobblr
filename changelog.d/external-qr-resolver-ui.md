Read QR labels printed by another app. Configuration → External QR rules lets you teach the scanner a foreign label format (a companion app URL, a bare Homebox number, anything) and map it to the matching item here, so labels you already printed keep working without a reprint. Scan one and it opens the item exactly like a native Cobblr label; paste a sample into the built-in tester to check a rule before you trust it. Fully opt-in: with no rules, the scanner behaves exactly as before.
