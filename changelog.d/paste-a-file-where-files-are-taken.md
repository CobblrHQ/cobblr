Pasting a file now works wherever the upload button takes files. A PDF copied in your file manager and pasted onto the scan inbox, or into its box at the top, is read as a receipt instead of putting its file name into the search. On a record, a pasted picture goes to its photos and a pasted PDF to its Files, with no question asked.
