Removing a bundle now asks the same question wherever you start: keep its records (the default, with Undo afterwards) or delete them too. Before, Remove on the Bundles page kept them and Remove in the bundle's sheet deleted them. A failed bundle install or feature save now always says so, a bundle keeps its id when you change its features, and tapping a feature on a collection opens the bundle with that feature ticked.
