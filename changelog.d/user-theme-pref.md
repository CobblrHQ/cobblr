**Dark mode follows *you*, everywhere.** Your light/dark choice is now saved to your account, so it carries across all your workspaces and every device you sign in on — flip it once, it sticks. It's purely how *you* view Cobblr: it never changes another member's view, and a workspace that wears its own branded theme still shows that theme to everyone (your preference just governs the normal, unskinned workspaces). Toggle it from the same places as before (the sidebar's theme icon, or Dark mode in your account menu). A device you haven't set still follows its own OS setting until you choose.
