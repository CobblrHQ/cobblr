Sync connections (Ravelry and friends) get a history. A connection you're done with — imported once and don't want it polling, say — can now be **Archived** instead of removed: it drops out of the Live-sync list into an **Archived** section below it and stops syncing, but nothing is lost. One click **Re-activate** puts it back where it left off (its id-map and settings survive). Archive ≠ remove — *Remove* still deletes for good; *Archive* just tucks it away for later.
