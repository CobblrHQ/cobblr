A scan of a thing you already have can be marked broken: the thing gets a repair entry in its service log instead of a second one being filed.

## docs

**Broken? Say so from the scan.** Scan the drill you already have and tap **Broken** on its card or the camera's sheet, and say what is wrong if you like. The drill gets a "Needs repair" entry in its service log, due now, and no second drill is filed. **Undo** takes the entry back off and returns the scan to your inbox. The mark is offered only when the scan matched one thing by its barcode or serial number, never on a guess by name or when it could be one of several things. The assistant can do the same ("the drill I just scanned is broken").
