**Supplies: a pattern knows its stitch markers, safety eyes and stuffing too.** The Yarn bundle gains a **Supplies** table (on in Cobblr for Yarn; call it **Notions** if that is your word), and a pattern's notions are read from its materials list beside its yarn and hooks: stitch markers, holders, a yarn or cable needle, safety eyes and their size, stuffing, buttons. Each is checked against your supplies, 6 mm eyes against 6 mm eyes, and what you lack goes on your shopping list in one tap.

## docs

**Supplies.** Turn on the Yarn bundle's **Supplies** table (Cobblr for Yarn has it on) and
keep your stitch markers, yarn needles, safety eyes, stuffing and buttons there, each
with its **Type** and, for eyes and buttons, its **Size**. If you call them notions,
pick **Notions** as the table's word in its Settings; searching for either word finds it.
A pattern's notions appear under **What this pattern needs** with *You have it* or
*No stuffing on hand* and **Add to shopping list**.
