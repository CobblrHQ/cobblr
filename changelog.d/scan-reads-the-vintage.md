**The scanner reads the year a bottle was made.** Scan a bottle and the vintage on its label fills the field you marked as *The year it was made*, a new field meaning. It reads the vintage and not the year the house was founded, and because one barcode covers every vintage, the year is never shared as a fact about the barcode.

## docs

Give a field the meaning **The year it was made** (a wine's vintage, a coin's year) and the scanner fills it from the label: the same look at the photo that reads a lot number reads the year. It picks the vintage, not a founding year or a best-before date, and shows it on the scan line, for example "Château Filhot · 2008". A year you typed yourself always stays.

One barcode covers every vintage of a wine, so the year belongs to your bottle only. It is never sent to Cobblr's barcode network, even if you typed it into the name.
