The "things to use up" reminder puts each item's Used up and Threw out
buttons beside the item, on Discord and in the app, instead of listing the
items and then stacking every button underneath. Pressing one settles that
line only; the others stay, so a batch can be dealt with from one message.
A very long list shows twelve with their buttons and says how many more are
waiting in the app.
