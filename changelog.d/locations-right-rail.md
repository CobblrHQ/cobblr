Every row's buttons line up in one column. A nested location was padded on both sides, so each level of nesting pulled its row's controls further from the right edge: across four levels the Cobb heads, pencils and kind labels sat 39px apart and there was no straight line for your eye to follow. Nesting indents on the left only now, which is what shows the hierarchy anyway, so the controls land on a single vertical line at every depth.
