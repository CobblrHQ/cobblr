Ran out on a scan keeps a thing at none on hand and puts it on the shopping list, whether Cobblr knew it or not

## docs

**Ran out.** When you scan something you have just run out of, choose **Ran out** (under Add on the camera, beside More at a scan station, or in ⋯ on an inbox item). A thing you do not have in Cobblr yet is added with none on hand; a thing you already keep goes to none, never a second copy. Either way it goes on your shopping list, and any rule you set for when something runs out fires as it would for a thing you used up. **Undo** takes it back off, list line and all. It is offered only where things are counted.
