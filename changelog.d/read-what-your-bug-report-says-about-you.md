On a self-hosted instance, the feedback form now shows you what the bug report collected about your setup before you send it. Open **What this says about your setup** under the save button and you can read the build version and channel, Node and Postgres, the server platform and the modules that were enabled. It is one paragraph tall and scrolls, so it is there if you want it and out of the way if you do not. Previously the form told you to read the report first while giving you no way to see it.
