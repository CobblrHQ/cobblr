Ask Cobb to group sections in your navigation and he does it. "Put Spices and Tea under Kitchen" creates the heading and moves them under it, as a change you confirm and can undo, instead of telling you which settings screen to open.

## docs
Cobb can rearrange your navigation. "Put Spices and Tea under Kitchen" is a change he proposes and you confirm, like any other: he matches the names to the sections you actually have, creates the heading if it does not exist, and moves them under it. A section belongs to one heading at a time, so this takes it out of any other. If a name matches two sections he asks which you meant rather than moving the wrong one, and if it matches none he says what your navigation does have. "Take Tea back out" puts it at the top level again.
