**The put-away card now names both what needs a home and what's ready to go.** Instead of only "3 scanned items without a home," the dashboard card and scan strip acknowledge the whole picture, e.g. "3 scanned items without a home, and 15 ready to put away", so items you've already assigned (waiting on an "I did it!") are counted too. The card also shows when everything is assigned and only needs putting away.
