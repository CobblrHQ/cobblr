**A pattern knows what it needs, and takes it off your stash when you finish.** Upload or import a pattern PDF and its yarns and hooks are read from its own materials list, each yarn with its brand, colour, weight and length, with no AI needed for the usual layouts. Each yarn is matched against your stash: you have it, you are short by so much, or nothing fits, with **Add to shopping list** for what is missing. **Use it** holds the balls for that yarn, **Change yarn** halfway keeps what you used on the old balls and moves the rest to the new one, and marking the pattern done takes each yarn's length off its own balls, from any screen.

## docs

**What this pattern needs.** On a pattern, the PDF's materials list becomes one
card per yarn and one per hook or needle. The card says whether your stash has
it, how much is short, or what comes closest. **Add to shopping list** lists
what is missing in the pattern's words. **Use it** holds the balls it needs.
**Change yarn** asks how much of the old yarn you used: that stays on its balls
and the rest moves to the new yarn. When you mark the pattern done, each yarn's
remaining length comes off its own balls, open ones first.

When a pattern gives one length for solid colours and another for prints, the
card asks which your colour is instead of guessing.
