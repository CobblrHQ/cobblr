When a sandbox's hour is up it is held for 24 hours instead of deleted on the spot, and the ending page offers three doors: keep it as a trial for a week, two weeks or a month (your pick, and it ends when that time is up), start a real account from scratch, or, once it exists, bring what you built into a real account.

## docs

### When the hour is up

A sandbox lasts an hour. When the hour is up the workspace freezes: nothing more can be added, and the page tells you what happens next. What you made is held for 24 hours so you can decide; do nothing and it is deleted after that.

You can:

- **Keep this trial so I can keep playing.** Give an email and pick a length: a week, two weeks or a month. The workspace stays exactly as it is, as a trial on the same demo site, and ends when that time is up. There is no longer option on purpose: the demo site is for trying things, not for running them for real.
- **Go for real, from scratch.** A blank account on Cobblr, with nothing carried over, kept for as long as you like.
- **Bring what I built** into a real account. This door opens once that path exists; until then the page says so plainly.

The strip at the foot of the page offers the same keep, with the same choice of length, during the last fifteen minutes of the hour.
