With both Spice Rack and Groceries installed, a seasoning now goes to Spice Rack. Spices are the narrower shelf inside the pantry, so a blend like "Garlic Seasoning Blend" lands with the spices even though its name has other grocery words, and asking Cobb to move the spices out of Inventory moves them there. Without Spice Rack, seasonings still go to Groceries. The same rule applies to any two tables that name the same thing: the more specific one wins.
