**A custom field can say what it means, not just what it is called.** Fields you create yourself can now carry a role from a short standard list (identifier, expiry, category, packaging, assignee), the same list bundles use. A field named "Best before" and one named "Use by" are the same idea, and saying so lets Cobblr fill it in for you, match it against a field someone else made, and warn you before you end up with two fields for one thing.
