Added controls to delete lists, revoke signup invites, test notification delivery and uninstall file previews.

## docs

### Workspace controls

In Configuration, Presentation, open Organise beside a list to delete it and
its records. Confirmation is required, and deletion cannot be undone. Default
lists cannot be deleted here. Choose Fold this list into another to keep its
records instead.

Members & invites lists your invites for someone to start their own workspace.
You can revoke an unused link even when creating new signup invites is turned
off. Someone who already joined keeps their account and workspace.

Under Your account, Notifications, Channels, choose Send me a test notification
to check your own delivery settings for the selected workspace. The result
reports which channels delivered it.

The renderers section in Bundles lists every installed file preview, including
ones no longer in the registry. An owner or admin can uninstall a preview after
confirmation. Your files are kept.
