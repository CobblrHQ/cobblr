**Custom fields open in place instead of in a pop-up.** Clicking a field used to throw an overlay over the whole list, so tidying up five fields meant five dismissals and losing your place each time. The field now expands under its own row: the rest of your fields stay on screen, clicking another one swaps straight to it, and the row you were on is still where you left it when you collapse. Everything the pop-up did (choices, where it appears, delete) is unchanged.
