Several things in one photo can now be added by name. On a photo in the scan inbox, **Split by names…** takes one thing per line; the assistant and connected AI tools do the same with a photo and a list. Each thing gets its own row with its own crop, the photo stays as their group shot, and **Undo split** puts it back as one. When the photo shows a different number of things than you named, it says what it sees and adds nothing.

## docs

- **Split a photo by naming what is in it.** On a photo in the inbox, **Split by names…** asks for one thing per line ("Midea dehumidifier", "Frigidaire dehumidifier"). Cobblr finds where each one sits, crops it, and makes one row for each with its crop as its picture; the photo stays as their **group shot** and the rows read as one group (*2 of 2*). **Undo split** on any of them puts the photo back as one. If the photo shows a different number of things than you named, it says what it sees ("I see 3 things in the photo, and 2 were listed") and adds nothing, so you can fix the list. An assistant does the same from a chat: "this photo is two dehumidifiers, the Midea on the left and the Frigidaire on the right", with what it knows about each.
