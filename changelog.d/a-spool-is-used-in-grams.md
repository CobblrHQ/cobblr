A spool's weight is no longer changed by a fixed tap. **Use some** takes the grams you used, so a tap can never empty a spool. Every spool now has **Mark empty** and **Remove this spool**, for one entered twice. A spool with nothing left reads "empty" instead of "sealed". A spool scanned twice is no longer listed twice: the card asks whether there really is another. The same typed amount applies anywhere a count is kept in kilograms, metres or litres.
