Ask Cobb now walks you to the screens he cannot operate for you, with the form already filled in: ask him to invite someone and you land on Members with the invite ready, you press Send.

## docs

**Cobb walks you there when the button has to be yours.** Some things Cobb will never do for you, no matter the mode: inviting or removing members, API tokens, backup and restore, AI provider settings, composing automations, editing or deleting fields. Those decisions belong on their own screens, where you can see everything they touch. What he CAN do now is take you there: say "invite sam@example.com as an editor" and your screen moves to Members with the email and role already filled in, and the Send invite button waiting for you. Say "I want to restore a backup" and you land on Backup & restore. Nothing is ever submitted on your behalf, nothing is prefilled that the page did not agree to accept, and the address bar is cleaned up so a copied link never carries someone's email along. The chat stays docked through the move, so you can keep talking to him on the new screen.
