Local AI endpoints can now ride your edge bridge: the **Ollama** and **OpenAI-compatible** (LM Studio, vLLM, …) providers have a **"How Cobblr reaches it"** choice — direct, or **via my edge bridge** when the endpoint runs on your own machine/LAN. The form shows live bridge status while you pick, so "will my local URL work?" answers itself.
