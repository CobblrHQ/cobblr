**A USB or Bluetooth barcode scanner now opens what the camera opens.** Scanning an action label, or a QR code that links to one of your records, opens it instead of turning the link into an item in your scan inbox. A QR code that links to Cobblr's own site is ignored, as the camera already does, and a sorting session says a link is not an item instead of filing it.
