**A design's materials and hooks pickers search your whole stash.** Both were a drop-down over one page of 200 records with no search, so a workspace with a few hundred grocery rows never saw its yarn in the picker at all, and nothing said so. Type a name and the picker finds it across every table, showing what is free; when the stash is bigger than one page the list says so at the top. A hook a design already needs is listed whatever else the stash holds.
