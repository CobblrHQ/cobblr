Record actions now sit in menus you can arrange, with your own pinned shortcuts and a single Actions sheet on a phone.

## docs

Record pages keep up to four primary actions in the open. Other actions sit
under Share, Service, Label and other menus, with a description of what each
does. On a phone, Actions opens the same groups in a sheet. Share keeps public
status and the names of people who can see the record visible.

Use ⋯ on an action to pin it to your bar or unpin a primary. Pins follow you
across devices. In Configuration → Record actions, an owner or admin can
choose a kind of record, rename or reorder groups, add a group, move or hide
actions and choose primaries. Save offers Undo. Cobb can arrange these menus
too. A newer edit prevents an old Undo from replacing it.
