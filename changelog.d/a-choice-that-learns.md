Every dropdown is now one picker you can type in: it opens with what your records already hold, typing filters it, and a value that is not on the list is simply the value, remembered for next time with an Undo. Brand, vendor and service jobs offer what you used before, and a vehicle or a 3D printer suggests its usual service jobs.

## docs

Click a field that has choices and the list opens without typing anything: the values your records already hold come first, the most recent and most used at the top, then the rest alphabetically. Type to filter. Arrow keys move, Enter takes the highlighted one, Escape backs out. On a phone it opens as a sheet with the search at the top.

What you type is the value. A name that is not on the list carries a small "new" tag, and Enter keeps it. If the field remembers new entries (a tick under its Choices on the Fields page, on by default for a field you make), the name joins the list and a message says so, with an Undo that takes back both the value and the new choice. A value a scan or a receipt read is never added to the list for you.

The field that says where a thing came from offers the seller beside the place, as a second column in the same picker ("eBay · detroitaxle"). Other dropdowns, like Fuel, offer no such box.

The brand of a part, an asset or a machine offers the makers your records already name. A purchase order's vendor offers your vendors, and a new name becomes one. A service schedule's "What needs doing" offers the jobs done before, plus the usual ones for vehicles and 3D printers.
