**"Put them away" opens instantly, and the plan sheet stopped being clunky.** The dashboard card and scan strip now pre-compute the plan in the background the moment they show the count, so clicking reveals a ready plan instead of a spinner (it refreshes automatically when your backlog changes). The sheet itself got wider (the same width the inbox uses), every item row expands with a proper right-side dropdown arrow (the whole row is the click target), a proposed new bin is renamed by clicking its chip and typing right there, and clicking any destination chip opens the location picker directly beneath it instead of a control stranded three levels down.
