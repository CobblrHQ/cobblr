## docs

### Tracking a parcel you have not filed yet

Once a receipt in your scan inbox carries a tracking number, Cobblr follows the
parcel from there. You do not have to file it into a purchase order first.

The receipt's row shows where the parcel is, updated on Cobblr's own schedule:
**Label created**, **In transit**, **Out for delivery**, **Delivered**, or
**Needs attention**. Hover it for the carrier's own wording and the last place
it was scanned.

You get a notification for the two states that change what you do today: out for
delivery, and delivered. The delivered one links straight back to the inbox,
because that is where the receipt is waiting and filing it is the next step. You
are told once per state, not once an hour, so a parcel sitting delivered over a
weekend does not keep nagging.

Filing the receipt hands the parcel over to the order, which carries the same
tracking number and keeps being followed there.
