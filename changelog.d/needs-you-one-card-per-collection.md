## docs

On the dashboard, **Needs you** now shows everything dated in a collection as one card: "Groceries · 22 overdue · 4 coming up". Open it to see what is late, then a **Today** line, then what is coming up.

Each item can be finished right there: **Used up**, **Tossed**, or **+3 days** when you have checked it and it is still fine (its date moves to three days from today). It leaves the card at once, and **Undo** on the message puts it back. To clear a long backlog, **Mark all as tossed** does them all after you confirm.

If the card spots the same thing filed twice (for example "Roma Tomatoes" and "Tomatoes Roma"), it says so, and **Put together** lets you keep one of them.
