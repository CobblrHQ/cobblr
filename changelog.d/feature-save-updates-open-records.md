**Turning a feature on updates the records you already have open.** After enabling a bundle feature such as labels, the new entry appeared in the navigation but an open item still lacked its new action (Print label) until the page was reloaded. Every change to the workspace's setup, a feature saved, a module turned on or off, a bundle installed or removed, a category created, a field edited, now refreshes everything on screen at once.
