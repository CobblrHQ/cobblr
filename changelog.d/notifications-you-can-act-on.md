Notifications you can answer where you read them, and that always take you somewhere.

## docs

A notification that offers you a choice now shows that choice as buttons in
Cobblr itself, not only in Discord. A workspace invite has **Accept** and
**Decline** on the row, and a service that is due has **Mark done**. Pressing
one runs it right there and the notification tells you what it did. The buttons
appear in both inboxes, the bell and the full notifications page, and they
follow your role, so a read-only guest sees the news without the button.

Every notification also has somewhere to go. One about a single record opens
that record, instance and all, so a jar expiring in your Groceries table opens
the jar rather than a list you have to search. When a message covers several
things at once it takes you to where they all are: several dated items open the
calendar on that day, several unblocked tasks open the projects list, a service
due opens the maintenance page.

Inviting somebody now tells you the truth about email, too. If your instance has
no mail sender configured, the toast says so and reminds you the link is already
on your clipboard, instead of claiming an invite was sent that never left.
