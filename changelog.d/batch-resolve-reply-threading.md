When we close several of your feedback items in one combined email, replying to that email now threads your reply back into Cobblr as a followup, the same way replying to a single-item resolution already did. Previously a reply to a combined email had no tokenized reply address, so it landed in an unmonitored mailbox instead of reaching your conversation.
