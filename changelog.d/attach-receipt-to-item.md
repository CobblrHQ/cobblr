You can attach a receipt to an item you already have. Choose the PDF from the item's own camera button and Cobblr reads it into a purchase, links the item to it, and puts nothing in your inbox, because that item is already there.

## docs

The camera button on a scan card takes a receipt as well as a picture. Choose a PDF or CSV and it is read as the paperwork for that item rather than as a new batch of things to sort: the purchase is recorded in full with every line on it, the item is linked to that purchase, and nothing new appears in your inbox.

That is the difference between the two doors. The upload button at the top of the inbox is an intake, where the receipt arrives first and its lines become your items. The button on a card is the other direction, where the item is already there and you are filing the paperwork behind it.

The link survives a re-run of the AI, because which receipt something was bought on is a fact about the purchase rather than a guess the AI made.
