Two things with different numbers are two things: a set number, a part number, a model number, a SKU, an ISBN, a serial, a VIN or a licence plate is the thing's identity, so two scans that carry different ones are never offered as one product and a scan is never told it is "already on the shelf" as the other, however alike their names read. A run of four or more digits in a name now counts as such a number (a "10 pk" or a year does not).
