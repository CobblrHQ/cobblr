The homepage is more useful. A pinned view that groups its data (a kanban "by state", "by type") now shows the group breakdown with counts and proportion bars, so you see the distribution at a glance instead of a flat list of the first few rows. The redundant full/half width toggle is gone. And your scanner's waiting-to-file queue (with the "what do you want to do" shortcuts) is now a proper section above recent activity, where you can reorder or hide it, instead of buried at the very bottom.
