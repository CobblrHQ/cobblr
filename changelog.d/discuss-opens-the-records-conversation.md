**Discuss on a record opens that record's conversation.** On record views that open as a window over a list, Discuss used to open the workspace-wide conversation with no sign of the record, so a message written there was not about it. Now the side panel opens on the record's own conversation, the message box shows the record as a chip (the same chip Ask Cobb uses), and what you send stays with the record. × on the chip talks to the whole workspace instead.
