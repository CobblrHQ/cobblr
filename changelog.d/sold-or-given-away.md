A thing you sold, gave away or threw out can now say so. **No longer mine…** on its page asks how it left, when, for how much and to whom; it then leaves your lists and totals (an asset no longer counts in "paid X in all"), stays findable under **Gone** and in search, and its page reads "Sold 2026-09-12 for $180 to a neighbour · kept 3 years 2 months". **Bring back** undoes it, and you can tell the assistant ("I sold the old Ender 3 for $120").

## docs

**When a thing is no longer yours.** On an asset, a machine or an item, **No longer mine…** under its buttons asks what happened: sold, given away, parted out, converted, traded, lost, or thrown out, the day, the price when it was sold, and who has it now. Saving it:

- takes it out of the list and its totals: an asset leaves the register and its "paid X in all";
- keeps the record whole, with its history, photos and printed labels, findable in search and under **Gone** (machines), **Gone or put away** (assets) or **Archived only** (items);
- puts one line at the top of its page: *Sold 2026-09-12 for $180 to a neighbour · kept 3 years 2 months* ("kept" is counted from its acquired date when it has one).

**Bring back** on that line makes it yours again, back in the list in the state it left from. Picking **sold** or **given away** in an asset's State does the same as **No longer mine…**, and picking a state it is still yours in brings it back. The assistant takes it in words: "I sold the old Ender 3 for $120", "I gave the drill press to my brother", "I got the drill press back".
