**The put-away front door.** Scanned things and never put them away? Now the dashboard says so: whenever captures exist with no home, a card appears ("14 scanned items don't have a home yet") with one action: **Put them away**. It previews where everything should go (grouped destinations, with the evidence), nothing moves until you confirm, and the guided put-away walk handles the physical half. The scan page carries the same strip above the inbox, and the card disappears when everything has a home. Live Sort stays where scanning starts (the scan page, the camera's Sort chip); if you re-scan something already captured mid-sort, it gets routed, never duplicated.
