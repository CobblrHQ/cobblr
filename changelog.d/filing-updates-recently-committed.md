Filing something from the Scan Inbox now updates Recently committed straight away. It used to keep its old count and leave the item out, so Send back was out of reach until you reloaded the page. Every way of filing (Add, File all, the dashboard's Add all, the put-away plan) now refreshes the inbox, its history and its counts together.
