**Replying to a workspace notification from Discord now respects your role.** A comment posted through a Discord card is a real write, so it now requires the same access the in-app reply does: you must be a current member of that workspace. A read-only guest, or someone whose access was removed after the notification was sent, is declined instead of being able to post. Members, editors, admins and owners reply exactly as before.
