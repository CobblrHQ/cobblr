**Every picture opens in the same viewer, and it steps through the set.** A feedback report's screenshots, a record's photo, a part's or a design's gallery, the Files page and a print's camera frame all open in one viewer now. Use the arrow keys, the buttons at the sides or a swipe on a phone to step through the set; it shows where you are, like "2 of 5", and Esc closes it. A report's screenshots used to open one at a time, and some galleries opened each picture in a new browser tab.
