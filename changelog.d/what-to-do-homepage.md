A brand-new workspace now opens with **"What do you want to do?"** — one distilling search over three converging lanes: **ready-made setups** (recipes), **building blocks** (capabilities), and **build-it-yourself** (describe/scan/AI). Type "parts" and all three narrow at once; pick a building block like Inventory and the recipes lane funnels to its ready-made versions (Home Inventory, Filament, …) with a "start blank" option. Modules and bundles finally converge on one on-ramp, so a new user can actually get started.
