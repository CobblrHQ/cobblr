Grouped table views now show counts and totals by one or two columns, with separate totals for each currency.

## docs

Group a collection with **Group by** in its header, or create a table view in
Views. Pick one column and optionally a second. Text, choices, linked records
and booleans can form groups; dates can use month, quarter or year.

Each group shows how many records it holds and the totals of its number and
money columns. Currency totals stay separate, such as $1,240 · €300. The grand
total at the bottom covers the whole filtered view, including records you have
not loaded yet. Collapse a section to keep the view compact. On a phone,
records appear as cards inside the sections.

Save the view to keep its grouping, sort and filters. You can make it private
or share it with the workspace. Use **Show more** inside a section to read its
next records. When exporting the view, check **Include subtotals** to put the
group and grand totals into the CSV file too.
