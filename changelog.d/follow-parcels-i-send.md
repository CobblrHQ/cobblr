Sales can now follow the parcels you send: turn on **Follow parcels I send** in Sales settings and each shipped order is marked delivered when the carrier says so, using your own tracking connection, never a paid one of Cobblr's.

## docs

**Follow parcels I send.** In Sales, the settings button (the gear beside
Customers) has **Follow parcels I send**, off until you turn it on. With it on,
Cobblr asks where each shipped order's parcel is a few times a day (more often
on the day it is due) and, when the carrier says it was delivered, marks the
order **delivered** on its card, on its history and in the activity log. It
stops asking two days after the delivered scan.

It asks through **your own** tracking connection, the one under Your account,
Connections (your own bridge works, and is the free way). Cobblr never pays a
carrier or a tracking service for this. If the service says it is busy, Cobblr
leaves it alone for as long as it asked; if it is full, until tomorrow. The
settings panel says which, and **Check now** asks straight away.
