Adding ?diag=1 to the scanner URL shows a readout of what your device is actually doing: which decode engine is running, which camera lenses it exposes, whether continuous autofocus was accepted, whether zoom and torch are available, and live decode timings. One tap copies it. It is off unless asked for, and the scanner now warns instead of staying silent when a browser refuses continuous autofocus.
