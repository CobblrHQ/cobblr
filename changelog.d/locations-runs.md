**Fifteen racks are one row.** When a room holds a numbered run of the same thing (Rack 1 to Rack 15, Bin 20 to Bin 60), the list draws it as one row, "Rack 1 – 15 · 15 racks · 75 inside", closed, and opens to the individual racks. Tick its box to select every rack in it for a bulk print or delete without unfolding. A run that is everything inside its parent stays plain, so a rack's shelves are still one click away, not two. Runs read in your order, so a bottom-up rack still shows shelf 5 first.
