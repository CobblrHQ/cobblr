A recount adds a new spool under its filament type, and asks which type when two read alike

## docs

In a collection whose things belong to kinds, such as Spools and your Filament Types, a recount matches something new against the kinds: a white Kingroon PETG spool you have no spool record for yet is noted as a new spool of that kind, and when it could be two kinds the recount asks which. When you finish, it is added under its kind, the same as a scanned spool.
