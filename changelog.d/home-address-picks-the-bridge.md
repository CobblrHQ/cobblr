**A home address picks your bridge for you.** Type an address that is on your own network, like 192.168.1.50 or partdb.local, into Part-DB, Homebox, a sync connection, a print manager or a 3D printer connection on a hosted Cobblr, and the form says so straight away. Where it can, it routes the connection through your bridge by itself; where it cannot, it says what to do next, with a link to set a bridge up. Before, nothing reacted until the connection failed. A self-hosted Cobblr, which usually sits on the same network, asks for nothing.
