**Pick where a label prints, right from the toolbar.** The printer chip on the Labels page is now a target selector: choose a saved printer or **System print** (the browser ⌘P dialog), and the whole toolbar follows your choice. The media list, the print button, and the preview all match the active target. So a Bluetooth label printer that is off, put away, or just not what you want right now is no longer a dead end: switch to System print and every size is available again (US Letter, 4x6, Avery, any roll). Your saved printer stays saved. Adding a printer, its settings, and Forget all live in the same menu, with Forget kept separate and behind a confirm so you never lose a printer by accident.
