A record never hides a value it holds. A machine imported as "5 of these" into a specialised collection showed no quantity at all, and nothing on the record could see or change it. A section a collection folds away now stays on any record that has something in it: the quantity of 5 shows and can be edited, a warranty date that is set stays in view, and units on file or contents inside a thing are always listed.
