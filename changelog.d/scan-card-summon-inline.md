**Less scrolling on an expanded scan item.** The "Add to ..." commit buttons no longer sit on a full-width row of their own beneath the card; they move into the bottom of the details column, which always ran shorter than the photo column beside it, so the row they used to occupy is simply gone. They are joined there by a collapse arrow in the card's bottom-right corner, so you can close a long item from where you finished reading instead of scrolling back up to the one at the top.
