**A scan changes the count of something you already have, both ways.** When a scan matches something you count, the card shows one control: **Change by**, starting at +1 for what is in your hands, where you can step, type any number, or go below zero to take one (scan the empty box, one is used up), with "You have 3 → 4" worked out for you; or **Set total**, a recount in its own colour, filed as a correction rather than as something used. Why is an optional chip, never a question. A take that empties the shelf offers **Add to shopping list**, and the camera's new **Count** mode opens every scan in Set total for a stocktake. The card no longer says "you already have this" four ways: one line, one control.
