AI Pick now uses the photo it chooses, with Undo, and the photo it replaces stays on the record. A record's photos and files share one row with the main photo first and starred, each photo labelled Your photo or Catalog photo, and any photo can be made main. The photo search remembers its results after a reload, outlines the one in use and keeps the AI's mark, and the chosen tile looks the same in every picker.
