An undo that stops short now offers you the rest instead of leaving it there. "Put back 5 of 6. Left Shelf 3 alone, because you have changed it since" comes with a "Take that one back too" button: nothing is decided for you, but the choice is one press away rather than something you have to go and do by hand. Taking the offer is itself a tracked change, so it can be undone in turn.

## docs
When an undo leaves something alone, it says which and why, and offers to take that back too. Cobb never decides to overwrite your own later edit, but he does put the option in front of you: "Left Shelf 3 alone, because you have changed it since" with a button beside it. Pressing that goes through with the parts that were held back, including deleting a container along with what is still inside it, and it is recorded like any other change, so you can undo the undo.
