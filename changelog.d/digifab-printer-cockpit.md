The printer detail modal is now a proper "cockpit": a wide, two-column dashboard (camera + live temps + filament on the left, controls + files on the right) that fits on one screen with no scrolling, and the motion controls are a real directional jog pad: X/Y as a cross with Home in the middle, a dedicated Z column, and a 1/10/100mm step selector, instead of a flat row of buttons.
