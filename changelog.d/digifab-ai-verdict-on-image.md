**The print-failure AI now shows its work on the picture it judges.** The camera wall and each fleet tile carry a graded verdict badge (green, amber, red rolling score, and a red "paused by AI" flag when it tripped), the printer cockpit overlays the same badge on the live camera frame with a banner when a print was auto-paused, and a new strip under the frame says when the AI last looked, through which detector, and how many samples this print. A one-tap "Check now" takes a single live reading and tells you whether it would trip the auto-pause. Printers watched by an external detector say so instead (Cobblr stands down its camera for those, as before).
