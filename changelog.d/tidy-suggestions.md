Turn on Tidy to get up to five overnight ideas for organising your workspace, with review, Undo and remembered dismissals.

## docs

On instances that offer it, open Settings → Tidy and turn on **Tidy-up
suggestions** to get up to five overnight suggestions in Needs you and your
morning notification digest. It starts off, and an update never turns it on.

Use **Do it** to review or open the existing edit screen. A move lists the
things first, then **Move all** applies it with **Undo**. **Not now** holds a
suggestion for seven days. **Never suggest this** remembers its kind and the
records it concerns. Turning the setting off stops the nightly review.
