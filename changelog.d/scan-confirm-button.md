New green **"This is good: lock it in"** button on a scanned barcode item: when the listing is correct, one tap verifies its name, brand, category and photo into the shared barcode database, so every future scan of that code, in any workspace, gets your clean entry instead of a thin crowdsourced one. It doesn't re-run the AI or commit to inventory; it just locks the data in.
