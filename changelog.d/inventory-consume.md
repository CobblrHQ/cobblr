The honest answer to "how does Cobblr know I used something" — **no chore, no counting.** Two zero-friction ways to log consumption arrived:

- **One-tap "Use one" / "Used up"** on any part. Tap "Use one" as you grab an item; "Used up" when you toss the empty. The moment you cross your reorder threshold, it lands on your shopping list automatically.
- **A replace-clock for scheduled swaps** (furnace filter, water filter, printer nozzle). Set "replace every N days" and Cobblr flags it the day it's due — then one **"Replaced"** tap resets the clock, uses up a spare, and reorders if you're short. Zero taps until due; one tap at the swap you're already doing.

Both are binary — a single tap at the moment you're already handling the thing — never a number to type.
