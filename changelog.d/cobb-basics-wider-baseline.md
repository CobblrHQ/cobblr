Ask Cobblr answers more out of the box. Even with no AI connected and nothing trained, Cobb now fields ~20 common questions — "what is Cobblr", find & search, add/edit/delete something, enable a module, custom fields, invite people, settings, export & backup, labels & QR, mobile, account & password, and feedback — on top of the originals. It's the untrained baseline every workspace gets; you can still tweak or add to it under Configuration → Assistant.
