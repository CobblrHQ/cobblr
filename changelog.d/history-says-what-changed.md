A record's History now says what each edit changed: every field, custom ones included, from what it was to what it became, under the field's own label, with money, dates, places and linked records in words. One person's edits within half an hour are grouped, a line per field. A photo change names the record and where the picture came from, with a card showing the picture, its file and when it changed. Built-in fields can now carry a label (Paid, Bought from), and editing a build or a catalog now shows in its History.
