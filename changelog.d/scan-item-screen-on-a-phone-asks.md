On a phone the scan item screen grows a dropdown's choices from the form ("New category…"), an empty picture slot is a tap that opens your camera roll, and the pinned footer sits on the screen's foot in the installed app instead of a band above it.

## docs

**A new choice from the form.** Every dropdown field on the item screen and the card's form ends with **New category…** (the field's own word). Pick it and a one-line box takes the name where the select was: **Enter** adds it to the field's definition for everyone, the same list Configuration → Fields edits, and selects it on this row; **Esc** backs out. A member can grow a list this way; a guest sees no entry.

**An empty picture slot is a tap.** On the item screen, an empty CATALOG slot or an empty YOUR PHOTO slot opens your picker (the camera roll, or the camera as the second choice your phone offers); the picture lands where the slot says, the same way the strip's Photo button files it.

**The footer sits on the foot.** In the installed app the item screen's pinned footer used to sit about 68px above the bottom of the screen: the sheet and its footer each padded for the home indicator. The inset is counted once now, on the footer; in Safari, where the layout already ends above the address bar, nothing changes.
