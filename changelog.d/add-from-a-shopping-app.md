## docs

**Add from a shopping app.** Looking at a product in a shopping app? Send it to Cobblr's inbox. The app shares the product's name and its link; Cobblr keeps the name as the item's name and the link as its source, so the item is named even when the shop's page refuses to be read.

- **Android, and Chrome on a computer:** install Cobblr from the browser, then in the shopping app tap **Share** and pick **Cobblr**. One new item lands in the scan inbox. A shared photo becomes a photo item.
- **iPhone and iPad:** Safari cannot make a web app a share target, so tap **Share**, then **Copy**, and paste anywhere on Cobblr's scan inbox or into the box at the top. Several lines, each a name and a link, become several items.

Cobblr still looks the product up and adds what it finds (a photo, the brand, the model), shown beside your name, never in its place. You can rename the item any time. When nothing else identified it, the card says **Named from what you shared**.
