**The end of a sandbox lists what you built there.** When the hour is up, the ending page now says what you made in your own words for it: the bundles you installed, the collections, fields and automations you set up, and the records you added, counted, with the ones you scanned. It never lists the demo's own stock, nor anything you deleted since, and it says plainly what stays behind (the stock, the activity feed, chats with the assistant, the guest account). The same list is what a real account will be offered to bring along once that door opens.
