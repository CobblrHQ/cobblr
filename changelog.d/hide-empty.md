Added Hide empty to Locations and Tags, with a count of what is hidden and your choice remembered on this device.

## docs

In Locations **List**, **Hide empty** leaves places with something inside them or their sub-places. Things kept inside a toolbox or backpack count too. On **Tags**, it leaves tags that are on records; a used tag inside an unused one stays visible. The count beside each toggle says how many are hidden. Both start off and remember your choices separately on this device. Turn them off to see everything again.
