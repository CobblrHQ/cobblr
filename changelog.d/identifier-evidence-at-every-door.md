A scan identified before the ISBN fix no longer offers its unconfirmed ISBN, serial or other identifier when you fill an existing record from it. "Is this the same one?" lists only what the scan can back up and names what it left out, and filing, filling in and File everything all leave such an identifier off the record. Drafts from before the fix now show those identifiers as suggestions.
