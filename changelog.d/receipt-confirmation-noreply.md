**Replying to a receipt confirmation email no longer creates junk in your scan inbox.** The confirmation used to invite a reply, and any reply, even a question like "what is this?", got pulled in as an unidentified inbox item. The confirmation now comes from a no-reply address and threads under your original, and a reply to it is ignored. To bring a receipt in, forward it to the receipts address shown on your Scan page, as before.
