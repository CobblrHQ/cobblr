**PDFs open in the app, every page.** A pattern, a manual or any PDF on a record or on the Files page opens in a viewer with zoom, a fit-to-width button, pinch-zoom on a phone, and "Keep screen on" while you work from it. "Open in a new tab" and Download are one tap away.

## docs

**Reading a PDF in the app.** Press a PDF anywhere files are shown (a pattern on a Design, a record's files, the Files page) and it opens in the viewer instead of downloading:

- **Every page, in order.** Pages draw as you scroll to them, so a long pattern opens as fast as its first page.
- **Zoom:** **-** and **+** step the size, **Fit** sets the page to the width of the screen, and on a phone you can pinch.
- **Keep screen on** stops the screen going dark while you knit or build from the page, where your browser supports it. Press it again to let the screen sleep.
- **Download** saves a copy, and **Open in a new tab** hands the PDF to your browser's own viewer.

A file the app has no viewer for still opens in a new tab.
