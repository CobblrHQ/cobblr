The homepage funnel now speaks in things you'd actually say. **Column 1 lists only kinds of things you track** — Labels and Digital Fabrication moved to a small "Add a capability" strip (they act *on* your things; nobody "tracks labels"). **Column 2 leads with pre-shaped trackers** — one-kind setups like the new **Fasteners** tracker (thread, length, head, drive as real columns, grouped by thread) — with the big multi-module setups tucked under their own "Full setups" heading instead of being the first thing a new user has to decode.
