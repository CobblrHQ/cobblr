Better catalog-photo search in the scan inbox: the image search now sharpens a weak title with the author and a media word (so "Farmer Boy" searches "Farmer Boy Laura Ingalls Wilder book" and finds the real cover, not generic farm pictures), there's now an editable search box to type your own term, and a photographed item auto-suggests a clean catalog photo on identify (it used to only do that on a barcode mismatch).
