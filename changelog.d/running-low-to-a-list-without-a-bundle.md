Something running low can go on a shopping list without installing a bundle. An item marked low in Inventory has **Add to list** beside it: one tap puts it on the list it last went on, and the first time it asks which list (or starts one). On the Lists page you can also say yes to having running-low items added to a list you pick by themselves. That is off until you turn it on, and nothing is added or created until you ask.

## docs

**Running low, onto a list.** In Inventory, an item marked **low** has **Add to
list** beside it. The first time, it asks which list, or you can type a new
one. After that the same item goes straight back onto the list it last went on,
even after you have ticked it off and cleared the done items.

To have it happen by itself, open **Lists**: the card at the top asks **Add
running-low items to a list automatically?** Pick a list and choose **Turn on**.
From then on anything that runs low is added to that list on its own, once, and
the card says where things go, with **Stop** to turn it off. It is off until you
turn it on, and a workspace set up from the Groceries bundle already has it.
