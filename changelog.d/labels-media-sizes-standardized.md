Common thermal label sizes like 50x30 mm are now built in, and you can pick them (and print two-up) even when no printer is connected to Cobblr, by choosing what you are printing on. Every size picker now groups into Label rolls and Sheets and shows only what fits your printer.

## docs

Cobblr keeps a single library of print sizes, split into two kinds: Label rolls (a thermal or die-cut roll, like 4x6 inch or 50x30 mm) and Sheets (a full page like US Letter or an Avery layout). Every place you pick a size shows the same library, grouped the same way.

When a printer is connected, the picker shows only the sizes that printer can run: a 2 inch thermal printer lists the rolls up to 2 inch, a regular inkjet or laser lists sheet layouts. When no printer is set up and you are printing through your computer's own print dialog, a "printing on" control appears so you can say whether you are printing on a label roll or a sheet, then pick the size (for example a 50x30 mm roll, one-up or two-up). You can also choose "Custom size" from the size dropdown to define your own.
