Cobblr now **predicts when you'll run out** and reorders *ahead* of empty — no counting, no waiting for zero. It learns each item's burn rate from the usage you already log (every "use one", every stock change), and when something's a few days from running out it lands on your shopping list early. Parts show a live "≈ N days left" estimate. Items with their own signal (a replace-clock, or stock tracked by another system) opt out automatically.
