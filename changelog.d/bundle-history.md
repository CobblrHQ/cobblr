Bundles got an undo. Updating or uninstalling no longer discards the old version: the bundle's detail modal now shows a **version history** with one-tap **Restore** (validated before anything changes, and the restore itself is undoable). Updates also show **exactly what changes**: which fields, wires and views are added, changed or removed, instead of asking you to trust a version number.
