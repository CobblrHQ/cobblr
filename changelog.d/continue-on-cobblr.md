The email with your sandbox's work now has a Continue on Cobblr button that brings everything straight into a workspace once you sign up or sign in, pictures included, and a new workspace's first screen can take a backup file or a sandbox's link directly.

## docs

**Carry on from the email.** The email with your sandbox's work leads with **Continue on Cobblr**. Open it, sign up or sign in, and everything you made comes into a workspace for you: your own, if it is still empty, or a new one beside it, so nothing already there is touched. The file itself is the second link in the email, to keep or to run Cobblr yourself.

**Bring a file into a new workspace.** A workspace you have not put anything in yet shows **Bring in a backup or a sandbox file** on its first screen, for owners and admins. Upload the .zip, or paste the link from a sandbox's email and press **Bring it in**. Everything in it comes in, pictures included.
