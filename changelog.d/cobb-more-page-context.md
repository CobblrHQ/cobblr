**Ask Cobb now sees more of your screens.** After the Scan Inbox, Cobb also knows what's on your **Purchases** (open orders + how many are in transit), **Machines** (count + a breakdown of whatever states your machines use), and **Inventory** (item count + how many are low on stock) pages. So "what do I have going on?" reflects where you are, and you can ask about what you're staring at.
