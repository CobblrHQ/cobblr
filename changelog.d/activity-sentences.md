Recent activity now names what changed in plain sentences, links to your things, shows photos and keeps the time in one place.

## docs

Activity entries use “You” for your own changes and another member's name for theirs. Updates name the changed fields. Scans name the thing or barcode, and attached photos name the record. Tap a sentence to open its subject. Identical consecutive events within two minutes show a count; grouped rows expand. Automation runs that changed nothing stay in the automation's history.
