Ask Cobb stops sending you to a settings screen for something it can do itself. Asked to hide a field or make one required, it used to open the Fields page and leave the work to you; now it runs the change and shows it to you to confirm. It also recognises an action when the assistant spells its name with an underscore instead of a hyphen, instead of failing.
