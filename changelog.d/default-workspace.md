**Pin the workspace that opens on a fresh device.** In the workspace switcher, a **star** on each row sets your default: the one a new device (or a bare URL with no history) opens into. Before, a fresh sign-in just landed on your first *owned* workspace, which isn't always the one you want; now you decide. A device you've already used still resumes wherever you last were; the default is the no-history landing. One default at a time; click the star again to clear it.
