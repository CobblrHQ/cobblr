Each Bambu printer now has a **transport mode** in its LAN-access panel: **All cloud** (status/control/history via Bambu's cloud), **Prefer LAN** (status + control + file-push over your bridge, cloud fills in print-history names), or **LAN only** (everything local, cloud off — no internet needed, but you lose cloud-only history names/covers). Cobblr pulls live telemetry over LAN for the LAN modes, so status keeps flowing even with the printer off the cloud.
