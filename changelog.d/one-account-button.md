You can now sign in with your Cobblr account from the sign-in page, and your account page lists the Cobblr instances you can reach.

## docs
If your instance is set up with a Cobblr account, the sign-in page now offers
"Continue with your Cobblr account" underneath the usual email and password. It takes
you to your account, and brings you straight back signed in.

Your account page also lists the Cobblr instances you can get to, and says which ones
you already have a workspace on, so you can move between them without signing in again.

Nothing changes if your instance does not use a central account: the button only
appears when signing in that way will actually work, so you never get sent somewhere
that cannot finish the job.
