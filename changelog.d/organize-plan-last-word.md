**The put-away plan gives you the last word on everything.** Four new controls on the plan sheet. Rename a proposed new bin inline (pencil next to the chip): your word for it beats the AI's, and the renamed bin becomes the evidence that routes every future item of that family. Split any item out of a group with the × ("not related"): it stays in the inbox, unfiled. Expand any item (the chevron on its row) to open its full card right in the plan, accordion-style, and fix a wrong name, photo, or identification in place; the card is a fixer there (no confirm-into-a-table, no discard, so nothing gets accidentally filed mid-plan), and the sheet notices your edit and offers Re-plan. And a hint box lets you tell the planner ground truth ("these are camping gear") and re-plan with it folded in; groups you already accepted stay filed.
