**The dashboard now opens with what is late and what is coming.** A new "Up next" section lists everything dated across your whole workspace in one time-ordered list: maintenance you scheduled, tasks with a due date, orders you are expecting, things that expire, and any date field on any of your own records. It is grouped into Overdue, Today, Next 7 days and Later, each row saying which tracker it came from and how far away it is, and each row clicks through to the thing itself. Anything you have set a date on is already in here, with nothing to configure. The section hides itself when nothing is scheduled, and you can move or hide it with Arrange dashboard like any other.

## docs

The dashboard's **Up next** section answers "what should I do" in date order, and it is the first thing on the page.

It reads your workspace calendar, so it covers every module that contributes dated things: **maintenance** entries you have scheduled, **tasks** with a due date, **purchase orders** by their expected arrival, **expiry** dates, and **any date field you have added to any of your own records** (a "next service" on a machine, a "renew by" on a document). Nothing needs wiring: if a thing has a date, it is here.

Rows are grouped into **Overdue**, **Today**, **Next 7 days** and **Later**. Overdue is coloured and sorted newest-first, because the thing that slipped yesterday is the one you can still do something about. Each row shows which tracker it came from and how far away it is ("3 days ago", "in 2 weeks"), and clicks through to the record. Long groups show the first few with a link to the full calendar.

It looks back a year and forward a quarter. If nothing is scheduled the section is not shown at all, and **Arrange dashboard** moves or hides it like any other section.
