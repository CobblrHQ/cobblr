## docs

**When a thing is lent out.** On an asset, a machine or an item, **Lend…** asks who has it (someone on your People list, or any name), why (**repair**, **borrowed**, or your own words), since when and, if you like, when it is due back. Nothing else about it changes: it keeps its place, which is where it goes back to, and its state. Its page says *With Sam Lee · repair · since Oct 13, 2026 (due Oct 27, 2026) · returns to Front Hallway*, marked **overdue** once the day has passed.

While it is out:

- **Move**, **Back where it was**, moving its stock and **Print label** are greyed, and each says *Lent to Sam Lee.*;
- a lent printer gets no jobs from the print farm, and its tile on the fleet says who has it;
- its service reminders wait, and come back when it does;
- its due day is on the calendar, and Needs you shows it due soon, due today or overdue. With no due day, Needs you mentions it once it has been out a month.

**It's back** on that line ends the loan. Back from a repair it asks **Was it repaired?**; a yes puts *Repaired by Sam Lee* in its service log, with what was done. History keeps the whole loan, and Undo puts it back out. On an asset, **Who has it** and **Due back** follow the loan. The assistant takes it in words: "I dropped the Dremel off with Sam to fix it, back in two weeks", "the ladder is back, the clip is fixed", "what have I lent out?".
