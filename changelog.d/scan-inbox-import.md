The scan inbox can now **import a whole batch from a file**: companion app inbox exports (JSON or CSV) work as-is — 497 real items in one go, photos fetched, duplicates skipped on re-import — and any other system's CSV works with a quick column mapping in the preview. Imported items arrive as ordinary pending scans with the source's suggestions riding along as hints, so Cobblr's own matching still decides where things go.
