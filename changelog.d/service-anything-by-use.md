**Service anything by how much it is used.** Mark a number field like Mileage or Hours run as what the thing counts, and a service schedule can come due every 5,000 miles, not only every few months.

## docs

A service schedule can come due by use on anything you keep, not only on a printer. Add a number field such as **Mileage** or **Hours run** on the collection, and set its meaning to **What it counts as it is used**.

Then **Set a service schedule** offers it: *every [5000] [mileage]*. The schedule starts from today's reading, and the service comes up in Needs you once you enter a reading past the mark.
