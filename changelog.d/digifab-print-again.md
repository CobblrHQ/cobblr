Recent prints are now a first-class, full-width section at the bottom of the printer cockpit, and every print that ran as a Cobblr job has a one-click "Print again" that clones the original (same file, routing, material draw and build link) and sends it. Also fixed a quiet hole where a printer's history could show empty because it was matched by display name instead of device identity.
