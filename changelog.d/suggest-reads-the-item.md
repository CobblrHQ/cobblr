Fresh food that knows it needs to stay cold is now offered the Fridge. The
suggestion used to work only from an item's category, and the category table
says nothing about fresh vegetables on purpose, since potatoes and onions are
happier in a cupboard. So a bag of carrots whose own storage field said
refrigerated got no suggestion at all, and then a warning once it was filed
somewhere warm. Both halves now read the item first and its category second.
