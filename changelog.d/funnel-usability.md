The homepage "just add it" box now actually finds homes for what you type — with or without AI. Typing "my passport", "Netflix subscription", "a monstera plant" or "spool of black PLA" suggests the right ready-made tracker (Important Documents, Subscriptions, Plant Care, Filament…), community setups like Lego and 3D Printer Parts are now suggestible too, and suggestions **keep working after your first save** (they used to silently stop once anything landed in Inventory). Saving an unmatched item now says where it went ("Saved to Inventory — it's in your navbar") instead of a vague "Filed it." On phones, the add-something box is now at the top of the panel instead of two screens down, and a few "try:" example chips demo the flow in one tap. Also: typing in the kinds column now ranks name matches first (typing "mach" + Enter picks Machines, not Assets), and the dashboard no longer fires a burst of failed background requests on every load.
