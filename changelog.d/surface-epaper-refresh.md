Public surfaces (wall panels) now have a per-surface auto-refresh setting and an E-paper mode. Pick how often a published screen re-fetches (every 30s up to hourly, or Off for a static panel) and flip on E-paper mode to force a light, high-contrast (black-on-white) layout with auto-refresh off, so a reflective panel in a cabinet doesn't ghost on a timer. Works for view, board, and whole-App surfaces.
