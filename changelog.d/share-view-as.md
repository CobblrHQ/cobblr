See a share the way the other person sees it: **View as** in Settings, Sharing opens their shared workspace in a new tab, read-only, for half an hour. They can see that you looked.

## docs

**View as.** On a share in Settings, Sharing, **View as <them>** opens their shared workspace exactly as they see it (their scan inbox, their suggestions, what your What's shared lets through) in a new tab. It is read-only: nothing can be changed from it. A bar across the top says whose view it is and how long is left, and **Exit** ends it. It lasts half an hour. Their Sharing page says when you last looked.
