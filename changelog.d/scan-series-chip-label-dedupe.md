Three scan-inbox clarity fixes. Each identified item now shows its **series as a chip** on the card (e.g. *Little House on the Prairie* on a stack of those books): the most useful at-a-glance media label, not buried in the "tag them all" banner. Two suggestion chips that read as the **same table** are collapsed to one: a workspace with two tables both named "Bookshelf" no longer shows "Bookshelf · 3 fields" next to "Bookshelf · 1 field" with no way to tell them apart (the richer match wins; either is still pickable in the confirm form). And the item **subtitle no longer starts with a stray "· "** when there's no barcode/ISBN, the author/publisher line is now built from the fields that exist, so nothing reads as missing.
