Yarn now counts in balls, the word everyone knows: Length / ball, Full ball, Price / ball, and Track each ball separately and Open a ball on a yarn's page. Yarn you already have moves over to balls when the Yarn app updates, and a yarn you set to another unit keeps it. A label or search that says skein still finds your yarn.
