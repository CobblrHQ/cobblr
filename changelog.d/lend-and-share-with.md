Lend something to the person you share with and it appears in their shared workspace until you get it back. Any record can now be shared on its own: **Share with…** on its page or in a list's selection bar, and its page says who it is shared with, with **Stop** beside it.

## docs

**Lending to someone you share with.** A share is with a person on your People list, so **Lend it** can name them. A thing you lend them appears in their shared workspace (with when it is due back) and leaves it when you mark it got back. To lend without sharing, turn off **What you lend to them** in What's shared.

**Sharing one thing at a time.** Once your workspace is shared with someone, every record offers **Share with <them>** (or **Share with…** when there are several people). A shared record says **Shared with <them>**; **Stop** takes it out of their shared workspace, even when a whole collection is shared. Tick several things in a list and use **Share with…** in the selection bar to share them together. Each can be undone from the message that follows.
