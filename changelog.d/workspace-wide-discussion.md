You can now talk to the whole workspace, not only about one record at a time.

## docs

### Talking to the workspace

A conversation does not have to be about anything. Open Discussion in the side
panel while you are not looking at a record and you get the workspace room: one
conversation for everyone here, for the things that do not belong to a
particular part or machine.

Mentions work the same way, so you can name somebody in the room and they hear
about it exactly as they would on a record.

Record conversations stay with their record, which is the point of them: what
was said about a machine is there next time anyone opens that machine, a year
later, without anybody having to remember. The room is for everything else.

The Discussion page lists both, so there is still one place to catch up.
