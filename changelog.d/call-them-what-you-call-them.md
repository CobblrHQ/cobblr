Call your things what you call them. Yarn asks whether you count in balls, skeins or hanks, and Groceries, Lego, Bookshelf and Home Inventory offer their own words (pantry items, kits, titles, belongings). Pick the word when you install, or change it later in the table's settings: every label, the New button, the count on each record and what Cobb says all use it, and an update never puts the old word back.

## docs

**What do you call these?** Some tables offer a choice of word for the things
they hold. Yarn asks whether you count your stash in **balls**, **skeins** or
**hanks** (ball unless you pick another). Groceries offers item, grocery or
pantry item; Lego sets offers set or kit; Bookshelf offers book or title; Home
Inventory offers item or belonging.

When you install one of these, the install screen asks **What do you call
these?** with the usual word already picked. Choose yours and every label says
it: "Length / skein (m)", "Price / skein", "Full skein", the New button, and
the unit each new record counts in.

To change it later, open the table's **Settings** tab and pick a different
word under **What these are counted in** (or **What one of these is called**);
the same choice is in **Configuration → Presentation** when you edit the table. The labels change at once,
and records that counted in the old word move to the new one. A unit you set by
hand on one record (a cone, say) is left alone. Updating the bundle keeps the
word you picked.
