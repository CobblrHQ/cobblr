**Scan a location to start filing into it — zero clicks.** Print a QR label for a room/shelf/bin (any `core-locations` place), and scanning it makes it the active filing bin: everything you scan next files in there. Scan a **container** while a room is active and it *nests* — the container is filed into the room and becomes the new bin (so "scan the garage, scan the tote, scan the parts" lands the parts in the tote, the tote in the garage). Scanning another **area** just switches context (it won't wrongly nest one room inside another), and a move that would create a loop is skipped. Works the same from the camera and a hardware scan gun.
