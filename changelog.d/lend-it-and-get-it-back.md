**Lend a thing out and get it back.** Lend it names who has it and the day it is due back, a late loan shows in Needs you, and Got it back clears both. A service schedule's sheet now also asks when it was last done.

## docs

**Lend it** on an asset hands it to someone: pick who from your People, a member or anyone listed by name, and if you like the day it is due back. Once that day has passed, the loan shows in Needs you as overdue.

**Got it back** clears who has it and the due day. It shows only while the thing is lent out. Both can be undone from the card they leave, and you can ask the assistant instead, like "Sam borrowed the drill until Friday".
