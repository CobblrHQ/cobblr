A scan inbox row has one name, yours when you gave it one: typing in a card's box is never interrupted by the row refreshing underneath, the desktop card's title edits in place with a tap, and a re-run of the AI offers its read beside your name instead of replacing it.

## docs

**A field you are typing in is yours until you leave it.** The inbox refreshes while the AI runs (a re-run, a picture sweep, the poll), and it used to re-seed every box on the item screen from the fresh row, throwing your caret and your half-typed text away. Now a box you are in, or have typed in, keeps what you typed; a value that changed underneath you is offered under the field as *updated underneath: "…"* with **Use theirs** and **Keep mine**, and only when it actually differs from what you have. A box you have not touched follows the row as before. This holds for every box on the screen and on the desktop card's form: the name, the brand, the serial, the quantity and the table's own fields.

**The title is the name.** On a desktop card, tap the title to edit it in place, on the closed card or the open one; **Enter** saves (the same rename the NAME chip and the phone's Name box use), **Escape** reverts, and a tap anywhere else on the card still opens it.

**A name you typed outlives a re-run.** Your rename is stamped as yours, like a field you answered (`field_provenance`), and a re-run of the AI never writes over it: every surface (the closed card, the open card, the phone row and screen, File N and the put-away plan) shows your name, and when the AI's newest read differs the card says *AI read "…"* with **Use it** beside the title. The stored name and the AI's read both stay on the row, so nothing is lost either way.
