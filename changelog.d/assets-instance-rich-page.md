**Assets instances (Vehicles, Plant Care, Documents…) now open the full page, not
a bare list.** Instead of a name-only stub, an assets instance renders the same
rich Assets page scoped to it: thumbnails, the list/gallery view toggle, saved
views, and full detail/edit, so a Vehicle on /vehicles is a complete record.
Adding a new one and opening detail stay inside the instance, and "New …" uses the
instance's own noun.
