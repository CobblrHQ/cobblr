**Find a book by the name you know it by, and read its title your way.** A titled work now keeps its other forms (the original, a translation, a transliteration) when it is filed, so searching "Yellow Fog" finds Желтый туман, and the record's title reads in your Titles in another language format on the record, in search results and when the assistant names it, the same way the scan inbox already showed it. Change the preference and every served title follows, on the parts table and an item's own page too; renaming still edits the stored name.
