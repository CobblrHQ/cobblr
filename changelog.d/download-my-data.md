You can download a copy of what Cobblr holds about you from your account page: your profile, the workspaces you belong to, your tokens and connections (never their secrets), your notification settings, what you did and what you sent us.

## docs

**Download your data.** Under **Your account → Identity & password**, above Delete account, press **Make a copy**. It takes a moment, and the page shows **Download** when it is ready (we also email you). The file holds one readable summary and a file per area: your profile and preferences, the workspaces you belong to and your role in each, your API tokens and connections (their names and dates, never the token or the key), your notification settings, every action you took, and the feedback you sent. The records inside a workspace belong to that workspace: the summary lists the ones you own and where to take each one's backup. You can make one copy a day, and each is kept for seven days; only you can download it.
