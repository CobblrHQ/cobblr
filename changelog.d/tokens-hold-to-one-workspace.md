A personal API token can now be held to one workspace and to **Look only**, **Look and make changes** or **Full access**. A new token starts on the workspace you are in with Look and make changes, and the Connect an AI assistant page makes one for its workspace only. Tokens made before this are marked **Full access, all workspaces**, with a button to narrow them.
