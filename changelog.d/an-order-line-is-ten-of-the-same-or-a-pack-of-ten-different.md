An order line of several now says whether it is several of the same thing or a pack of different things, and you can scan its units in one by one.

## docs

**Ten of the same, or a pack of ten different.** A line on an order for several of something says which it is: **10 of the same** (ten white spools: one thing, ten of it) or **A pack of 10 different** (a multicolour pack: ten things). It is worked out from the line (a mixed variant like Multicolor is different, a plain colour is the same), and one tap switches it. A pack of different things becomes one line per thing, each matched on its own and each with its share of the price and its own line on the purchase. **Undo split** in Recently committed puts it back as one line.

**Split off the ones that differ.** On a line of the same thing, **Split off…** takes the few that are not like the rest ("8 white, 2 black") into a line of their own, which you name. Both keep their share of the price and their place on the purchase. **Put back into its line** undoes it.

**Scan the items in.** On a line of the same thing, **Scan the items** opens the scan page for that line, and every scan you make there, by camera, scanner or photo, counts as one of its units: the line reads **3 of 10 received** and nothing new is made, since the line is already the one thing. The scan page shows how many are in as you go. A scan made anywhere else that looks like one of an open line's units asks **Part of Kingroon 3D #KR52731, line 1?**; yes counts it there. A scan is never counted twice. The purchase line shows the same count, offers **Scan the items** too, and is received when the last unit is in.
