**Put a thing away instead of deleting it.** Put away takes something you sold or lost off the register and out of search and Needs you, and keeps its history and what it cost. Restore brings it back.

## docs

**Put away** on an asset takes it off the register without deleting it. It keeps its history, what it cost and anything linked to it, and it no longer shows in search, saved views or Needs you.

**Put away (N)** in the Assets header lists what is put away. Open one and press **Restore** to bring it back as it was. Either can be undone from its card. **Delete** still removes a thing for good.
