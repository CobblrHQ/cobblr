A bundle update that changes something you customised now shows exactly what: your version beside the update's, with a choice for each (keep mine, use the update's, or keep both for a list of options). It also lists what is new in every version since yours, and an update that only adds options you already have no longer asks at all.

## docs

### Choosing what to keep on an update

When an update would change something you customised, its row on your home
page says **Choose what to keep**. Open it and each of those things is listed
with your version beside the update's.

- **Keep mine** leaves yours exactly as it is.
- **Use the update's** takes the new one.
- **Keep both**, for a list of options, keeps every option from either side,
  yours first. It is where a list starts, since it loses nothing.

Each choice is for that one thing: taking the update's options for one field
leaves a name you gave another field alone. Below the choices, the update lists
what is new in every version since the one you have.
