**A print plate can come out partly good, and that is the build's yield.** When a plate makes more than one, clearing the printer now offers "Some scrapped…" to say how many came out good; those count as made and the rest as scrap. The build shows its yield ("Made 9 good · 1 scrap, 90%") and each work order shows how many of its run are made, all from that one verdict.
