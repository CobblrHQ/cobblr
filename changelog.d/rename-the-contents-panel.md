The Contents of a kind of record can be called what you call them, like Parts for a computer or In the bag for a backpack: a pencil on the panel heading renames it for every record of that kind, and the word follows into messages, history and the assistant's answers.

## docs

**Call it what you call it.** An owner or an admin sees a pencil beside a record's **Contents** heading. Type a word (Parts, In the bag, Tools) and press Enter: every record of that kind or list now says it, for everyone. Esc cancels, and an empty word puts the default back. The same setting is under **Configuration, Presentation**, as "What is inside one is called".

The word follows wherever what is inside is named: the panel heading, the messages when you add or take something out ("Took RAM stick out of Parts"), the record's History ("added 2 × RAM stick to Parts"), and the assistant's answers ("the Gaming PC's parts: …"). You can also ask the assistant: "call what is inside my computers Parts".

A bundle can choose the word for its list (Computers calls them Parts). Your own word always wins, and a bundle update never changes it.
