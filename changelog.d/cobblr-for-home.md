### Cobblr for Home

**Cobblr for Home** is Cobblr wearing one outfit: a home-inventory app, signed up for at `/start/home`, with nothing else in the way. On a phone the first screen after signup is the **camera**. Point it at a thing (a barcode, or just the thing), and it arrives in your catalog with a name, a photo and the room you are standing in, chosen once from the **Set area** chip and kept for the session. For a barcode, the "Added" note that follows carries **Print label**: one tap prints a QR label on a Bluetooth label printer, so the box says what is in it and scanning the label later opens the record. Nothing prints on its own. A photo capture waits in the scan inbox with its best match suggested; file it from there, and the record page prints the label.

**Pairing a printer** lives under **Your account → Label printer** (`/me/printer`), because a Bluetooth printer is paired by the browser on the device you scan with. Print a test label there and set it as the default. Web Bluetooth is a Chrome/Android feature; on an iPhone the label is queued and prints from a device that has one.

**Starter rooms** (Kitchen, Living room, Bedroom, Bathroom, Garage, Closet) are real Locations from day one; rename or add rooms any time. On a desktop the app opens on the catalog and offers **Pair your phone** so the camera is still one tap away.

The same three extras (**Scan to add**, **Labels**, **Starter rooms**) are checkboxes on the Home Inventory bundle for a full workspace, so the app and the full platform share one bundle. When the house is no longer the only thing you track, the app grows into the whole of Cobblr with everything you entered.
