Two new ways to see the farm: the print queue gained a Timeline view — one row per machine showing what's running (with its finish clock) and the jobs lined up behind it in the order they'll actually run — and the Fleet section gained a Cameras view, a webcam wall where every machine's feed is the card with status and progress as an overlay.
