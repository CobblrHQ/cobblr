Scan a bin's QR label and — when that bin holds exactly one SKU (a bin of M3 screws, a drawer of GT2 pulleys) — go straight to adjusting its count: +1/+5/+10 and −1/−5/−10 chips, a "set exact count" recount, and undo. The count shown is that bin's row only. Works from the phone scanner, a desktop wedge gun, and the bin's own page. Derived automatically: a bin with a second SKU keeps the normal filing flow, and an "Add something else to this bin" chip hands off to filing when a bin is gaining one.
