**Act where you see it.** The "needs you" rows now carry their actions: overdue tasks show **inline checkboxes right on the row** (≤3 items: check them off without opening anything), a printer holding a finished plate shows **Good / Scrapped verdict buttons in place**, and rows with more items expand inline to a per-item list. Printers waiting for a bed-clear verdict and failed prints now appear in the feed at all. At-a-glance tiles slimmed to **stat-chips** (same info, half the height), an empty *your views* section now invites you to **pin a saved view** (dismissible), toast bursts cap at 4 with a "+N earlier" pill, and the account menu gains **Pair phone**. The standing scan card is gone from the dashboard, scanning lives in the sidebar, the header, your paired phone, and any barcode reader (which works from every screen).
