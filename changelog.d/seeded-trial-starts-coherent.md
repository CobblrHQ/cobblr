**A trial opens on a kitchen that reads as a kitchen, not a backlog.** A ready-made sandbox could wait days before a visitor got it, and its groceries had drifted past their dates by then: a first screen showed six overdue items with blank locations. Sandboxes now wait at most six hours before being replaced, every seeded grocery is filed in the place its storage names, and the seed shows one item past due and one due today, the rest ahead. The dashboard's "needs you" list now counts by day the way the schedule does, with its own "due today" row, so the two never disagree.
