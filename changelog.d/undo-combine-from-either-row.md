Combining two scans can now be undone. A scan that was combined into another shows **Undo combine** in Recently deleted instead of a Restore that refused, and the combined scan offers it too, on its card and in its tools menu. Either one puts both scans back in the inbox with their own quantities; a count or photo you changed on the combined scan since stays as you left it.
