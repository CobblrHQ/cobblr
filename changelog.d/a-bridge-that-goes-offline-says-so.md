**You hear when a bridge goes offline.** Ten minutes after a bridge stops answering, the workspace's owners and admins get one notification naming it, and another when it is back. Anything that needs the bridge while it is down now says which bridge, how long ago it was last heard from, and what to check, instead of a line about a channel.
