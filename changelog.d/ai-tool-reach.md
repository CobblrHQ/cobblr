**Cobb (and MCP) can now reach everywhere.** The AI tool registry grew real coverage of the whole workspace: purchase orders + vendors, sales customers + orders, builds, locations, tags, and catalogs are fully creatable/editable/deletable through the AI (digifab jobs: edit/delete; files: delete). Your **custom fields**: including their dropdown choices. Now ride along with every kind, so "set the finish to matte" works on a field you defined yourself. A new **list_related** tool follows a record's links ("which projects use this part?"). And **tagging + placement** are now invokable actions, so "tag these summer-stock and put them in Bin 4" just works, in Ask Cobb (with the usual confirm cards + undo ledger) and over the MCP server alike. Long lists are honest too: a capped page says "there may be more" instead of letting the AI overclaim.
