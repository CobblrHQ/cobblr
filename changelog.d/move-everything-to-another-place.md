A place's page can move everything in it to another place in one go, with the places inside it if you like, and can merge two places that are really one. It can be undone, and Cobb can do it too.

## docs

**Move everything to another place.** On a place's page, press **Move everything
to…** and pick where it should all go. Everything kept there moves, and each
thing's history says so. Tick **Also move the places inside it** to take its
shelves or bins along, with what is in them.

**Merge two places.** Tick **Then delete** as well, and everything goes to the
other place before this one is deleted. It is the way to tidy up two places
that are really one, such as two "Bin 3"s from two imports.

Either way, the message afterwards has an **Undo** that puts everything back.
Anything someone has moved again in the meantime stays where they put it. You
can also ask Cobb: "move everything from Bin 3 to Bin 7".
