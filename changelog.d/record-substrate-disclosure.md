A collection you make now shows a lean catalog by default (title, photo, your fields) and only reveals stock controls (quantity, reorder, cost, warranty) once it actually holds stock, and it stays stock after that even if you run it down to zero. Recording a price alone no longer tips a catalog into a stock list, and you can still pin either face under Settings, Stock tracking.
