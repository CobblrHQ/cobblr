**A scanned item keeps its name when its colour is filed as a swatch.** A receipt line like "Blue cotton yarn 100g 200m" showed in the inbox as "#0000ff cotton yarn 100g 200m" once the Color field had been filled with the hex, while the preview and the saved record kept the words. The inbox title, the File everything preview and the record now read the same name, and the colour shows as a swatch beside it. A colour you say in words (a hint, or what the photo shows) still reaches the title as a word.
