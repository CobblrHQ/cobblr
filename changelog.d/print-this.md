## docs

**Print this.** Open a model file (STL or 3MF, or your own G-code) and press **Print this**, or press it on a Make that has its model, or ask Cobb to print it. Say the material and, if you like, the colour. Cobblr opens a Make, picks the printer the part fits that has the material loaded, and has your connected slicer slice it for that printer. When the slice is done, the Make shows one card with a preview, the printer, the spool and its slot, the grams and the time. Nothing prints until an owner or admin presses **Start the print** on that card, and pressing it twice starts it once. If no printer can take the part, Cobblr says which printers it tried and why, and nothing is queued.
