The end of a sandbox can now bring what you built into a real account: the bundles, collections, fields, automations and records it listed, with your photos, made again in the new account the same way you would have made them there.

## docs

**Bring what you built to a real account.** When the demo box offers it, the
ending page has a third door, **Bring it along**. Give an email and it keeps
your trial for a month, then takes you to the real Cobblr's **Bring what you
built** page. Make an account there (or sign in), and the page shows the same
list your ending showed. Choose the workspace to bring it into and press
**Bring it into**; every line comes back with what happened to it.

- It is made again, not copied: each bundle is installed, each collection and
  field set up, each record created through the same doors you would use by
  hand. Your photos come with the records they belong to, as the new
  workspace's own files.
- A bundle arrives as the catalog has it now, and the line says so when your
  trial had an older version. A bundle you made yourself in the trial comes as
  you built it.
- A record you added to one of the sandbox's own collections brings that
  collection with it, so your groceries have somewhere to live.
- Anything that could not come is named on its line with the reason: a record
  whose kind has nowhere to go, a picture that could not be read, a link to
  something that stays in the trial.
- Bringing the same trial into the same workspace twice is refused, so a reload
  does not double anything. What stays behind is the same list the ending page
  names under "Stays here".
