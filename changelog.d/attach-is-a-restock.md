**A scan you add to something you already have now restocks it properly.** Pressing "+2, still had some", "Replaced the one that ran out", or filing a receipt line onto the milk you have, dates the new stock the way a check-off does: a fresh lot from today, good until today plus its shelf life, with its bought-on date. Before, the count went up and the old dates stayed. Sending the scan back removes that lot again, or, if you have used some since, takes back what is left and says so.
