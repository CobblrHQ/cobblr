## docs

### Cobblr for Groceries

**Cobblr for Groceries** is the pantry as an app, signed up for at `/start/groceries`. The front door is the **receipt**: on a phone, the scan inbox's **Upload a receipt photo** opens the camera, and one photo puts every line of the trip in the inbox with a name, a picture, and a quantity. **File them all** puts them in the pantry.

**Every food item gets a use-by date without being asked.** Cobblr knows roughly how long milk, berries, chicken, bread, or a tin keep, and counts from the receipt date, so nothing is dated by the day you happened to scan it. The guess is deliberately a little early. A thing that is not food (dish soap, a light bulb) gets no date rather than a wrong one, and a date you type always wins. Once you mark something opened or thrown out, the household's own numbers take over.

**The board.** The app opens on **What's on hand**: every item as a tile with its picture, a count you tap up or down, and a dot that turns as the date nears. It is the Groceries bundle's pinned view, and any bundle's pinned view now opens as a **tab beside the table** on its own page, in the app and in a full workspace.

**The morning something expires, you hear about it.** Each date earns two lines: a heads-up a few days out, and a day-of line the morning it expires, delivered the way you chose under **Your account → Communication preferences** (in-app, Discord DM, or email).
