**Give a task to someone.** Open a task and pick who it is for from your workspace's people, including someone without an account. They are told when a task is given to them, their name shows on the task's line, and **Mine** on a project shows only your tasks.

## docs

**Assigned to** on a task picks whose job it is from your workspace's People: a member, or anyone listed by name (a family member without an account, say). Their name shows on the task's line in the project.

- A member given a task is told, in the bell and wherever they get notifications, with a link straight to it. You are never told about a task you gave yourself.
- **Mine**, beside **All** above a project's tasks, shows only the tasks given to you.
