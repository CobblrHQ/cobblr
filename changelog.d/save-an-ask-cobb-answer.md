Every Ask Cobb answer can be saved. A crochet or knitting pattern becomes a Pattern that lists the yarn, hook and notions it names, checked against your stash like a PDF pattern; anything else becomes a Knowledge Base note that says where it came from.

## docs

**Save an answer from Ask Cobb.** A longer answer in the Ask Cobb panel has a **Save** button under it. It proposes where the answer goes, and you can change it before saving:

- **Patterns**, when your workspace has them and the answer reads as a pattern (a materials list and steps). The pattern's text is its body, no PDF needed, and the yarn, hook and notions it names are read into **What this pattern needs** straight away, the same way a PDF's materials list is. So your stash is checked against it, and anything missing can go on the shopping list. You can still attach the PDF later.
- **Knowledge Base**, for anything else. The note keeps the whole answer and ends with where it came from ("From Ask Cobb, 2026-09-28, asked: …").

After saving, the answer says **Saved to Patterns · Open** (or Knowledge Base), and that line is still there after a reload. Saving is the same change Cobb makes when you ask him to save something, so it is recorded in the same way.

A pattern saved earlier with its words but nothing listed under **What this pattern needs** shows **Read what it needs from its text** on its page: one tap reads its yarn, hook and notions from what is already written there.
