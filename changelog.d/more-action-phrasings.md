Cobb recognises far more of the ways people ask for things. Another thirty actions now carry the words somebody would actually use, so "part out this kit", "where is my parcel", "split one off that lot" and "that order went out" reach the right action instead of being guessed at from an internal name. Every action a person can ask for now has its phrasings, and a new one cannot ship without them.
