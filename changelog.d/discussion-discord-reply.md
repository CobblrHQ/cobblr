When someone mentions you, the Discord DM now carries a Reply button: press it, type, and your answer lands in the discussion without opening Cobblr.

## docs

If you have Discord connected, being mentioned in a discussion already reaches
you as a DM straight away. That DM now carries a **Reply** button.

Press it and Discord opens a text box. What you type is posted into the
discussion as your comment, attributed to you, visible to everyone in the
workspace. You never leave Discord and you never pick a workspace: the message
already knows which record it was about.

**Typing in the DM instead does nothing.** Discord only tells us about the
button, not about ordinary messages you send to the bot, so use the button.

**Not connected?** The Discussion panel says so once, with a link, and an X to
put it away for good. Without Discord, a mention still reaches the bell and
your inbox, just not immediately.
