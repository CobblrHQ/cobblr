**Asking Cobb to delete a record and turn a feature off gives you two cards.** With a record above the box, "delete this and yarn as a whole" used to come back as one card that only turned the feature off, titled "Turn off a feature" with "The feature, by its name in the app (e.g. Shipments): yarn" underneath. Now the record gets its own card, "Delete (the record's name) (permanent)", and the feature card reads "Turn off the Yarn feature" and says what that does: it leaves the menu, its records are kept, and the fields and automations it brought are removed. Every card now shows an action's details as plain labels, without the hints written for Cobb.
