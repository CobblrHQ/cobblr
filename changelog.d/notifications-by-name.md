**Notifications are named, and you pick which ones a channel gets from a list.** The notifications page showed a code under some notifications ("digifab.print.failed") instead of what kind they were; every kind now has a name, like "A print failed" or "Approval requested". Choosing which notifications go to Discord, Slack or email used to mean typing one of those codes into a box; you now pick from the kinds your workspace sends, by name and under the app that sends them, or All events.

**The Delivery page only lists messages Cobblr actually sends.** Three rows ("Messages from Claude", "Feature announcements", "Workspace updates") were switches for messages nothing ever sent, and are gone. The locked "Account deletion" row is now "Trial ending", the warning it really is, and "Password & security" no longer mentions two-factor sign-in, which Cobblr does not have.
