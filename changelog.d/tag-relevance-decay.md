Tags now sort by how much they still matter, and a tag you have not used in months folds away behind a "+N" instead of sitting on every record forever.

## docs

Tags used to be listed alphabetically everywhere, which meant a tag called
"3DPrintopia 2026" sat at the top of every printer's tag row long after the
trade show, purely because it starts with a digit.

Tags are now ordered by how much they still matter, and a tag that has gone
quiet folds away rather than crowding the record.

**How a tag goes quiet.** Two things have to be true: it has not been applied
to anything for about six months, AND it is only on a handful of records. Both
matter. A tag like `fragile` or `in service` might get no new use for a year
and still describe how your workspace is organised, so a tag on a dozen or more
records never goes quiet no matter how long it has been. What goes quiet is the
narrow, one-off tag: the event, the batch, the shipment.

**Nothing is deleted or hidden.** A quiet tag folds behind a small `+2` button
at the end of the tag row. Click it to bring them back, and hover it to see why
each one folded ("3DPrintopia 2026 · last used 6mo ago"). The tag still exists,
still has its records, and still turns up when you search or filter for it. It
just stops asking for your attention every time you open a printer.

**Pinning.** If you want a tag up front regardless, pin it. A pinned tag sorts
first and never folds away, which is the escape hatch for anything the
automatic ordering gets wrong for you.

**Nothing to set up.** This works off tag history you already have, so it
applies to your existing tags immediately. There is no field to create and no
switch to turn on.
