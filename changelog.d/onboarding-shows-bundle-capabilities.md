**Setting up a bundle with optional capabilities from onboarding now shows what it can do.** Picking a recipe like Lego in "What do you want to do?" used to install it silently and drop you on an empty page, with no hint that Bricks bins, the Rebrickable catalog link, Disassemble, labels, and scanning were even part of it. Now, when a recipe has optional capabilities, "Set up" opens its detail card so you see and choose them first, then land in your new tracker. A recipe with nothing to choose still installs in one tap. Also fixed a bundle card that printed its description twice.
