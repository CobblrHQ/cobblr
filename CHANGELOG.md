# Changelog

User-facing changes, newest first. Consolidated daily from `changelog.d/`.

## 2026-06-16

- **Improvement** (scan): Scanning a Polar Filament spool QR now pulls full details (including print temperatures) from their official API.
- **Feature** (tags): Tags can now be nested under a parent tag and given an emoji icon — organize them into groups and recognize them at a glance.

## 2026-06-16

- **Improvement** (scan): Barcode scans now also check Open Food Facts and Open Beauty Facts (both free), so groceries and cosmetics resolve more often without falling back to a paid lookup.
- **Improvement** (fields): Yes/No fields now display as Yes/No (or your custom labels) instead of true/false.
